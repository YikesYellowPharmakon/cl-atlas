Chapter 14: Self-supervised learning and foundation models

Despite its huge success, supervised learning with neural networks typically relies on the availability of a labeled dataset of decent size, which is sometimes costly to collect. Recently, AI and machine learning are undergoing a paradigm shift with the rise of models (e.g., BERT [Devlin et al., 2019] and GPT-3 [Brown et al., 2020]) that are pre-trained on broad data at scale and are adaptable to a wide range of downstream tasks. These models, called foundation models by Bommasani et al. [2021], oftentimes leverage massive unlabeled data so that much fewer labeled data in the downstream tasks are needed. Moreover, though foundation models are based on standard deep learning and transfer learning, their scale results in new emergent capabilities. These models are typically (pre-)trained by self-supervised learning methods where the supervisions/labels come from parts of the inputs.

This chapter will introduce the paradigm of foundation models and basic related concepts.

监督学习依赖规模可观的标注数据, 而标注往往很贵. 近年的范式转变: BERT、GPT-3 这类模型先在大规模的广泛数据上预训练, 再适配到各种下游任务. Bommasani 等 (2021) 称之为基础模型. 它们利用海量无标注数据, 使下游任务所需的标注大大减少; 技术上仍是深度学习和迁移学习, 但规模带来了新的涌现能力. 预训练通常用自监督学习: 「标签」来自输入本身的一部分 (例如用前文预测下一个词).

预训练与适配
14.1 Pretraining and adaptation

Page 168–169

The foundation models paradigm consists of two phases: pretraining (or simply training) and adaptation. We first pretrain a large model on a massive unlabeled dataset (e.g., billions of unlabeled images). Then, we adapt the pretrained model to a downstream task (e.g., detecting cancer from scan images). These downstream tasks are often prediction tasks with limited or even no labeled data. The intuition is that the pretrained models learn good representations that capture intrinsic semantic structure/ information about the data, and the adaptation phase customizes the model to a particular downstream task by, e.g., retrieving the information specific to it. For example, a model pretrained on massive unlabeled image data may learn good general visual representations/features, and we adapt the representations to solve biomedical imagining tasks.

(Footnote 1) Sometimes, pretraining can involve large-scale labeled datasets as well (e.g., the ImageNet dataset).

基础模型范式分两个阶段: ① 预训练: 在海量无标注数据 (如数十亿张图片) 上训练一个大模型; ② 适配: 把预训练模型调整到一个下游任务 (如从扫描图像中检测癌症), 下游任务的标注数据往往很少甚至没有. 直观: 预训练学到捕捉数据内在语义结构的好表示, 适配阶段从中提取与特定任务相关的信息. 预训练有时也用大规模标注数据 (如 ImageNet).

We formalize the two phases below.

Pretraining. Suppose we have an unlabeled pretraining dataset {x(1),x(2),⋯,x(n)} that consists of n examples in Rd. Let ϕθ​ be a model that is parameterized by θ and maps the input x to some m-dimensional representation ϕθ​(x). (People also call ϕθ​(x)∈Rm the embedding or features of the example x.) We pretrain the model θ with a pretraining loss, which is often an average of loss functions on all the examples: Lpre​(θ)=n1​∑i=1n​ℓpre​(θ,x(i)). Here ℓpre​ is a so-called self-supervised loss on a single datapoint x(i), because as shown later, e.g., in Section 14.3, the “supervision” comes from the data point x(i) itself. It is also possible that the pretraining loss is not a sum of losses on individual examples. We will discuss two pretraining losses in Section 14.2 and Section 14.3.

We use some optimizers (mostly likely SGD or ADAM [Kingma and Ba, 2014]) to minimize Lpre​(θ). We denote the obtained pretrained model by θ^.

预训练的形式化: 无标注数据 x(i)∈Rd; 模型 ϕθ​ 把输入映成 m 维表示 (也叫嵌入、特征), 正是第 7 章说的「学到的特征映射». 预训练损失通常是各样本自监督损失的平均 (监督信号来自样本本身); 也可以不是逐样本求和 (下面的对比学习就是). 用 SGD 或 Adam 最小化, 得到预训练参数 θ^.

讲义笔误: "mostly likely SGD or ADAM" 中 mostly 应为 most.

Page 169

Adaptation. For a downstream task, we usually have a labeled dataset {(xtask(1)​,ytask(1)​),⋯,(xtask(ntask​)​,ytask(ntask​)​)} with ntask​ examples. The setting when ntask​=0 is called zero-shot learning—the downstream task doesn't have any labeled examples. When ntask​ is relatively small (say, between 1 and 50), the setting is called few-shot learning. It's also pretty common to have a larger ntask​ on the order of ranging from hundreds to tens of thousands.

An adaptation algorithm generally takes in a downstream dataset and the pretrained model θ^, and outputs a variant of θ^ that solves the downstream task. We will discuss below two popular and general adaptation methods, linear probe and finetuning. In addition, two other methods specific to language problems are introduced in 14.3.1.

适配: 下游任务有 ntask​ 个标注样本. ntask​=0 叫零样本学习; 很少 (约 1 到 50 个) 叫少样本学习; 几百到几万个也很常见. 适配算法输入下游数据和预训练模型, 输出一个解决下游任务的变体. 两种通用方法: 线性探测和微调; 语言模型特有的两种方法见 14.3.1.

The linear probe approach uses a linear head on top of the representation to predict the downstream labels. Mathematically, the adapted model outputs w⊤ϕθ^​(x), where w∈Rm is a parameter to be learned, and θ^ is exactly the pretrained model (fixed). We can use SGD (or other optimizers) to train w on the downstream task loss to predict the task label

w∈Rmmin​ntask​1​i=1∑ntask​​​ℓtask​(ytask(i)​,w⊤ϕθ^​(xtask(i)​))(14.1)​

E.g., if the downstream task is a regression problem, we will have ℓtask​(ytask​,w⊤ϕθ^​(xtask​))=(ytask​−w⊤ϕθ^​(xtask​))2.

The finetuning algorithm uses a similar structure for the downstream prediction model, but also further finetunes the pretrained model (instead of keeping it fixed). Concretely, the prediction model is w⊤ϕθ​(x) with parameters w and θ. We optimize both w and θ to fit the downstream data, but initialize θ with the pretrained model θ^. The linear head w is usually initialized randomly.

Various other adaptation methods exists and are sometimes specialized to the particular pretraining methods. We will discuss one of them in Section 14.3.1.

线性探测: 预训练模型固定不动, 只在表示上训练一个线性「头» w (14.1). 例如回归任务就是在特征 ϕθ^​(x) 上做线性回归, 这正是第 5 章「固定特征映射上的线性模型». 微调: 预测模型结构相同, 但 θ 也参与优化, 以预训练参数为初始值, w 随机初始化 (14.2)–(14.4). 线性探测便宜、不易过拟合, 适合标注很少时; 微调通常效果更好, 但要更新全部参数, 数据少时可能过拟合.

介于两者之间的方法: 大模型全部参数微调代价很高 (每个任务都要存一份完整的模型), 于是有参数高效微调: 只训练少量新增参数. 典型的是 LoRA (Hu 等 2021): 把权重矩阵的更新写成低秩形式 W+BA, B、A 是窄矩阵, 只训练 B、A; 一个 4096 × 4096 的矩阵有约 1680 万个参数, 秩取 8 时 B、A 合计只有 2 × 4096 × 8 = 65536 个. 另一种思路是提示微调 (prompt tuning): 只训练加在输入前面的一小段「软提示」向量.

14.2 Pretraining methods in computer vision

Page 170

This section introduces two concrete pretraining methods for computer vision: supervised pretraining and contrastive learning.

Supervised pretraining. Here, the pretraining dataset is a large-scale labeled dataset (e.g., ImageNet), and the pretrained models are simply a neural network trained with vanilla supervised learning (with the last layer being removed). Concretely, suppose we write the learned neural network as Uϕθ^​(x), where U is the last (fully-connected) layer parameters, θ^ corresponds to the parameters of all the other layers, and ϕθ^​(x) are the penultimate activations layer (which serves as the representation). We simply discard U and use ϕθ^​(x) as the pretrained model.

Contrastive learning. Contrastive learning is a self-supervised pretraining method that uses only unlabeled data. The main intuition is that a good representation function ϕθ​(⋅) should map semantically similar images to similar representations, and that random pair of images should generally have distinct representations. E.g., we may want to map images of two huskies to similar representations, but a husky and an elephant should have different representations. One definition of similarity is that images from the same class are similar. Using this definition will result in the so-called supervised contrastive algorithms that work well when labeled pretraining datasets are available.

有监督预训练: 在 ImageNet 这样的大型标注数据集上正常训练分类网络, 然后去掉最后一层 U, 用倒数第二层的激活作为表示. 对比学习: 只用无标注数据的自监督方法. 直观: 好的表示应把语义相似的图像映到相近的表示, 随机两张图的表示应该不同 (两只哈士奇相近, 哈士奇和大象不同). 若用「同一类」定义相似, 就是有监督的对比学习, 需要标注.

Page 171

Without labeled data, we can use data augmentation to generate a pair of “similar” augmented images given an original image x. Data augmentation typically means that we apply random cropping, flipping, and/or color transformation on the original image x to generate a variant. We can take two random augmentations, denoted by x^ and x~, of the same original image x, and call them a positive pair. We observe that positive pairs of images are often semantically related because they are augmentations of the same image. We will design a loss function for θ such that the representations of a positive pair, ϕθ​(x^),ϕθ​(x~), as close to each other as possible.

On the other hand, we can also take another random image z from the pretraining dataset and generate an augmentation z^ from z. Note that (x^,z^) are from different images; therefore, with a good chance, they are not semantically related. We call (x^,z^) a negative or random pair. We will design a loss to push the representation of random pairs, ϕθ​(x^),ϕθ​(z^), far away from each other.

(Footnote 2) Random pair may be a more accurate term because it's still possible (though not likely) that x and z are semantically related, so are x^ and z^. But in the literature, the term negative pair seems to be also common.

没有标注时, 用数据增强制造「相似」的图像: 对同一张图随机裁剪、翻转、变换颜色, 得到两个变体, 叫正样本对 (语义相关, 因为来自同一张图), 损失要让它们的表示尽量接近. 另取一张图 z 的增强 z^, 与 x^ 组成负样本对 (随机对), 损失要把它们的表示推开. 脚注: 叫「随机对」更准确, 因为两张随机图也可能语义相关, 只是概率小.

讲义笔误: "such that the representations of a positive pair, …, as close to each other as possible" 漏了动词 (应为 "are as close").

There are many recent algorithms based on the contrastive learning principle, and here we introduce SIMCLR [Chen et al., 2020] as an concrete example. The loss function is defined on a batch of examples (x1​,⋯,x(B)) with batch size B. The algorithm computes two random augmentations for each example x(i) in the batch, denoted by x^(i) and x~(i). As a result, we have the augmented batch of 2B examples: x^1​,⋯,x^(B),x~1​,⋯,x~(B). The SIMCLR loss is defined as

​Lpre​(θ)=−i=1∑B​logexp(sii​)+∑j=i​exp(sij​)exp(sii​)​,sij​=ϕθ​(x^(i))⊤ϕθ​(x~(j))​

The intuition is as follows. The loss is increasing in ϕθ​(x^(i))⊤ϕθ​(x~(j)), and thus minimizing the loss encourages ϕθ​(x^(i))⊤ϕθ​(x~(j)) to be small, making ϕθ​(x^(i)) far away from ϕθ​(x~(j)). On the other hand, the loss is decreasing in ϕθ​(x^(i))⊤ϕθ​(x~(i)), and thus minimizing the loss encourages ϕθ​(x^(i))⊤ϕθ​(x~(i)) to be large, resulting in ϕθ​(x^(i)) and ϕθ​(x~(i)) to be close.

(Footnote 3) This is a variant and simplification of the original loss that does not change the essence (but may change the efficiency slightly). (Footnote 4) To see this, you can verify that the function −logp+qp​ is decreasing in p, and increasing in q when p, q > 0.

SimCLR: 一个批次 B 张图, 每张做两次增强, 共 2B 张. 损失 (为排版, 笔记把内积 ϕθ​(x^(i))⊤ϕθ​(x~(j)) 简记为 sij​, 这是表示之间的相似度): 对每个 i, 分子是正样本对的相似度 (内积) 的指数, 分母再加上 x^(i) 与批内其他图的增强 (负样本) 的相似度的指数. 这正是一个 B 选 1 的 softmax 分类: 在 B 个候选中认出哪一个是自己的另一个增强, 损失是交叉熵. 脚注 4: −logp+qp​ 对 p 递减、对 q 递增, 所以最小化损失会增大正对的相似度、减小负对的相似度.

讲义笔误: 批次写成 (x1​,⋯,x(B)), 下标和上标记法混用, 应统一为 (x(1),⋯,x(B)) (增强后的批次同样); "as an concrete example" 应为 a concrete example; "resulting in … to be close" 搭配不当.

数值例子 (自编, 用 Python 核对): B = 2. 两个正对的相似度为 3.0 和 2.5, 负对的相似度为 0.5 (i = 1 时) 和 1.0 (i = 2 时). 两项的 softmax 概率分别为 e3/(e3+e0.5)≈0.924 和 e2.5/(e2.5+e1)≈0.818, 损失 = −log 0.924 − log 0.818 ≈ 0.280. 若负对相似度升高, 分母变大, 损失上升. 实践中表示通常先归一化到单位长度, 内积再除以一个温度参数 τ (如 0.1), 批次越大负样本越多, 效果越好.

14.3 Pretrained large language models

Page 172

Natural language processing is another area where pretraining models are particularly successful. In language problems, an examples typically corresponds to a document or generally a sequence/trunk of words, denoted by x=(x1​,⋯,xT​) where T is the length of the document/sequence, xi​∈{1,⋯,V} are words in the document, and V is the vocabulary size.

A language model is a probabilistic model representing the probability of a document, denoted by p(x1​,⋯,xT​). This probability distribution is very complex because its support size is VT—exponential in the length of the document. Instead of modeling the distribution of a document itself, we can apply the chain rule of conditional probability to decompose it as follows:

​p(x1​,⋯,xT​)=p(x1​)p(x2​∣x1​)⋯p(xT​∣x1​,⋯,xT−1​).(14.5)​

Now the support of each of the conditional probability p(xt​∣x1​,⋯,xt−1​) is V.

(Footnote 5) In the practical implementations, typically all the data are concatenated into a single sequence in some order, and each example typically corresponds a sub-sequence of consecutive words which may corresponds to a subset of a document or may span across multiple documents. (Footnote 6) Technically, words may be decomposed into tokens which could be words or sub-words (combinations of letters), but this note omits this technicality. In fact most commons words are a single token themselves.

NLP 是预训练最成功的领域之一. 一个样本是一个文档 (或一段词序列) x1​,…,xT​, 每个 xt​ 是词表中的编号 (词表大小 V). 语言模型给出文档的概率. 直接建模不可行, 因为可能的文档有 VT 种; 用链式法则分解成一系列条件概率 (14.5), 每个条件概率只是「在 V 个词上的分布», 即预测下一个词. 脚注: 实现中把所有数据拼成一条长序列, 每个样本是其中连续的一段, 可能跨越文档边界; 「词」实际是 token (词或子词), 常见词通常就是一个 token.

讲义笔误: "an examples" 单复数不一致; "sequence/trunk of words" 中 trunk 应为 chunk; "each of the conditional probability" 应为 probabilities; 脚注 "corresponds a sub-sequence" 漏了 to, "which may corresponds" 应为 correspond, "most commons words" 应为 common.

补充: 分词 (tokenization). 现代语言模型多用 BPE (字节对编码) 等子词分词: 从单个字符 (或字节) 出发, 反复把语料中最常相邻出现的一对合并成一个新符号, 直到词表达到预定大小 (如 5 万或 10 万). 结果是常见词是单个 token, 罕见词被拆成几段 (如 "unhappiness" 可能拆成 "un" + "happiness"), 任何字符串都能表示, 不会出现「词表外」的词. 这与第 4 章朴素贝叶斯把整词作为特征的做法不同: 那里遇到没见过的词就只能靠平滑.

We will model the conditional probability p(xt​∣x1​,⋯,xt−1​) with some parameterized form. To this end, we first turn the discrete words into word embeddings.

Let ei​∈Rd be the embedding of the word i ∈ {1, 2, ⋯, V}. We call [e1​,⋯,eV​]∈Rd×V the embedding matrix. The most commonly used model is transformer [Vaswani et al., 2017]. We will introduce the basic concepts regarding the inputs and outputs of a transformer, but treat the intermediate computation in transformer as a blackbox. We refer the students to more advanced courses or the original paper for more details. The high-level pipeline is visualized in Figure 14.1. Given a document (x1​,⋯,xT​), we first compute the corresponding word embeddings (ex1​​,⋯,exT​​). Then, the word embeddings is passed to a transformer model, which takes in a sequence of vectors and outputs a sequence of vectors (c2​,⋯,cT+1​). In particular, here we use the autoregressive version of the transformers which makes sure that ct​ only depends on x1​,⋯,xt−1​. The ct​'s are often called the representations or the contextualized embeddings, and we write ct​=ϕθ​(x1​,…,xt−1​) where ϕθ​ denotes the mapping from the input to the representations.

(Footnote 7) This property no longer holds in masked language models [Devlin et al., 2019] where the losses are also different.

先把离散的词变成词嵌入: 每个词 i 对应一个 d 维向量 ei​, 所有词嵌入组成嵌入矩阵 (它也是可学习的参数). 最常用的模型是 Transformer, 讲义只讲输入输出接口, 中间当作黑箱. 读图: 输入 T 个词嵌入, 输出 T 个向量 c2​,…,cT+1​; 用的是自回归版本, 保证 ct​ 只依赖前面的词 x1​,…,xt−1​ (不能「偷看」要预测的词). ct​ 叫上下文嵌入: 同一个词在不同上下文中的表示不同 (例如 "bank" 在「河岸」和「银行」两种语境中). 脚注: 掩码语言模型 (BERT) 不满足这个性质, 损失也不同.

讲义笔误: "the word embeddings is passed" 应为 are passed.

补充: Transformer 中间做了什么. 核心是自注意力: 每个位置的向量生成查询 q、键 k、值 v (都是线性变换), 位置 t 的输出是所有位置的值的加权平均, 权重 = softmax(qt⊤​ks​/dk​​), 即查询与各个键的相似度. 这让每个词能直接「看到」序列中任何位置的词, 不像循环网络那样逐步传递. 自回归 (因果) 版本把 s > t 的权重置为 0 (因果掩码), 保证不看未来. 每层由多头注意力 + 逐位置的前馈网络 (两层 MLP) 组成, 配合残差连接和层归一化, 堆叠几十层. 注意力本身不分顺序, 所以要另外加入位置编码.

​p(xt​=1∣x1​⋯,xt−1​)p(xt​=2∣x1​⋯,xt−1​)⋮p(xt​=V∣x1​⋯,xt−1​)​​

​=softmax(Wt​ct​)∈RV(14.6)=softmax(Wt​ϕθ​(x1​,⋯,xt−1​)),(14.7)​

where W∈RV×d is a weight matrix that maps the contextualized embedding ct​ to the logits. In other words, Wt​ is an additional linear layer for the prediction of the conditional probability. Recall that softmax(⋅):RV↦RV maps the logits to the probabilities:

softmax(u)=​∑i=1V​exp(ui​)exp(u1​)​⋮∑i=1V​exp(ui​)exp(uV​)​​​(14.8)

Let pt​=softmax(Wt​ϕθ​(x1​,⋯,xt−1​))∈RV be the predicted conditional probability at position t. Let W be the concatenation of W1​,⋯,WT​. The loss function is often called language modeling loss and defined as

L(W,θ)​=t=2∑T​(cross entropy lossat position t)=t=2∑T​−logpt,xt​​,(14.9)​

where pt,j​ denotes the j-th entry of the probability vector pt​.

语言建模损失: 每个位置的交叉熵之和, 即对真实下一个词的预测概率取负对数 (14.9). 最小化它就是最大化整个文档在模型下的对数概率 (由链式法则 (14.5)), 也就是最大似然估计. 「自监督」体现在: 标签就是文本中的下一个词, 不需要人工标注, 所以可以用整个互联网规模的文本训练.

困惑度 (perplexity): 语言模型常用的评估指标, 等于平均每个 token 交叉熵的指数. 例 (用 Python 计算): 三个位置上真实词的预测概率为 0.5、0.25、0.1, 平均交叉熵 ≈ 1.461, 困惑度 ≈ 4.31, 可以理解为模型「平均在约 4 个词之间犹豫». 困惑度越低越好; 完全均匀地猜 V 个词时困惑度就是 V.

补充: 掩码语言模型 (BERT). 自回归模型只用左边的上下文预测下一个词. BERT 随机遮住输入中约 15% 的 token, 让模型同时利用左右两侧的上下文去预测被遮住的词. 这样得到的表示更适合理解类任务 (分类、抽取), 通常用微调来适配; 但它不能直接逐词生成文本. GPT 系列是自回归的, 适合生成, 也正是下面零样本和上下文学习的基础.

14.3.1 Zero-shot learning and in-context learning

For language models, there are many ways to adapt a pretrained model to downstream tasks. In this notes, we discuss three of them: finetuning, zero-shot learning, and in-context learning.

Finetuning is not very common for the autoregressive language models that we introduced in Section 14.3 but much more common for other variants such as masked language models which has similar input-output interfaces but are pretrained differently [Devlin et al., 2019]. The finetuning method is the same as introduced generally in Section 14.1—the only question is how we define the prediction task with an additional linear head. One option is to treat cT+1​=ϕθ​(x1​,⋯,xT​) as the representation and use w⊤cT+1​=w⊤ϕθ​(x1​,⋯,xT​) to predict task label. As described in Section 14.1, we initialize θ to the pretrained model θ^ and then optimize both w and θ.

语言模型的三种适配方式. ① 微调: 对讲义介绍的自回归模型不太常用, 对掩码语言模型 (BERT) 很常用. 做法与 14.1 节相同: 取最后一个上下文表示 cT+1​ (它看过了整个输入) 作为整段文本的表示, 加一个线性头预测标签, θ 从预训练值出发一起优化.

Zero-shot adaptation or zero-shot learning is the setting where there is no input-output pairs from the downstream tasks. For language problems tasks, typically the task is formatted as a question or a cloze test form via natural language. For example, we can format an example as a question:

xtask​=(xtask,1​,⋯,xtask,T​) = “Is the speed of light a universal constant?”

Then, we compute the most likely next word predicted by the language model given this question, that is, computing argmaxxT+1​​p(xT+1​∣xtask,1​,⋯,xtask,T​). In this case, if the most likely next word xT+1​ is “No”, then we solve the task. (The speed of light is only a constant in vacuum). We note that there are many ways to decode the answer from the language models, e.g., instead of computing the argmax, we may use the language model to generate a few words word. It is an active research question to find the best way to utilize the language models.

② 零样本: 下游任务没有任何标注样本. 把任务写成自然语言的问题或完形填空, 让模型预测下一个词. 例: 问「光速是普适常数吗?», 取概率最大的下一个词, 若是 "No" 就答对了 (讲义的理由是光速只在真空中是常数). 解码方式有很多, 例如不取 argmax 而是让模型生成几个词, 怎样最好地利用语言模型仍在研究中.

讲义问题: ① "there is no input-output pairs" 应为 are; "For language problems tasks" 中 problems 多余; "generate a few words word" 中 word 多余. ② 这个例子的「标准答案」本身有争议: 物理学中所说的光速常数 c 指真空中的光速, 它是普适常数, 不少人 (和模型) 会答 "Yes". 用一个有争议的问题演示零样本, 不太理想.

In-context learning is mostly used for few-shot settings where we have a few labeled examples (xtask(1)​,ytask(1)​),⋯,(xtask(ntask​)​,ytask(ntask​)​). Given a test example xtest​, we construct a document (x1​,⋯,xT​), which is more commonly called a “prompt” in this context, by concatenating the labeled examples and the text example in some format. For example, we may construct the prompt as follows

x1, ..., xT = "Q: 2 ~ 3 = ?      ← x_task(1)
               A: 5             ← y_task(1)
               Q: 6 ~ 7 = ?      ← x_task(2)
               A: 13            ← y_task(2)
               ···
               Q: 15 ~ 2 = ?"   ← x_test

Then, we let the pretrained model generate the most likely xT+1​,xT+2​,⋯. In this case, if the model can “learn” that the symbol ∼ means addition from the few examples, we will obtain the following which suggests the answer is 17.

xT+1​,xT+2​,⋯ = “A: 17”.

The area of foundation models is very new and quickly growing. The notes here only attempt to introduce these models on a conceptual level with a significant amount of simplification. We refer the readers to other materials, e.g., Bommasani et al. [2021], for more details.

③ 上下文学习 (in-context learning): 用于少样本. 把几个标注样本和测试样本按固定格式拼成一个提示 (prompt), 让模型续写. 例: 符号 ∼ 被定义为加法, 提示中给出 2 ∼ 3 = 5、6 ∼ 7 = 13 等例子, 再问 15 ∼ 2, 模型若从例子中「学会」∼ 表示加法, 就会续写出 "A: 17". 关键: 模型参数完全不更新, 「学习」只发生在一次前向计算中, 这是规模带来的涌现能力之一. (讲义中的符号在 PDF 里是 ∼, 代码块中写作 ~.)

讲义笔误: "concatenating the labeled examples and the text example" 中 text 应为 test (测试样本).

补充: 从预训练模型到对话助手. 只做语言建模预训练的模型擅长「续写», 但不一定听从指令. 现代的对话模型还要经过: ① 指令微调 (监督微调, SFT): 在大量「指令 → 高质量回答」的样本上继续用 (14.9) 式的损失训练, 只是只计算回答部分的 token; ② 人类反馈强化学习 (RLHF): 让人比较模型的两个回答哪个更好, 用这些比较训练一个打分的奖励模型, 再用强化学习 (第 15、17 章的策略梯度方法, 如 PPO) 让语言模型生成高奖励的回答, 同时用 KL 惩罚防止偏离原模型太远. 也有不训练奖励模型、直接从偏好数据优化的方法, 如 DPO.

补充: 规模定律与提示技巧. 实验发现语言模型的测试损失随参数量、数据量和计算量呈幂律下降 (Kaplan 等 2020; Hoffmann 等 2022 的 Chinchilla 结果指出, 给定计算预算, 参数量与训练 token 数应大致同比例增长, 每个参数约 20 个 token). 这是第 8.2 节「更大的过参数化模型往往更好」在语言模型上的体现. 提示方面, 「思维链」(chain-of-thought) 提示让模型先写出推理步骤再给答案, 在算术和推理题上显著提高准确率; 上面 15 ∼ 2 的例子若换成多步运算, 思维链往往就是答对的关键.
