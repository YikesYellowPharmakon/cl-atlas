正定性的四个等价判据
Positive definite matrices

Page 1

Studying positive definite matrices brings the whole course together; we use pivots, determinants, eigenvalues and stability. The new quantity here is x⊤Ax; watch for it.

This lecture covers how to tell if a matrix is positive definite, what it means for it to be positive definite, and some geometry.

Given a symmetric two by two matrix [ab​bc​], here are four ways to tell if it's positive definite:

  1. Eigenvalue test: λ1​>0,λ2​>0.
  2. Determinants test: a>0, ac−b2>0.
  3. Pivot test: a>0, aac−b2​>0.
  4. x⊤Ax is positive except when x=0 (this is usually the definition of positive definiteness).

本讲把全课三条线拧成一股: 消元 (主元)、行列式、特征值, 再加一个新主角 二次型 quadratic form x⊤Ax. 2×2 对称矩阵 [ab​bc​] 正定的四个判据互相等价:

① 特征值判据: λ1​>0,λ2​>0 (定义本身); ② 顺序主子式判据: a>0 且整个行列式 ac−b2>0; ③ 主元判据: a>0 且第二个主元 aac−b2​>0 —— ②③其实是同一回事, 因为 2×2 里「第二个主元 × 第一个主元 = 行列式」, 第一个主元就是 a; ④ 能量判据: x⊤Ax>0 对所有非零 x 成立, 这是大多数教材的定义, 也最几何.

四个判据难度不同: ④是定义但没法直接验算 (要检查无穷多个 x); ①要解二次方程; ②③只做消元和乘法, 是最常用的计算判据. 后面会看到 ④ 正是「二元函数在原点有极小值」的线性代数语言.

Using the determinants test, we know that [26​6y​] is positive definite when 2y−36>0 or when y>18.

The matrix [26​618​] is on the borderline of positive definiteness and is called a positive semidefinite matrix. It's a singular matrix with eigenvalues 0 and 20. Positive semidefinite matrices have eigenvalues greater than or equal to 0. For a singular matrix, the determinant is 0 and it only has one pivot.

x⊤Ax​=[x1​​x2​​][26​618​][x1​x2​​]=[x1​​x2​​][2x1​+6x2​6x1​+18x2​​]=2x12​+12x1​x2​+18x22​=ax12​+2bx1​x2​+cx22​.​

If this quadratic form is positive for every (real) x1​ and x2​ then the matrix is positive definite. In this positive semi-definite example, 2x12​+12x1​x2​+18x22​=2(x1​+3x2​)2=0 when x1​=3 and x2​=−1.

把判据用到含参数的例子: [26​6y​] 的行列式是 2y−36, 配上 a=2>0, 正定当且仅当 y>18. 边界情形 y=18 叫 positive semidefinite 半正定: 行列式为 0 (奇异), 一个特征值是 0, 另一个等于迹 20, 都非负; 消元只做了一步, 只有一个非零主元.

二次型怎么读: x⊤Ax=ax12​+2bx1​x2​+cx22​, 注意交叉项的系数是 2b (矩阵里只写一次 b, 展开时出现两次). 半正定例子里 2x12​+12x1​x2​+18x22​=2(x1​+3x2​)2 是平方和, 可以为 0: 取 (x1​,x2​)=(3,−1) 正是零空间的方向 [26​618​][3−1​]=[00​]. 半正定 = 「非负, 但在某个非零方向上躺平」.

已用 Python 验证: 三个特征值断言 (0 和 20)、配方恒等式、(3,−1) 为零向量方向, 全部精确成立.

极小值、鞍点与配方
Tests for minimum

If we apply the fourth test to the matrix [26​67​] which is not positive definite, we get the quadratic form f(x,y)=2x2+12xy+7y2. The graph of this function has a saddle point at the origin; see Figure 1.

The matrix [26​620​] is positive definite – its determinant is 4 and its trace is 22 so its eigenvalues are positive. The quadratic form associated with this matrix is f(x,y)=2x2+12xy+20y2, which is positive except when x=y=0. The level curves f(x,y)=k of this graph are ellipses; its graph appears in Figure 2. If a>0 and c>0, the quadratic form ax2+2bxy+cy2 is only negative when the value of 2bxy is negative and overwhelms the (positive) value of ax2+cy2.

The first derivatives fx​ and fy​ of this function are zero, so its graph is tangent to the xy-plane at (0,0,0); but this was also true of 2x2+12xy+7y2. As in single variable calculus, we need to look at the second derivatives of f to tell whether there is a minimum at the critical point.

两个对照例子把「正定 ⇔ 极小值」讲透. ① 非正定的 [26​67​]: 行列式 14−36=−22<0, 两个特征值一正一负 (Python 算出 11 和 -2), 曲面沿两个特征方向一个上一个下, 原点是鞍点 saddle point (Figure 1). ② 正定的 [26​620​]: 行列式 4、迹 22, 特征值积为正、和为正, 故两特征值全正 (约为 21.82 和 0.18), 曲面是碗口朝上的抛物面, 等高线是椭圆 ellipses (Figure 2).

读图: 两张图的区别不在原点 (一阶导数都是 0, 曲面都与 xy 平面相切), 而在二阶行为—— 这正是微积分里二阶导数检验的多变量版本, 马上由 Hessian 矩阵接棒.

Hessian 矩阵与二阶导数检验
Hessian matrix

Page 3

The matrix of second derivatives of f(x,y) is:

[fxx​fyx​​fxy​fyy​​].
Page 4

This matrix is symmetric because fxy​=fyx​. Its determinant is positive when the matrix is positive definite, which matches the fxx​fyy​>fxy2​ test for a minimum that we learned in calculus.

对 f(x,y)=ax2+2bxy+cy2 求二阶导: fxx​=2a, fxy​=fyx​=2b, fyy​=2c, 所以 Hessian 矩阵 (二阶导数矩阵) 是 2[ab​bc​]=2A —— 二次型的矩阵本身. 混合偏导相等保证它对称.

和微积分的接口: 一元函数 f′′>0 判极小; 多元版本是 Hessian 正定, 2×2 时即 fxx​>0 且 fxx​fyy​−fxy2​>0, 也就是讲义写的 fxx​fyy​>fxy2​ 再配上 fxx​>0. 「Hessian 正定 ⇔ 临界点处有严格极小值」是优化、最小二乘、机器学习里二阶条件的原型.

三阶正定例子
n by n

Page 4

A function of several variables f(x1​,x2​,...,xn​) has a minimum when its matrix of second derivatives is positive definite, and identifying minima of functions is often important. The tests we've just learned for 2 by 2 matrices also apply to n by n matrices.

A 3 by 3 example:

A=​2−10​−12−1​0−12​​.

Is this matrix positive definite? Our tests will say yes. What's the function x⊤Ax associated with this matrix? Does that function have a minimum at x=0? What does the graph of its quadratic form look like?

Looking at determinants we see:

det[2]=2,det[2−1​−12​]=5,det​2−10​−12−1​0−12​​=4.

These are all positive, so A is positive definite.

The pivots of A are 2, 3/2 and 4/3 (all positive) because the products of the pivots equal the determinants.

The eigenvalues of A are positive and their product is 4. It's not difficult to check that they are 2−2​,2 and 2+2​ (all positive).

幻灯片错误: Page 4 的顺序主子式第二个印成 det[2−1​−12​]=5, 实际 2⋅2−(−1)(−1)=4−1=3. 若真是 5, 主元乘积 2、5、4 与主元 2、3/2、4/3 (乘积 2、3、4) 对不上. 已用 Python 重算确认正确值是 3, 卡片保留原文.

三阶 (及以上) 判据和 2×2 完全同款, 各判据还互相印证: 顺序主子式应为 2、3、4 (全正); 主元 2、3/2、4/3 —— 第 k 个主元 = 第 k 个主子式 ÷ 第 k−1 个主子式: 3/2=3/2, 4/3=4/3, 连乘回到行列式 4; 特征值 2−2​≈0.59,2,2+2​≈3.41, 全正且乘积为 4. 三种算法在 Python 中全部复算通过.

这个矩阵是二阶差分矩阵的 3×3 版, 物理上是三个质点串联弹簧系统的刚度矩阵, 是 PDE 离散化后最常见的矩阵类型.

Rn 中的椭球
Ellipsoids in Rn

Page 4
f(x)=x⊤Ax=2x12​+2x22​+2x32​−2x1​x2​−2x2​x3​.

Because A is positive definite, we expect f(x) to be positive except when x=0. Its graph is a sort of four dimensional bowl or paraboloid. If we wrote f(x) as a sum of three squares, those squares would be multiplied by the (positive) pivots of A. Earlier, we said that a horizontal slice of our three dimensional bowl shape would be an ellipse. Here, a horizontal slice of the four dimensional bowl is an ellipsoid – a little bit like a rugby ball. For example, if we cut the graph at height 1 we get a surface whose equation is: 2x12​+2x22​+2x32​−2x1​x2​−2x2​x3​=1.

Just as an ellipse has a major and minor axis, an ellipsoid has three axes. If we write A=QΛQ⊤, as the principal axis theorem tells us we can, the eigenvectors of A tell us the directions of the principal axes of the ellipsoid. The eigenvalues tell us the lengths of those axes.

二次型展开注意交叉项: x⊤Ax=2x12​+2x22​+2x32​−2x1​x2​−2x2​x3​, 系数 −2 来自矩阵里的 −1 (每对只写一次、展开出现两次). 图像是四维碗面 (抛物面), 水平截面 f=1 是椭球 ellipsoid.

主轴定理的几何意义在这里完全展开: A=QΛQ⊤ 里, 特征向量给出椭球三个主轴的方向, 特征值给出轴的长度 (严格说是 1/λi​​: 在特征基下椭球方程为 λ1​y12​+λ2​y22​+λ3​y32​=1, 第 i 个半轴长 1/λi​​). 用主轴的平方长度理解: 特征值越大, 沿那个方向「碗壁越陡」, 截面越窄. 第 31 讲 SVD 会把这张图推广到任意矩阵.
