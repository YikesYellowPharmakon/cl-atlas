图与网络
Graphs and networks

Page 1

When we use linear algebra to understand physical systems, we often find more structure in the matrices and vectors than appears in the examples we make up in class. There are many applications of linear algebra; for example, chemists might use row reduction to get a clearer picture of what elements go into a complicated reaction. In this lecture we explore the linear algebra associated with electrical networks.

用线性代数研究实际系统时, 矩阵和向量往往带有课堂人为例子所没有的结构. 例如, 化学家可以用行消元看清复杂反应中有哪些元素参与. 本讲研究电网背后的线性代数.

这里的「结构」包括矩阵中大量的 0、行之间的依赖关系, 以及零空间的特殊含义. 它们不是计算中的偶然现象, 而是直接来自图的连接方式.

Page 1

A graph is a collection of nodes joined by edges; Figure 1 shows one small graph.

graph 图由node 节点和连接节点的edge 边组成. 原图有 n=4 个节点和 m=5 条边: 节点 1、2、3 两两相连, 节点 4 再分别连接节点 1 和节点 3.

这张图只记录连接关系. 节点画在什么位置、边画成多长, 都不改变图本身. 后面会把这些连接关系写成矩阵.

Page 1

We put an arrow on each edge to indicate the positive direction for currents running through the graph.

给每条边画上箭头后, 就得到directed graph 有向图. 箭头规定电流的正方向: ① 从节点 1 到 2, ② 从 2 到 3, ③ 从 1 到 3, ④ 从 1 到 4, ⑤ 从 3 到 4.

正方向只是计算时采用的约定, 不表示真实电流一定沿箭头流动. 若算得某条边的电流为负, 就表示实际方向与箭头相反.

关联矩阵
Incidence matrices

Page 1

The incidence matrix of this directed graph has one column for each node of the graph and one row for each edge of the graph:

A=​−10−1−10​1−1000​0110−1​00011​​.

有向图的incidence matrix 关联矩阵 A 用行表示边, 用列表示节点. 本例有 5 条边和 4 个节点, 所以 A 是 5×4 矩阵.

读矩阵时要固定这个顺序: 第几行对应第几条边, 第几列对应第几个节点. 后面电势向量 x 的长度是节点数 4, 电流向量 y 的长度是边数 5.

Pages 1–2

If an edge runs from node a to node b, the row corresponding to that edge has −1 in column a and 1 in column b; all other entries in that row are 0. If we were studying a larger graph we would get a larger matrix but it would be sparse; most of the entries in that matrix would be 0. This is one of the ways matrices arising from applications might have extra structure.

一条边若从节点 a 指向节点 b, 对应行就在第 a 列写 −1, 在第 b 列写 1, 其余位置写 0. 例如边①是 1→2, 所以第一行是 (−1,1,0,0).

每行至多有两个非零元. 大图的边数和节点数增加时, 矩阵会变大, 但每行仍只保留同样的记录方式, 因而大部分元素仍是 0. 这种sparse 稀疏结构来自应用本身, 可以减少存储和计算成本.

Page 2

Note that nodes 1, 2 and 3 and edges ①, ② and ③ form a loop. The matrix describing just those nodes and edges looks like:

​−10−1​1−10​011​000​​.

Note that the third row is the sum of the first two rows; loops in the graph correspond to linearly dependent rows of the matrix.

节点 1、2、3 与边①、②、③组成一个loop 回路. 单独取出这三条边, 就得到图中的三行子矩阵.

第三行恰好等于前两行之和: 沿 1→2 和 2→3 的电势差相加, 就等于沿 1→3 的电势差. 因此绕回路的边给出的方程并不完全独立, 图中的回路对应矩阵行的线性依赖.

零空间与电势
The nullspace and potentials

Page 2

To find the nullspace of A, we solve Ax=0:

Ax=​x2​−x1​x3​−x2​x3​−x1​x4​−x1​x4​−x3​​​=​00000​​.

要求 A 的nullspace 零空间, 就解 Ax=0. 矩阵乘法把每个节点的电势分量组合成各条边两端的差: 边①给出 x2​−x1​, 边②给出 x3​−x2​, 其余三行同理.

这五个差必须同时为 0. 因而 Ax 不只是抽象乘积, 它把节点上的数转换成边上的差.

Page 2

If the components xi​ of the vector x describe the electrical potential at the nodes i of the graph, then Ax is a vector describing the difference in potential across each edge of the graph. We see Ax=0 when x1​=x2​=x3​=x4​, so the nullspace has dimension 1. In terms of an electrical network, the potential difference is zero on each edge if each node has the same potential. We can’t tell what that potential is by observing the flow of electricity through the network, but if one node of the network is grounded then its potential is zero. From that we can determine the potential of all other nodes of the graph.

把 xi​ 理解为节点 i 的electrical potential 电势, Ax 就是各条边上的电势差. Ax=0 要求所有相邻节点电势相同; 图又是连通的, 所以四个电势必须全部相等.

零空间由 (c,c,c,c)T 组成, 维数为 1. 共同的常数 c 不影响任何电势差, 因而不能只靠电势差确定绝对电势. 若把一个节点ground 接地, 就是令它的电势为 0, 于是其余节点也都确定为 0.

Page 2

The matrix has 4 columns and a 1 dimensional nullspace, so its rank is 3. The first, second and fourth columns are its pivot columns; these edges connect all the nodes of the graph without forming a loop – a graph with no loops is called a tree.

矩阵有 4 列, 零空间维数是 1. 由秩–零化度关系, rankA=4−1=3. 第一、第二、第四列是主元列.

与这三条独立方程对应的是第一、第二、第四条边. 它们把四个节点全部连起来, 又不形成回路, 因而组成一棵tree 树. 在连通图中, 生成树正好提供 n−1 条独立的边.

原文指代错误: 原文先说第一、第二、第四列是主元列, 随后写「these edges connect all the nodes」 → 连接节点的应是与第一、第二、第四行对应的边①、②、④, 不是列. A 的列对应节点, 行才对应边.

左零空间与欧拉公式
The left nullspace and Euler’s formula

Page 2

The left nullspace of A consists of the solutions y to the equation: ATy=0. Since AT has 5 columns and rank 3 we know that the dimension of N(AT) is m−r=2. Note that 2 is the number of loops in the graph and m is the number of edges. The rank r is n−1, one less than the number of nodes. This gives us

# loops =# edges −(# nodes −1).

left nullspace 左零空间是 N(AT), 由满足 ATy=0 的 y 组成. AT 有 5 列、秩为 3, 所以左零空间维数是 m−r=5−3=2.

本图有两个独立回路: 小三角形 1→2→3→1, 以及三角形 1→3→4→1. 连通图的秩为 n−1, 因而独立回路数就是 m−(n−1).

Page 2

or:

number of nodes−number of edges+number of loops=1.

This is Euler’s formula for connected graphs.

把回路数公式移项, 得到「节点数 − 边数 + 回路数 =1」. 这是连通平面图的Euler’s formula 欧拉公式在本讲记号下的形式.

本例 n=4,m=5, 独立回路数为 2, 等式成立. 这里数的是独立回路, 不是把所有能走出的闭合路线都重复计算; 外侧大回路可以由两个小回路组合得到.

基尔霍夫定律
Kirchhoff’s law

Pages 2–3

In our example of an electrical network, we started with the potentials xi​ of the nodes. The matrix A then told us something about potential differences. An engineer could create a matrix C using Ohm’s law and information about the conductance of the edges and use that matrix to determine the current yi​ on each edge. Kirchhoff’s Current Law then says that ATy=0, where y is the vector with components y1​,y2​,y3​,y4​,y5​. Vectors in the nullspace of AT correspond to collections of currents that satisfy Kirchhoff’s law.

节点电势 xi​ 经 A 变成边上的电势差. 工程师再根据Ohm’s law 欧姆定律和各条边的conductance 电导构造矩阵 C, 由电势差求出各边电流 yi​.

Kirchhoff’s Current Law 基尔霍夫电流定律要求每个节点流入与流出的电流平衡, 写成 ATy=0. 所以 AT 的零空间不是无关的代数对象, 它正好收集所有满足节点电流守恒的电流组合.

x=(x1​,x2​,x3​,x4​)potentials at nodese=Ax ↓(x2​−x1​, etc.)potential differences​y=CeOhm’s Law​​ATy=0Kirchhoff’s Current Law↑ ATy(y1​,y2​,y3​,y4​,y5​)currents on edges​

这张流程图把三个量连起来: 从节点电势 x 出发, 先用 e=Ax 求各边电势差, 再用 y=Ce 求各边电流, 最后用 ATy=0 检查节点处是否守恒.

两个矩阵分工不同. A 从节点量得到边量, AT 则把边上的电流按节点汇总. 欧姆定律的 C 位于中间, 把几何连接关系与具体电路元件联系起来.

Page 3

Written out, ATy=0 looks like:

​−1100​0−110​−1010​−1001​00−11​​​y1​y2​y3​y4​y5​​​=​0000​​.

把 A 转置后, 得到 4×5 矩阵. 现在每一行对应一个节点, 每一列对应一条边; 乘以五维电流向量 y, 得到四个节点上的电流平衡式.

转置正好交换了「按边记录节点」和「按节点汇总边」这两种观点. 等号右边的四个 0 表示四个节点都没有电流净积累.

Page 3

Multiplying the first row by the column vector y we get −y1​−y3​−y4​=0. This tells us that the total current flowing out of node 1 is zero – it’s a balance equation, or a conservation law. Multiplying the second row by y tells us y1​−y2​=0; the current coming into node 2 is balanced with the current going out. Multiplying the bottom rows, we get y2​+y3​−y5​=0 and y4​+y5​=0.

第一行给出 −y1​−y3​−y4​=0: 从节点 1 流出的三条边电流之和为 0. 第二行给出 y1​−y2​=0: 经边①流入节点 2 的电流, 等于经边②流出的电流.

节点 3 的方程是 y2​+y3​−y5​=0, 节点 4 的方程是 y4​+y5​=0. 正负号都由先前选定的箭头决定, 所以这些方程本质上是同一个conservation law 守恒律在四个节点上的展开.

Pages 3–4

We could use the method of elimination on AT to find its column space, but we already know the rank. To get a basis for N(AT) we just need to find two independent vectors in this space. Looking at the equations y1​−y2​=0 we might guess y1​=y2​=1. Then we could use the conservation laws for node 3 to guess y3​=−1 and y5​=0. We satisfy the conservation conditions on node 4 with y4​=0, giving us a basis vector

​11−100​​.

This vector represents one unit of current flowing around the loop joining nodes 1, 2 and 3; a multiple of this vector represents a different amount of current around the same loop.

左零空间是二维的, 因而只需找到两个独立向量就能组成基. 第一个向量令 y1​=y2​=1,y3​=−1,y4​=y5​=0, 逐个代入四个节点方程, 都能看到流入与流出相抵.

它表示一单位电流沿 1→2→3→1 的小回路流动. 边③的参考方向是 1→3, 而回路实际沿 3→1, 所以 y3​=−1. 整个向量乘以常数, 只改变回路电流的大小或方向.

原文表述错误: 原文写「use the method of elimination on AT to find its column space」 → 本段目标是求 N(AT), 应写成用消元求 AT 的nullspace 零空间, 不是 column space 列空间.

​001−11​​.

The vector

​101−11​​

that represents a current around the outer loop is also in the nullspace, but it is the sum of the first two vectors we found.

第二个基向量对应回路 1→3→4→1: 边③沿正方向, 边④与参考方向相反而取 −1, 边⑤沿正方向, 边①、②不用而取 0.

外侧大回路的电流向量是 (1,0,1,−1,1)T, 也满足节点守恒, 但它等于两个小回路向量之和. 因此它不提供第三个独立方向; 前两个向量已经构成二维左零空间的一组基.

Page 4

We’ve almost completely covered the mathematics of simple circuits. More complex circuits might have batteries in the edges, or current sources between nodes. Adding current sources changes the ATy=0 in Kirchhoff’s current law to ATy=f. Combining the equations e=Ax, y=Ce and ATy=f gives us:

ATCAx=f.

简单电路的核心方程已经齐全. 若边上有电池, 或节点之间接入current source 电流源, 节点方程右侧就不再全是 0, 而要写成 ATy=f, 用 f 记录外部注入的电流.

把 e=Ax 代入 y=Ce, 得 y=CAx; 再代入 ATy=f, 得 ATCAx=f. 这个方程直接以节点电势 x 为未知量: 左边汇总由电势产生的电流, 右边给出外部电流源.
