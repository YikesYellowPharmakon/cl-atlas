Slide 3

NFAs and DFAs

  • DFAs (Deterministic Finite Automata) are machines for accepting/rejecting strings.
    • The language of a DFA is the set of strings it accepts.
    • The set of languages for which there exists a DFA is called the Regular Languages.
      • Spoiler: not all languages are Regular! We'll find some that you can't build a DFA for, due to DFA's finite limitation.
  • NFAs (Nondeterministic Finite Automata) are DFAs but with some bonus superpowers of having more options for how we move from state to state.

复习: DFA 接受或拒绝字符串, 存在 DFA 识别的语言叫正则语言; 预告: 并非所有语言都是正则的, 因为 DFA 只有有限内存. NFA 是带「超能力」的 DFA, 每一步可以有多种选择. 见 CS103 15.

Slide 4–5

Quick orientation to material for the rest of the quarter

Automata Progression

  • DFAs (Deterministic Finite Automata)
  • NFAs (DFA + superpowers of guessing)
  • PDAs (NFA + a stack to use as memory)
    • We'll actually explore an equivalent mechanism called CFGs, you'll see soon...
  • Turing Machines (DFA or NFA + an infinite array to use as memory)

At each step, we'll try to find a problem (language) that's too hard for the existing machine type, and motivate moving up a level in power.

后半学期的路线: DFA → NFA (加上猜测能力) → PDA 下推自动机 (NFA 加一个栈作内存; 课程实际讲的是与之等价的上下文无关文法 CFG) → 图灵机 (加一条无限长的纸带作内存). 每一步都先找一个现有机器解决不了的语言, 以此说明为什么要升级到更强的模型.

NFA 与 DFA 的等价性
DFAs and NFAs

Slide 17–22

DFAs and NFAs

  • Every language for which there exists a DFA also has an NFA.
  • Why?
    • NFAs are just DFAs with (optional to use) extra superpowers
    • So every DFA is essentially already an NFA!

NFAs and DFAs

  • What about the reverse?
  • Question: Can any language recognized by an NFA also be recognized by a DFA?
    • Seems unlikely: NFAs can do everything DFA can do, plus they have superpowers of perfect guessing the future!
  • Surprisingly, the answer is yes!
  • Theorem: For all languages \( L \), if \( L \) is recognized by an NFA, then there exists a DFA that also recognizes \( L \).
  • Proof plan:
    • Assume: Pick an arbitrary language \( L \), for which an NFA exists.
    • Want to show: We want to show that there exists a DFA for \( L \).
    • Describe how we would construct a DFA with the same language, in a generalizable way. For the next few slides, we'll ponder how to approach that...

一个方向显然: 每个 DFA 本身就是一个 NFA (只是不使用多选和 ε 转移), 所以 DFA 能识别的语言 NFA 都能识别.

反过来呢? NFA 能「完美猜测」, 看起来应该更强. 但答案出人意料: 能. 定理: 任何被 NFA 识别的语言, 都有 DFA 识别它.

证明思路按 Assume / WTS: 任取一个有 NFA 的语言 L, 要证存在 DFA 识别 L. 存在命题 → 给出一个通用的构造方法, 把任意 NFA 变成 DFA.

Slide 23–29

Key Insight: Our NFA transition function, and Massive Parallelism [from last time]

  • An NFA can be thought of as a DFA that can be in many states at once.
  • At each point in time, when the NFA needs to follow a transition, it tries all the options at the same time.

“Massive parallelism” matches how we defined the NFA transition function—we think of “where I am right now” as not just one state, but simultaneously all of some subset of the states. NFA: \( \delta : (\wp(S) \times \Sigma) \to \wp(S) \)

关键想法来自上一讲的「大规模并行」视角: NFA 在任一时刻处在一个状态集合里, 读一个字符后转到另一个确定的状态集合. 这个「集合到集合」的变化是确定的. 所以只要把每个状态集合当成一个 DFA 状态, 就得到一个 DFA.

子集构造
The Subset Construction

Slide 30–90

Putting our NFA insight into the DFA table representation...

ab
\( \{q_0\} \)\( \{q_0, q_1\} \)\( \{q_0\} \)
\( \{q_0, q_1\} \)\( \{q_0, q_1\} \)\( \{q_0, q_2\} \)
\( \{q_0, q_2\} \)\( \{q_0, q_1, q_3\} \)\( \{q_0\} \)

Quick check: What are the contents of the next row? Answer like “a = {..}, b = {...}” all in one response.

Think hard: Remember that our NFA transition function says all the possibilities for states we can be in is \( \wp(S) \). So if the original NFA had \( n = |S| \) states, how many rows might this table need to have?

Think hard follow-up: It could need up to \( 2^n \) rows! For simplicity in this lecture example, we'll stop here, but you could imagine to fully formally define the transition function \( \delta \), we would need some token entries for all \( 2^n \) elements of \( \wp(S) \).

(动画: 在输入 a b a a b a 上运行构造出的 DFA.)

以上一讲的 NFA (以 aba 结尾: q₀ 对 \( \Sigma \) 自环, a → q₁, b → q₂, a → q₃) 为例, 逐行建表:

  1. 从起始集合 {q₀} 开始. 读 a: q₀ 可留在 q₀ 或去 q₁, 得 {q₀, q₁}; 读 b: 只能留在 q₀, 得 {q₀}.
  2. 新出现的集合 {q₀, q₁} 加一行. 读 a: q₀ 给 {q₀, q₁}, q₁ 读 a 无路, 合并得 {q₀, q₁}; 读 b: q₀ 给 {q₀}, q₁ 给 {q₂}, 合并得 {q₀, q₂}.
  3. {q₀, q₂} 一行 (课堂练习): a = {q₀, q₁, q₃}, b = {q₀}.
  4. {q₀, q₁, q₃} 一行: a = {q₀, q₁}, b = {q₀, q₂}. 不再出现新集合, 停止.

含有 NFA 接受状态 q₃ 的集合 (只有 {q₀, q₁, q₃}) 是 DFA 的接受状态, 用星号标出. 下方的图就是这张表画成的 DFA.

表最多有多少行? 状态集合是 S 的子集, 共 \( 2^n \) 个, 所以最多 \( 2^n \) 行. 例子里只从起始集合出发、遇到新集合才加行, 所以只用了 4 行; 严格的定义要为所有 \( 2^n \) 个子集都定义转移, 到不了的子集不影响语言.

Slide 99–101

The Subset Construction

  • This procedure for turning an NFA for a language \( L \) into a DFA for a language \( L \) is called the subset construction.
    • It's sometimes called the powerset construction; it's different names for the same thing!
  • Intuitively:
    • Each state in the DFA corresponds to a set of states from the NFA.
    • Each transition in the DFA corresponds to what transitions would be taken in the NFA when using the massive parallel intuition.
    • The accepting states in the DFA correspond to which sets of states would be considered accepting in the NFA when using the massive parallel intuition.
  • There's an online Guide to the Subset Construction with a more elaborate example involving ε-transitions and cases where the NFA dies; check that for more details.
  • In converting an NFA to a DFA, the DFA's states correspond to sets of NFA states.
  • Useful fact: \( |\wp(S)| = 2^{|S|} \) for any finite set \( S \).
  • So, in the worst-case, the construction can result in a DFA that is exponentially larger than the original NFA.
  • Question to ponder: Can you find a family of languages that have NFAs of size \( n \), but no DFAs of size less than \( 2^n \)?

Why This Matters

  • We now have two perspectives on regular languages:
    • Regular languages are languages accepted by DFAs.
    • Regular languages are languages accepted by NFAs.
  • We can now reason about the regular languages in two different ways.

这个方法叫 subset construction 子集构造 (也叫 powerset construction 幂集构造):

  • DFA 的每个状态是 NFA 状态的一个集合;
  • DFA 的转移就是按「并行」视角, 集合里每个状态各自转移后取并集;
  • 包含 NFA 接受状态的集合是 DFA 的接受状态.

有 ε 转移时还要在每一步之后把能通过 ε 到达的状态都加进集合 (ε 闭包); NFA 路径死亡对应集合变小, 全部死亡就是空集 \( \varnothing \) (一个死状态). 课程的 Guide to the Subset Construction 有详细例子, 见本课的指南合集.

代价: n 个状态的 NFA 可能变成 \( 2^n \) 个状态的 DFA, 指数级增长. 课件留的思考题有经典答案: 语言「倒数第 n 个字符是 a」有 n + 1 个状态的 NFA (猜哪个位置是倒数第 n 个), 但任何 DFA 至少要 \( 2^n \) 个状态, 因为它必须记住最后 n 个字符.

意义: 正则语言现在有两种等价描述, 「有 DFA」和「有 NFA」. 证明某语言正则时, 可以构造更方便的 NFA.

正则语言的性质
Properties of Regular Languages

Slide 103–107

The Union of Two Languages

  • If \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) are languages over the alphabet \( \Sigma \), the language \( L_1 \cup L_2 \) is the language of all strings in at least one of the two languages.
  • If \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) are regular languages, is \( L_1 \cup L_2 \)?

Question to ponder: where have you seen this idea before?

并集: 若 L₁、L₂ 正则, \( L_1 \cup L_2 \) 也正则. 构造: 拿到识别 L₁ 和 L₂ 的自动机, 加一个新起始状态, 用两条 ε 边分别通向两个旧起始状态. 新 NFA 猜测字符串属于哪一个语言, 再交给对应的机器验证. 这正是上一讲「以 010 或 101 结尾」的「NFA 设计技巧」, 也就是课件问「在哪里见过」的答案. 得到的是 NFA, 由子集构造, 存在等价的 DFA, 所以并集正则.

Slide 108–112

The Intersection of Two Languages

  • If \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) are languages over \( \Sigma \), then \( L_1 \cap L_2 \) is the language of strings in both \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \).
  • Question: If \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) are regular, is \( L_1 \cap L_2 \) regular as well?

Hey, it's De Morgan's laws!

交集也正则, 证明不需要新构造, 用德摩根律:

\[ L_1 \cap L_2 = \overline{\overline{L_1} \cup \overline{L_2}} \]

补运算保持正则, 并运算保持正则, 所以右边每一步都正则. 这里的德摩根律是第 3 讲命题版本 \( \neg(p \lor q) \equiv \neg p \land \neg q \) 的集合版本: 「不在补集的并里」就是「两个补集都不在」, 也就是同时在 L₁ 和 L₂ 里.

Slide 114–117

String Concatenation

  • If \( w \in \Sigma^* \) and \( x \in \Sigma^* \), the concatenation of \( w \) and \( x \), denoted \( wx \), is the string formed by tacking all the characters of \( x \) onto the end of \( w \).
  • Example: if \( w = \) quo and \( x = \) kka, the concatenation \( wx = \) quokka.
  • This is analogous to the + operator for strings in many programming languages.
  • Some facts about concatenation:
    • The empty string \( \varepsilon \) is the identity element for concatenation: \( w\varepsilon = \varepsilon w = w \)
    • Concatenation is associative: \( wxy = w(xy) = (wx)y \)

Concatenation

  • The concatenation of two languages \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) over the alphabet \( \Sigma \) is the language

    \( L_1 L_2 = \{ wx \in \Sigma^* \mid w \in L_1 \land x \in L_2 \} \)

  • Two views of \( L_1 L_2 \):
    • The set of all strings that can be made by concatenating a string in \( L_1 \) with a string in \( L_2 \).
    • The set of strings that can be split into two pieces: a piece from \( L_1 \) and a piece from \( L_2 \).
  • This is closely related to, but different than, the Cartesian product.

Question to ponder: In what ways are concatenations similar to Cartesian products? In what ways are they different?

字符串的 concatenation 连接: 把 x 接在 w 后面, 如 quo 接 kka 得 quokka, 相当于编程里字符串的 +. 空串是单位元 (接上空串不变), 连接满足结合律, 但不满足交换律 (ab ≠ ba).

语言的连接 \( L_1 L_2 \): 所有「L₁ 中一个串接上 L₂ 中一个串」得到的串. 也可以从反方向看: 能被切成「前半属于 L₁、后半属于 L₂」的串.

思考题: 和笛卡尔积相似之处是都把两个集合的元素两两组合; 不同之处是笛卡尔积得到有序对, 不同的对一定不同; 连接把两部分粘成一个串, 不同的组合可能粘出同一个串 (如 {a, ab} 和 {b, ε}: a·b 和 ab·ε 都是 ab), 所以 \( |L_1 L_2| \) 可能小于 \( |L_1| \cdot |L_2| \).

Slide 116

Concatenation Example

  • Let \( \Sigma = \{ a, b, \dots, z, A, B, \dots, Z \} \) and consider these languages over \( \Sigma \):

    Noun = { Puppy, Rainbow, Whale, … }
    Verb = { Hugs, Juggles, Loves, … }
    The = { The }

  • The language TheNounVerbTheNoun is

    { ThePuppyHugsTheWhale, TheWhaleLovesTheRainbow, TheRainbowJugglesTheRainbow, … }

例子: 名词、动词、The 各是一个语言. 依次连接 The · Noun · Verb · The · Noun, 得到所有形如「The 名词 动词 The 名词」的串 (没有空格). 这有点像一个极简的语法, 预示后面的上下文无关文法.

Slide 118–129

Concatenating Regular Languages

  • If \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) are regular languages, is \( L_1 L_2 \)?
  • Intuition – can we split a string \( w \) into two strings \( xy \) such that \( x \in L_1 \) and \( y \in L_2 \)?
  • Idea:
    • Run a DFA/NFA for \( L_1 \) on \( w \).
    • Whenever it reaches an accepting state, optionally hand the rest of \( w \) to a DFA/NFA for \( L_2 \).
    • If the automaton for \( L_2 \) accepts the rest, \( w \in L_1 L_2 \).
    • If the automaton for \( L_2 \) rejects the remainder, the split was incorrect.

(动画: 字符串 bookkeeper, 试各种切分位置.)

连接也保持正则. 难点是不知道在哪里切分 (例如 bookkeeper 可以切成 book · keeper, 也可以 bookk · eeper). 用非确定性来猜切分点:

  1. 先在 L₁ 的机器上运行;
  2. 每当到达 L₁ 的接受状态, 可以选择沿 ε 边跳到 L₂ 机器的起点, 把剩下的部分交给它;
  3. L₂ 的机器接受剩余部分, 就说明找到了正确的切分.

构造上: L₁ 的接受状态改为非接受, 并加 ε 边到 L₂ 的起点; 只保留 L₂ 的接受状态. 猜错的切分只是一条死掉或不接受的路径.

Slide 130–131

Lots and Lots of Concatenation

  • Consider the language \( L = \{ aa, b \} \)
  • \( LL \) is the set of strings formed by concatenating pairs of strings in \( L \).

    { aaaa, aab, baa, bb }

  • \( LLL \) is the set of strings formed by concatenating triples of strings in \( L \).

    { aaaaaa, aaaab, aabaa, aabb, baaaa, baab, bbaa, bbb }

  • \( LLLL \) is the set of strings formed by concatenating quadruples of strings in \( L \).

    { aaaaaaaa, aaaaaab, aaaabaa, aaaabb, aabaaaa, aabaab, aabbaa, aabbb, baaaaaa, baaaab, baabaa, baabb, bbaaaa, bbaab, bbbaa, bbbb }

Language Exponentiation

  • We can define what it means to “exponentiate” a language as follows:
  • \( L^0 = \{\varepsilon\} \)
    • Intuition: The only string you can form by gluing no strings together is the empty string.
    • Notice that \( \{\varepsilon\} \ne \varnothing \). Can you explain why?
  • \( L^{n+1} = L L^n \)
    • Idea: Concatenating \( (n+1) \) strings together works by concatenating \( n \) strings, then concatenating one more.
  • Question to ponder: Why define \( L^0 = \{\varepsilon\} \)?
  • Question to ponder: What is \( \varnothing^0 \)?

反复连接同一个语言: L = {aa, b} 时, LL 有 4 个串, LLL 有 8 个, LLLL 有 16 个 (每个位置选 aa 或 b, 这里不同选法给出不同的串).

语言的「幂」用递归定义: \( L^0 = \{\varepsilon\} \) (零个串粘起来只得到空串), \( L^{n+1} = L L^n \). 几个思考题:

  • \( \{\varepsilon\} \ne \varnothing \): 前者有一个元素 (空串), 后者没有元素.
  • 为什么 \( L^0 = \{\varepsilon\} \): 它是连接的单位元, \( \{\varepsilon\} L = L \), 这样 \( L^1 = L L^0 = L \), 符合直觉; 类似数的 \( x^0 = 1 \).
  • \( \varnothing^0 = \{\varepsilon\} \): 按定义如此, 和 \( \varnothing \) 本身无关. 而 \( \varnothing^1 = \varnothing \{\varepsilon\} = \varnothing \).

The Kleene Star

Slide 133–134

The Kleene Closure

  • An important operation on languages is the Kleene Closure, which is defined as

    \( L^* = \{ w \in \Sigma^* \mid \exists n \in \mathbb{N}.\ w \in L^n \} \)

  • Mathematically: \( w \in L^* \leftrightarrow \exists n \in \mathbb{N}.\ w \in L^n \)
  • Intuitively, \( L^* \) is the language all possible ways of concatenating zero or more strings in \( L \) together, possibly with repetition.
  • Question to ponder: What is \( \varnothing^* \)?

If \( L = \{ a, bb \} \), then \( L^* = \{ \varepsilon, a, bb, aa, abb, bba, bbbb, aaa, aabb, abba, abbbb, bbaa, bbabb, bbbba, bbbbbb, \dots \} \)

Think of \( L^* \) as the set of strings you can make if you have a collection of stamps – one for each string in \( L \) – and you form every possible string that can be made from those stamps.

Kleene closure 克林闭包 (克林星号) \( L^* \): 由 L 中的串任意选取、可重复、连接零个或多个得到的所有串, 即所有 \( L^n \) 的并. 比喻: L 的每个串是一枚印章, \( L^* \) 是用这些印章能盖出来的所有串 (包括一枚都不盖的空串).

思考题: \( \varnothing^* = \{\varepsilon\} \). 没有印章可用, 只能盖出空串 (n = 0). 这也解释了 \( \Sigma^* \) 的记号: 把字母表看成单字符串的语言, 它的克林闭包正是所有字符串.

Slide 135–136

Reasoning about Infinity

  • If \( L \) is regular, is \( L^* \) necessarily regular?
  • ⚠ A Bad Line of Reasoning: ⚠
    • \( L^0 = \{ \varepsilon \} \) is regular.
    • \( L^1 = L \) is regular.
    • \( L^2 = LL \) is regular
    • \( L^3 = L(LL) \) is regular
    • …
    • Regular languages are closed under union.
    • So the union of all these languages is regular.

We won't get into the reasons why, but infinity just doesn't work this way where it neatly extends from the combination of a bunch of finite things.

Idea: Can we directly convert an NFA for language \( L \) to an NFA for language \( L^* \)?

一个错误论证: 每个 \( L^n \) 都正则 (反复用连接的封闭性), 正则语言对并封闭, 所以无穷多个的并 \( L^* \) 也正则. 错在: 封闭性只保证有限次并保持正则 (每次并一个, 可以归纳到任意有限次), 不能推广到无穷多个. 反例: 每个单元素语言 \( \{a^n b^n\} \) 都正则, 但它们的无穷并 \( \{a^n b^n \mid n \in \mathbb{N}\} \) 不正则 (后面会证明).

所以要换思路: 直接把 L 的 NFA 改造成 \( L^* \) 的 NFA.

Slide 137–143

The Kleene Star

Question: Why add the new state out front? Why not just make the old start state accepting?

构造 \( L^* \) 的 NFA:

  1. 加一个新的起始状态, 设为接受 (这样接受空串, 对应连接零个串).
  2. 从新起始状态用 ε 边进入 L 的旧起始状态.
  3. L 的每个接受状态用 ε 边回到新起始状态: 读完一段 L 中的串后, 可以选择再开始下一段.

思考题的答案: 为什么不直接把旧起始状态设为接受? 因为旧机器里可能有边回到旧起始状态. 例如 L = 「以 a 结尾的串」, 其 DFA 读 b 回到起始状态. 把起始状态设为接受后, 串 b 也会被接受, 但 b 不在 \( L^* \) 里 (b 不能由以 a 结尾的串拼成). 新加的起始状态没有边进来 (除了从接受状态来的 ε 边), 不会出现这种问题.

Slide 144–145

Closure Properties

  • Theorem: If \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) are regular languages over an alphabet \( \Sigma \), then so are the following languages:
    • \( \overline{L_1} \)
    • \( L_1 \cup L_2 \)
    • \( L_1 \cap L_2 \)
    • \( L_1 L_2 \)
    • \( L_1^* \)
  • These properties are called closure properties of the regular languages.
