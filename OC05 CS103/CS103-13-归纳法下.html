归纳法的变体: 推迟起点, 改变步长
Variations on Induction: Starting Later, Step Sizes Other than +1

Slide 11–12

Induction Starting at 0

  • To prove that \( P(n) \) is true for all natural numbers greater than or equal to 0:
    • Show that \( P(0) \) is true.
    • Show that for an arbitrary \( k \ge 0 \), that if \( P(k) \) is true, then \( P(k+1) \) is true.
    • Conclude \( P(n) \) holds for all natural numbers greater than or equal to 0.

Induction Starting at m

  • To prove that \( P(n) \) is true for all natural numbers greater than or equal to \( m \):
    • Show that \( P(m) \) is true.
    • Show that for an arbitrary \( k \ge m \), that if \( P(k) \) is true, then \( P(k+1) \) is true.
    • Conclude \( P(n) \) holds for all natural numbers greater than or equal to \( m \).

有的命题只对 \( n \ge m \) 成立 (例如 \( 2^n \gt n^2 \) 只对 \( n \ge 5 \) 成立). 这时把起点挪到 m: 基础步证 P(m), 归纳步对任意 \( k \ge m \) 证 P(k) → P(k + 1), 结论是 P(n) 对所有 \( n \ge m \) 成立.

易错: 归纳步里的 k 也要限制为 \( k \ge m \), 因为 k < m 时 P(k) 可能根本不成立, 证明里也可能用到 \( k \ge m \) 这个条件.

例子: 证明 \( n \ge 5 \) 时 \( 2^n \gt n^2 \). n = 4 时 16 = 16 不成立, 所以必须从 5 起步.

  1. 基础步: \( 2^5 = 32 \gt 25 = 5^2 \).
  2. 归纳步: 任取 \( k \ge 5 \), 假设 \( 2^k \gt k^2 \). 则 \( 2^{k+1} = 2 \cdot 2^k \gt 2k^2 \). 只需 \( 2k^2 \ge (k+1)^2 \), 即 \( k^2 \ge 2k + 1 \), 这在 \( k \ge 3 \) 时成立, 当然也在 \( k \ge 5 \) 时成立.

归纳步里用到了 k 的下界, 这就是为什么归纳步的 k 要写成「任取 \( k \ge m \)」.

Each of the original corners needs to be covered by a corner of the new smaller squares.

# corners: 4   # squares: <4

By the pigeonhole principle, at least one smaller square needs to cover at least two of the original square's corners.

逐个看 n. n = 1 平凡成立 (不分). n = 2, 3 不可能:

  • 原正方形的 4 个角, 每个都必须被某个小正方形的一个角盖住.
  • 只有 2 或 3 个小正方形时, 4 个角分给不到 4 个正方形, 由鸽巢原理, 有一个小正方形要同时盖住原正方形的两个角.
  • 盖住两个相邻的角, 它的边长就等于原正方形的边长, 于是它就是整个原正方形, 不可能还有别的小正方形. 盖住两个对角也一样. 所以 n = 2, 3 不行.

n = 4 可以: 横竖各切一刀.

Slide 30–34

# corners: 4   # squares: 5

At least one square cannot be covering any of the original corners

n = 5 也不可能. 思路: 4 个角至多被 4 个小正方形盖住, 所以 5 个里至少有一个碰不到任何角. 课件只给了这个提示, 完整论证要再细一些: 如果某个小正方形盖住两个角, 它就是整个正方形; 所以四个角恰好由四个不同的小正方形盖住, 它们分别靠在四个角上, 第五个正方形夹在中间, 可以论证此时剩下的区域无法恰好是一个正方形.

Slide 43–54

An Insight

  • If we can subdivide a square into \( n \) squares, we can also subdivide it into \( n + 3 \) squares.
  • Since we can subdivide a bigger square into 6, 7, and 8 squares, we can subdivide a square into \( n \) squares for any \( n \ge 6 \):
    • For multiples of three, start with 6 and keep adding three squares until \( n \) is reached.
    • For numbers congruent to one modulo three, start with 7 and keep adding three squares until \( n \) is reached.
    • For numbers congruent to two modulo three, start with 8 and keep adding three squares until \( n \) is reached.

关键观察: 若已能分成 n 个, 把其中任意一个小正方形再横竖切成 4 份, 去掉 1 个、加上 4 个, 就得到 n + 3 个. 所以从 1 能跳到 4, 7, 10, 13, ….

要覆盖所有 \( n \ge 6 \), 需要三条起点不同的链: 按 n 除以 3 的余数分类, 余 0 从 6 出发, 余 1 从 7 出发, 余 2 从 8 出发, 每次加 3. 6, 7, 8 三个起点覆盖了三种余数.

Slide 55–67

Theorem: For any \( n \ge 6 \), there is a way to subdivide a square into \( n \) smaller squares.

Proof: Let \( P(n) \) be the statement “there is a way to subdivide a square into \( n \) smaller squares.” We will prove by induction that \( P(n) \) holds for all \( n \ge 6 \), from which the theorem follows.

As our base cases, we prove \( P(6) \), \( P(7) \), and \( P(8) \), that a square can be subdivided into 6, 7, and 8 squares. This is shown here:

For the inductive step, assume that for some arbitrary \( k \ge 6 \) that \( P(k) \) is true and that there is a way to subdivide a square into \( k \) squares. We want to show \( P(k+3) \), that there is a way to subdivide a square into \( k+3 \) squares. To see this, start by obtaining (via the inductive hypothesis) a subdivision of a square into \( k \) squares. Then, choose any of the squares and split it into four equal squares. This removes one of the \( k \) squares and adds four more, so there will be a net total of \( k+3 \) squares. Thus \( P(k+3) \) holds, completing the induction. \( \blacksquare \)

正式证明的两处变化:

  • 三个基础情形 P(6)、P(7)、P(8), 用图直接构造.
  • 步长为 3: 归纳步证 P(k) → P(k + 3), 对任意 \( k \ge 6 \).

为什么这就覆盖了所有 \( n \ge 6 \): 任何这样的 n 都可以写成 6、7 或 8 再加上若干个 3, 从对应的基础情形出发, 反复用归纳步即可到达.

Slide 68

Generalizing Induction

  • When doing a proof by induction,
    • feel free to use multiple base cases, and
    • feel free to take steps of sizes other than one.
  • If you do, make sure that…
    • … you actually need all your base cases. Avoid redundant base cases that are already covered by a mix of other base cases and your inductive step.
    • … you cover all the numbers you need to cover. Trace out your reasoning and make sure all the numbers you need to cover really are covered.
  • As with a proof by cases, you don't need to separately prove you've covered all the options. We trust you. 😃

推广的归纳法可以用多个基础情形, 也可以用不是 1 的步长. 但要注意两点:

  • 不要多余的基础情形: 比如步长 3 时, 如果再加上 P(9) 作基础情形就是多余的, 它已经能由 P(6) 加一步得到.
  • 不要漏掉数: 自己顺着推理走一遍, 确认需要的每个数都能到达. 例如步长 3 只给 P(6)、P(7) 两个基础情形, 余 2 的那些 n 就漏了.

和分情况证明一样, 不必在证明里正式论证「覆盖了所有情况」, 但自己要确认.

强归纳
Complete Induction

Slide 71–73

Let \( P \) be some predicate. The principle of complete induction states that if

\( P(0) \) is true — If it starts true…
and
for all \( k \in \mathbb{N} \), if \( P(0) \), …, and \( P(k) \) are true, then \( P(k+1) \) is true — …and it stays true…
then
\( \forall n \in \mathbb{N}.\ P(n) \) — …then it's always true.

Mathematical Induction

  • You can write proofs using the principle of mathematical induction as follows:
    • Define some predicate \( P(n) \) to prove by induction on \( n \).
    • Choose and prove a base case (probably, but not always, \( P(0) \)).
    • Pick an arbitrary \( k \in \mathbb{N} \) and assume that \( P(k) \) is true.
    • Prove \( P(k+1) \).
    • Conclude that \( P(n) \) holds for all \( n \in \mathbb{N} \).

Complete Induction

  • You can write proofs using the principle of complete induction as follows:
    • Define some predicate \( P(n) \) to prove by induction on \( n \).
    • Choose and prove a base case (probably, but not always, \( P(0) \)).
    • Pick an arbitrary \( k \in \mathbb{N} \) and assume that \( P(0) \), \( P(1) \), \( P(2) \), …, and \( P(k) \) are all true.
    • Prove \( P(k+1) \).
    • Conclude that \( P(n) \) holds for all \( n \in \mathbb{N} \).

complete induction 强归纳 (也叫完全归纳): 归纳步不只假设 P(k), 而是假设 P(0), P(1), …, P(k) 全部成立, 再证 P(k + 1). 写法和普通归纳几乎一样, 只有归纳假设那一句不同.

为什么合理: 当我们要证 P(k + 1) 时, 前面的 P(0) 到 P(k) 本来都已经一个个推出来了, 用其中任意一个都没问题. 两种归纳的证明能力其实相同, 强归纳只是用起来方便.

强归纳的经典例子: 每个 \( n \ge 2 \) 的整数都能写成素数的乘积. 归纳步: 若 k + 1 是素数, 它本身就是; 否则 k + 1 = ab, 其中 \( 2 \le a, b \le k \). a 和 b 比 k + 1 小, 但具体小多少不知道, 用强归纳假设 P(a)、P(b) 成立, 把两者的分解乘起来就得到 k + 1 的分解. 普通归纳只假设 P(k), 这里用不上.

Slide 74–81

Eating a Chocolate Bar

  • You have a \( 1 \times n \) chocolate bar subdivided into \( 1 \times 1 \) squares.
  • You eat the chocolate bar from left to right by breaking off one or more squares and eating them in one (possibly enormous) bite.
  • How many ways can you eat a… \( 1 \times 1 \) chocolate bar? \( 1 \times 2 \) chocolate bar? \( 1 \times 3 \) chocolate bar? \( 1 \times 4 \) chocolate bar?

There are eight ways to eat a \( 1 \times 4 \) chocolate bar.

  • There's… 1 way to eat a \( 1 \times 1 \) chocolate bar, 2 ways to eat a \( 1 \times 2 \) chocolate bar, 4 ways to eat a \( 1 \times 3 \) chocolate bar, and 8 ways to eat a \( 1 \times 4 \) chocolate bar.
  • Our guess: There are \( 2^{n-1} \) ways to eat a \( 1 \times n \) chocolate bar for any natural number \( n \ge 1 \).
  • And we think it has something to do with this insight: we eat the bar either by
    • eating the whole thing in one bite, or
    • eating some piece of size \( k \), then eating the remaining \( n - k \) pieces however we'd like.
  • Let's formalize this!

一条 1 × n 的巧克力, 从左往右吃, 每口掰下若干块 (至少一块) 吃掉. 有多少种吃法? 1 × 4 时有 8 种 (图中每行一种). 列出来: 1, 2, 4, 8, 猜想是 \( 2^{n-1} \).

直觉: 方块之间有 n − 1 条缝, 每条缝要么在这里掰开, 要么不掰, 共 \( 2^{n-1} \) 种. 课件用另一个思路做归纳: 第一口要么整条吃掉, 要么先吃掉长度 r 的一段, 剩下的长度 n − r 随便怎么吃.

用「缝」的说法验证 n = 3: 两条缝, 各自掰或不掰, 4 种: 一口吃完 (都不掰), 1 + 2 (掰第一条), 2 + 1 (掰第二条), 1 + 1 + 1 (都掰). 和课件的 4 种一致. 这种直接计数也是一个完整的证明, 课件选择归纳是为了演示强归纳.

Slide 82–98

Theorem: For any natural number \( n \ge 1 \), there are exactly \( 2^{n-1} \) ways to eat a \( 1 \times n \) chocolate bar from left to right.

Proof: Let \( P(n) \) be the statement “there are exactly \( 2^{n-1} \) ways to eat a \( 1 \times n \) chocolate bar from left to right.” We will prove by induction that \( P(n) \) holds for all natural numbers \( n \ge 1 \), from which the theorem follows.

As our base case, we prove \( P(1) \), that there is exactly \( 2^{1-1} = 1 \) way to eat a \( 1 \times 1 \) chocolate bar from left to right. The only option here is to eat the entire chocolate bar at once, so there's just one way to eat it, as needed.

For our inductive step, assume for some arbitrary natural number \( k \ge 1 \) that \( P(1) \), …, and \( P(k) \) are true. We need to show \( P(k+1) \) is true, that there are exactly \( 2^k \) ways to eat a \( 1 \times (k+1) \) chocolate bar.

There are two options for how to eat the bar. First, we can eat the whole chocolate bar in one bite. Second, we could eat a piece of size \( r \) for some \( 1 \le r \le k \), leaving a chocolate bar of size \( k+1-r \), then eat that chocolate bar from left to right. Since \( 1 \le r \le k \), we know that \( 1 \le k+1-r \le k \), so by our inductive hypothesis there are \( 2^{k-r} \) ways to eat the remainder. Summing up this first option, plus all choices of \( r \) for the second option, we see that the number of ways to eat the chocolate bar is

\[ 1 + 2^{k-1} + 2^{k-2} + \dots + 2^2 + 2^1 + 2^0 = 1 + 2^k - 1 = 2^k. \]

Thus \( P(k+1) \) holds, completing the induction. \( \blacksquare \)

为什么这里需要强归纳: 吃掉第一口 r 块之后, 剩下的长度是 k + 1 − r, 可以是 1 到 k 之间的任何长度, 不只是 k. 普通归纳只假设了 P(k), 不够用; 强归纳假设 P(1) 到 P(k) 都成立, 每种剩余长度都能处理.

计数:

  1. 一口吃完: 1 种.
  2. 第一口吃 r 块 (r = 1, …, k): 剩下长度 k + 1 − r, 由归纳假设有 \( 2^{(k+1-r)-1} = 2^{k-r} \) 种吃法.
  3. r 从 1 到 k, 得到 \( 2^{k-1} + \dots + 2^0 \), 用上一讲的公式等于 \( 2^k - 1 \).
  4. 加上一口吃完的 1 种, 共 \( 2^k \) 种, 正是 \( 2^{(k+1)-1} \).

这就是上一讲预告的「2 的幂求和公式的另一个用途」.

Slide 100–102

Induction vs. Complete Induction

Regular induction is great when you know exactly how much smaller your “smaller” problem instance is.

Complete induction is great when you know things get smaller, but you're not sure by how much.

选择的经验: 如果把大问题化小时确切知道会小多少 (比如正好少一个元素), 用普通归纳; 如果只知道会变小、但不知道小多少 (比如巧克力剩下的长度不定, 或者把一个数分解成两个因数), 用强归纳.

归纳法的错误示范: 所有马都同色
How Not to Induct

Slide 104–112

All Horses are the Same Color

\( P(n) \) = “All groups of \( n \) horses always have the same color”

Base case: \( n = 0 \). \( P(0) \) = “All groups of 0 horses always have the same color” — Vacuously true!

Assume \( P(k) \) = “All groups of \( k \) horses always have the same color” (for the sake of illustration, pretend arbitrarily picked \( k = 5 \))

Prove \( P(k+1) \) = “All groups of \( k+1 \) horses always have the same color” (so \( k + 1 \) group is 6)

By \( P(k) \), these \( k \) horses have the same color

These horses in the middle were in both sets

And we said that both horses on the ends are the same color as these overlapping horses

So all \( k+1 \) horses have the same color!

一个荒谬的「定理」: 所有马都是同一种颜色. 「证明」用归纳法:

  1. P(n): 任何 n 匹马的一组颜色都相同. P(0) 空真.
  2. 假设 P(k), 拿一组 k + 1 匹马 (图示 k = 5, 共 6 匹).
  3. 去掉最后一匹, 剩下 k 匹, 由归纳假设同色; 去掉第一匹, 剩下 k 匹, 也同色.
  4. 中间的马在两组里都有, 所以两端的马都和中间的马同色, 全部同色.

结论显然是错的, 错在哪里?

Slide 113–114

⚠ Incorrect! ⚠ Proof: Let \( P(n) \) be the statement “all groups of \( n \) horses are the same color.” We will prove by induction that \( P(n) \) holds for all natural numbers \( n \), from which the theorem follows.

As our base case, we prove \( P(0) \), that all groups of 0 horses are the same color. This statement is vacuously true because there are no horses.

For the inductive step, assume that for an arbitrary natural number \( k \) that \( P(k) \) is true and that all groups of \( k \) horses are the same color. Now consider a group of \( k+1 \) horses. Exclude the last horse and look only at the first \( k \) horses. By the inductive hypothesis, these horses are the same color. Next, exclude the first horse and look only at the last \( k \) horses. Again we see by the inductive hypothesis that these horses are the same color.

Therefore, the first horse is the same color as the non-excluded horses, who in turn are the same color as the last horse. Hence the first horse excluded, the non-excluded horses, and last horse excluded are all of the same color. Thus \( P(k+1) \) holds, completing the induction. \( \blacksquare \)

Which paragraph has the error?

正式写出的错误证明有四段. 课堂提问「哪一段有错」: 错在第四段 (「Therefore, the first horse is the same color as the non-excluded horses …」). 它默认存在「两组共有的中间的马」, 而这并不是对每个 k 都成立. 前三段本身没有问题.

Slide 115–124

What's going on here?

The logic in our inductive step does not allow us to get from \( P(1) \) to \( P(2) \). Specifically, there are no non-excluded horses that were in both sets.

Assume \( P(k) \) = “All groups of \( k \) horses always have the same color” (now let's pretend arbitrarily picked \( k = 1 \))

Prove \( P(k+1) \) = “All groups of \( k+1 \) horses always have the same color” (so \( k + 1 \) group is 2)

By \( P(k) \), these \( k \) horses have the same color (so \( k + 1 \) group is 1)

The horses in the middle were in both sets !!!

问题出在 k = 1: 一组 2 匹马, 去掉最后一匹得到第一匹, 去掉第一匹得到第二匹. 两组各只有一匹, 没有重叠, 第四段的「中间的马」不存在, 推不出两匹马同色.

所以 P(1) → P(2) 这一步断了. 归纳步必须对每一个 k 都成立; 只要有一个 k 失败, 后面所有的数都推不到. k = 5 的图示恰好挑了一个能成立的 k, 让错误看不出来. 这也提醒: 用具体例子理解证明时, 要特别检查最小的几个 k.

那 k = 0 的一步 (从 0 匹到 1 匹) 呢? 一组 1 匹马, 「第一匹」和「最后一匹」是同一匹, 结论「它和自己同色」平凡成立, 所以 P(1) 本身是真的. 真正断掉的只有 P(1) → P(2) 这一环, 但这一环断了, P(2), P(3), … 就全都推不出来. 如果基础步改成 P(2) 并能证明 (任意两匹马同色), 那么后面每一步都有重叠的马, 结论才会成立; 可惜 P(2) 本身是假的.

幻灯片小错: Slide 119 的注释写 “By P(k), these k horses have the same color (so k + 1 group is 1)”, 这里说的是 k = 1 的那一组, 应为 “(so k group is 1)”; 后一页 Slide 120 又恢复为 “(so k + 1 group is 2)”.
