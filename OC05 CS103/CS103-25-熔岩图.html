(Lava Diagram Problem Tips)

  • You've seen two examples of RE (but not R) problems so far: \( A_{TM} \) and HALT. They both have this form:

    L = {⟨M,w⟩ | /* something about M's behavior on w */}

  • You've also seen how we used the Trickster approach to prove both are undecidable (not in R).
  • You may have found yourself thinking, “Couldn't I always just use Trickster on any language that looks like ⟨M⟩ or ⟨M,w⟩ or similar, to show it's undecidable?”
  • And the answer, in fact, is yes! (with a couple caveats)

「熔岩图」(lava diagram) 是课程对语言分类图的叫法: 正则 ⊂ 上下文无关 ⊂ R ⊂ RE ⊂ 所有语言, 越往外越「烫手」. 作业要求把给定语言放进正确的区域. 这一讲给出分类的工具: Rice 定理 (判断是否在 R 外) 和 RE 外语言的直觉. 重点是 Rice 定理的两条附加条件.

已知的 RE − R 例子 \( A_{TM} \) 和 HALT 都形如「⟨M, w⟩ 使得 M 在 w 上的行为满足某条件」, 都用 trickster 证明不可判定. 是否任何这种形式的语言都可以如法炮制? 答案是可以, 但有两条附加条件.

Slide 5–6

Rice's Theorem

Any language that consists of strings that are TM code, and filters those TMs based on a criteria that has anything to do with the TM's language or behavior, is undecidable.

  • So languages matching these templates will always be undecidable:
    • L = {⟨M⟩ | /* something about M's behavior */}
    • L = {⟨M⟩ | /* something about M's language */}
    • L = {⟨M1, M2⟩ | /* something comparing M1 and M2's languages or behaviors */}
  • Example language or behavior criteria: “M accepts at least one string,” “M's language is finite,” “M loops on w”

Reminder: Undecidable means not in R. At best in RE, maybe not even that.

Rice's theorem 莱斯定理 (课件的通俗表述): 由图灵机代码组成、按图灵机的语言或行为筛选的语言, 都不可判定. 三种模板: 关于 M 的行为、关于 M 的语言、比较两台机器. 例子: 「M 至少接受一个串」「M 的语言有限」「M 在 w 上死循环」.

不可判定只说明不在 R 中, 它可能在 RE 中, 也可能连 RE 都不在.

课件的说法比教科书宽. 严格的 Rice 定理只针对语言的性质 (semantic property): 若一个性质只取决于 \( \mathscr{L}(M) \) (两台语言相同的机器要么都满足要么都不满足), 并且非平凡 (有机器满足、也有机器不满足), 则 \( \{ \langle M\rangle \mid \mathscr{L}(M) \text{ 具有该性质} \} \) 不可判定. 关于「行为」的条件 (例如「M 在 w 上死循环」「M 运行超过 100 步」) 不在定理的直接范围内, 有的仍不可判定 (前者), 有的反而可判定 (后者, 只需模拟 100 步). 做题时遇到行为类条件, 最好用 trickster 单独论证.

Slide 7–9
  • Caveat 1: not all languages of the form L = {⟨M⟩ | /* criteria */} are undecidable; only criteria about the TM's language or behavior.
  • Questions relating purely to the text of the code, and not its behavior when run are generally decidable. Decidable examples:
    • L1 = {⟨M,w⟩ | the string ⟨M⟩ contains more a's than the string w}
    • L2 = {⟨M⟩ | the string ⟨M⟩ has odd length}
  • Intuition: You don't need to try running the TM M to see how many a's its code has or that its code is an odd-length string.

附加条件一: 条件必须关于 M 的语言或行为. 只关于代码文本的问题通常可判定, 例如「M 的代码里的 a 比 w 多」「M 的代码长度是奇数」. 这些只需数一数字符, 不用运行 M.

Slide 10–12
  • Caveat 2: not all languages of the form L = {⟨M⟩ | /* criteria */} are undecidable, only if the criteria actually filters. In other words the criteria can't be true of zero TMs or all TMs.
    • L = {⟨M⟩ | /* criteria true of all TMs */} = Σ*, a regular language!
      • Example: {⟨M⟩ | /* M has at least one state */}
    • L = {⟨M⟩ | /* criteria true of zero TMs */} = ∅, also a regular language!
      • Example: ??
    • Intuition: A problem can't be hard if there's no real question there.

PollEv: Give an example of a criteria that is true of zero TMs.

附加条件二: 条件必须真正起到筛选作用, 不能对所有图灵机都成立, 也不能对所有都不成立. 对所有都成立时语言就是 Σ* (假设每个串都解释成某台图灵机), 对所有都不成立时是空集, 两者都是正则语言, 当然可判定. 没有真正的问题, 也就谈不上难.

投票题的答案举例: 「M 接受的串的个数是负数」「M 既接受又不接受某个串」「\( \mathscr{L}(M) \) 不是 Σ* 的子集」, 这些对任何图灵机都不成立.

L = Σ* 可判定 (正则, 全部接受). L = {⟨M⟩ | M 是 ∅ 的识别器}, 也就是「M 不接受任何串」, 是关于 M 的语言的非平凡性质 (有的机器满足, 有的不满足), 由 Rice 定理不可判定. 进一步, 它连 RE 都不在: 它的补「M 至少接受一个串」在 RE 中却不在 R 中, 由下文「不可判定 RE 语言的补在 RE 外」可知.

RE 之外
Beyond RE

Slide 14–18

(图: Rice's Theorem helps us identify what is outside R (may or may not be in RE). What is outside RE?)

How Many Problems Are Outside the Reach of Turing Machines?

  • It's sad to think about problems that no TM can even recognize!
    • Not just undecidable but unrecognizable!
    • How many are there? Hopefully only a few!
  • A TM is fundamentally just a string (a piece of code). Let's say we can interpret any string as a TM (if not syntactically correct according to some TM code system, we'll just say it is a TM that always rejects).
  • So the number of TMs that exist is \( |\Sigma^*| \) (the count of all possible strings).
  • The number of languages that exist is \( |\wp(\Sigma^*)| \) (the count of all possible sets of strings).
  • Sadly, Cantor's Theorem says \( |\Sigma^*| \lt |\wp(\Sigma^*)| \).
    • Not nearly enough Turing machines to go around for all the languages that exist.
  • So there are many problems (languages) that we can't solve with TMs.
    • Infinitely many!!

Rice 定理帮我们判断语言在 R 之外; 那 RE 之外有多少语言? 计数论证 (回到第 00 讲): 每台图灵机就是一段代码, 即一个字符串 (语法不合法的串约定为总拒绝的机器), 所以图灵机最多 \( |\Sigma^*| \) 个; 语言是字符串的集合, 共 \( |\wp(\Sigma^*)| \) 个. 由康托尔定理后者严格更大, 每台机器只识别一个语言, 所以绝大多数语言没有识别器, 有无穷多个.

Slide 19–20

(图: What does an outside-RE problem look like?)

The Class Unrecognizable (Lava Diagram Category “All Languages”)

  • Languages \( L \) that are outside RE are those where:
    • We cannot even build a recognizer TM \( M \), where \( \mathscr{L}(M) = L \).
    • We cannot build a verifier for \( L \).
      • i.e., there is no finite-length certificate/hint that proves a string is in \( L \).
  • Fun fact: this class includes all the complements of undecidable RE languages.

RE 外 (熔岩图最外层) 的语言: 造不出识别器, 也造不出验证器, 即不存在有限长的证书能证明某个串属于它. 一个重要事实: 所有「不可判定的 RE 语言」的补都在 RE 外. 理由 (见 CS103 21 的补充): 若 L 和它的补都可识别, L 就可判定; 所以 L ∈ RE − R 时, 补不可能在 RE 中.

Slide 21–23

What makes a language non-RE? In our discussion of verifiers, we've brushed up against one reason a language could be unverifiable: it's inherently hard to provide a piece of evidence to prove a negative.

The complements of undecidable RE languages are always outside of RE.

\( A_{TM} \) — Certificate (hint): n, the number of steps of M(w) execution until we will observe M halt and accept or reject w.

\( \overline{\mathrm{HALT}} \) — Certificate (hint): n, the number of steps of M(w) execution until we observe…what? Not halting?? A step count doesn't work. :-(

为什么会在 RE 外? 一个原因: 很难为「否定」提供证据. \( A_{TM} \) 的证书是「M 在第 n 步接受」, 可以检查. 但 HALT 的补 (「M 在 w 上不停机」) 用什么当证书? 「到第 n 步还没停」证明不了永远不停. 步数行不通, 所以 HALT 的补在 RE 外.

Slide 24–26

The Class Unrecognizable (Lava Diagram Category “All Languages”)

  • Example languages:
    • The complements of undecidable RE languages.
    • \( \mathrm{EQ}_{TM} \) = {⟨M1, M2⟩ | where ℒ(M1) = ℒ(M2) }
    • L = {⟨M⟩ | M loops on at least five strings }
  • For each of the above, ask yourself: what certificate/hint could possibly help?
    • Of course, not being able to think of one isn't a proof that these are unrecognizable, but it's a good intuition for judging.

PollEv: A very similar language is: L' = {⟨M⟩ | M accepts at least five strings }. How would you categorize it on the Lava Diagram?

  • Note that we can write a verifier for the language {⟨M⟩ | M accepts at least five strings }, using the certificate ⟨w1,n1,w2,n2,w3,n3,w4,n4,w5,n5⟩, which is a mouthful, but still finite.

RE 外语言的例子: 不可判定 RE 语言的补; \( \mathrm{EQ}_{TM} \) (两台机器的语言是否相同); 「M 在至少五个串上死循环」. 试着想想什么证书能证明它们, 想不出来不是证明, 但是很好的直觉: 语言相等要比较无穷多个串, 死循环要证明「永远不停」.

投票题: 相似的语言 L' = 「M 至少接受五个串」在 RE 中但不在 R 中. 在 RE 中: 证书是五个不同的串和各自被接受的步数, 有限长, 验证器逐个模拟即可. 不在 R 中: 由 Rice 定理, 它是关于语言的非平凡性质. 对比可见: 「接受」可以被证明, 「死循环」不能.

Slide 27–30

The Language \( L_D \)

  • \( L_D \) = {⟨M⟩ | M does not accept ⟨M⟩ } = {⟨M⟩ | ⟨M⟩ ∈ ℒ(M)}
  • This is a classic example of an unrecognizable language.
  • To see why we can't build a TM for this language, ask yourself:
    • If you did build a TM for \( L_D \), called \( M_{L_D} \), would \( M_{L_D} \) accept ⟨\( M_{L_D} \)⟩?

Happy Story Time

In a certain isolated town, every house has a lawn and the city requires them all to be mowed. The town has only one gardener, who is also a resident of the town, and this gardener mows the lawns of residents iff they do not mow their own lawn.

True or false: The gardener mows their own lawn.

The existence of a TM for \( L_D \) creates a contradiction, so that's our proof that a TM for \( L_D \) cannot exist.

经典的不可识别语言 \( L_D \) (D 指 diagonal 对角): 所有不接受自己的代码的图灵机. 假设有识别器 \( M_{L_D} \), 问它接受自己的代码吗?

  • 若接受, 由识别器定义 ⟨\( M_{L_D} \)⟩ ∈ \( L_D \), 即它不接受自己, 矛盾.
  • 若不接受, 则 ⟨\( M_{L_D} \)⟩ 满足 \( L_D \) 的条件, 属于 \( L_D \), 识别器应该接受它, 又矛盾.

所以 \( L_D \) 没有识别器, 不在 RE 中. 这和园丁的故事一样: 园丁给所有「不自己修草坪」的居民修草坪, 他自己修不修? 修, 就违反规则; 不修, 他就得修. 问题无法回答, 结论是这样的园丁不可能存在. 这又是康托尔对角线论证的结构: \( L_D \) 恰好是 CS103 08 里的对角集合 D.

幻灯片错误: \( L_D \) 的第二种写法 “{⟨M⟩ | ⟨M⟩ ∈ ℒ(M)}” 应为 “{⟨M⟩ | ⟨M⟩ ∉ ℒ(M)}”: 「M 不接受 ⟨M⟩」就是「⟨M⟩ 不在 M 的语言里」. 原文写成 ∈ 恰好是相反的语言 (接受自己代码的机器), 那个语言是可识别的. Slide 27 和 Slide 30 两处都有这个错.
