  • When there's an edge between two nodes, we say they are adjacent.
  • If there's a path between two nodes, we say they are reachable from one another.

(图: 一条链 a – b – c – d – e.)

填空复习: 有边相连叫 adjacent 相邻, 有路径相连叫 reachable 可达. 在链 a–b–c–d–e 里, a 和 b 相邻, a 和 e 不相邻但可达. 相邻一定可达, 反之不然.

Slide 8–11

Theorem (The Pigeonhole Principle): If m objects are distributed into n bins and m>n, then at least one bin will contain at least two objects.

A More General Version

  • The generalized pigeonhole principle says that if you distribute m objects into n bins, then
    • some bin will have at least ⌈m/n⌉ objects in it, and
    • some bin will have at most ⌊m/n⌋ objects in it.

m=11, n=5, ⌈m/n⌉=3, ⌊m/n⌋=2

鸽巢原理及其推广, 详见 CS103 10 · 图论下. 11 个物品放进 5 个盒子, 平均 2.2 个, 一定有盒子至少 3 个, 也一定有盒子至多 2 个.

原始版本是推广版的特例: m > n 时 m/n > 1, 上取整至少是 2, 所以某个盒子至少两个物品. 推广版两句话各自的反证都是同一个套路: 假设所有盒子都低于 (或都高于) 某个值, 加起来就和总数 m 矛盾.

应用: 朋友与陌生人
An Application: Friends and Strangers

Slide 14–26

Friends and Strangers

  • Suppose you have a party of six people. Each pair of people are either friends (they know each other) or strangers (they do not).
  • Theorem (“Theorem on Friends and Strangers”): Any such 6-person party must have a group of three mutual friends (three people who all know one another) or three mutual strangers (three people, none of whom know any of the others).

This graph is called K6​, the complete graph of order 6. More generally, the graph Kn​ consists of n mutually adjacent nodes.

This is a monochrome (one-color) copy of K3​.

Friends and Strangers Restated

  • From a graph-theoretic perspective, the Theorem on Friends and Strangers can be restated as follows:

    Theorem: Color every edge of K6​ either red or blue. The resulting graph always contains a monochrome copy of K3​.

  • How can we prove this result?

复习: 六人派对上每两人要么相识要么陌生, 定理断言一定有三人互相认识或三人互不认识. 同上一讲, 这次用标准术语: n 个节点两两相邻的图叫 complete graph 完全图 Kn​ (上一讲叫 n 团). K3​ 就是三角形. monochrome 单色 K3​ 是三条边同色的三角形. 定理: 把 K6​ 的每条边涂成红或蓝, 一定有单色三角形.

Slide 30–37

Nodes adjacent to this one: 5. Colors available: 2.

Generalized Pigeonhole Principle says that some color must have at least X edges of this node that are that color.

Warmup: what are the bins, what are the birds, in this scenario?

Question: What is X?

Generalized Pigeonhole Principle: If we pick any node in the graph, that node will have at least ⌈5/2⌉=3 edges of the same color incident to it.

Question: if we reason through this with blue, will our analysis be any different than if we reasoned through with red?

Let's just say the 3 that are the same are blue (as opposed to red).

Magic words in proof: “without loss of generality” — Means the argument would be the same in two different cases, so you just pick one and focus on that.

热身问答:

  • 谁是鸽子、谁是巢? 选定一个节点, 它的 5 条边是「鸽子」, 2 种颜色是「巢」.
  • X 是多少? ⌈5/2⌉=3: 至少 3 条边同色.
  • 按蓝色推理和按红色推理有区别吗? 没有, 把两种颜色互换, 论证一字不变.

所以证明里可以写「without loss of generality 不失一般性, 设这三条边是蓝色」, 只写一种情况. 用这句话的前提是: 另一种情况真的只是换个名字, 论证完全对称.

Slide 45

Theorem: Color each edge of K6​ red or blue. The resulting graph contains a monochrome copy of K3​.

Proof: We need to show that the colored K6​ contains a red copy of K3​ or a blue copy of K3​.

Pick some node x from K6​. It is incident to five edges and there are two possible colors for those edges. Therefore, by the generalized pigeonhole principle, at least ⌈5/2⌉=3 of those edges must be the same color. Without loss of generality, assume those edges are blue.

Let r, s, and t be three of the nodes adjacent to node x along a blue edge. If any of the edges {r,s}, {r,t}, or {s,t} are blue, then one of those edges plus the two edges connecting back to node x form a blue K3​. Otherwise, all three of those edges are red, and they form a red K3​. Overall, this gives a red K3​ or a blue K3​, as required. ■

证明逐步看:

  1. 要证的是「有红 K3​ 或有蓝 K3​」, 一个「或」命题; 证明直接构造出其中之一.
  2. 任取节点 x. 它和其余 5 个节点各有一条边, 5 条边 2 种颜色, 由推广的鸽巢原理至少 3 条同色. 不失一般性设为蓝色.
  3. 这 3 条蓝边的另一端记为 r、s、t. 它们之间还有 3 条边 {r, s}、{r, t}、{s, t}.
  4. 情况一: 这 3 条里有一条蓝的, 比如 {r, s}, 那么 x–r、x–s、r–s 三条全蓝, 是蓝 K3​.
  5. 情况二: 这 3 条全是红的, 那么 r、s、t 本身组成红 K3​.

两种情况必居其一 (这是假设里的「或」: 每条边非红即蓝), 所以一定有单色 K3​. 和上一讲相比, 这里只是把「三角形」统一写成 K3​, 详见 CS103 10 · 朋友与陌生人.

Slide 46

Ramsey Theory

  • This proof is a special case of a broader family of results called Ramsey theory.
  • Theorem (Ramsey): For any natural number s, there is a number R(s) such that
    • for all n<R(s), there's a way to color the edges of Kn​ red and blue so there are no monochrome copies of Ks​, and
    • for all n≥R(s), every way of coloring the edges of Kn​ red and blue always has a monochrome copy of Ks​.
  • Take Math 108 (combinatorics) to learn more!
  • A more philosophical (and less literal) take on this theorem: true disorder is impossible at a large scale, since no matter how you organize things, you're guaranteed to find some interesting substructure.

用 Kn​ 的语言重述拉姆齐定理: 对每个 s 有一个阈值 R(s), 比它小的完全图存在避开单色 Ks​ 的涂法, 达到或超过它的完全图怎么涂都有单色 Ks​. 六人派对说明 R(3) ≤ 6; 五边形涂法 (相邻红、不相邻蓝) 说明 K5​ 可以避开, 所以 R(3) = 6.

R(s) 增长得极快, 目前只知道 R(4) = 18, R(5) 只知道在 43 到 46 之间. 拉姆齐理论的哲学含义是: 规模足够大时, 无论怎样安排, 总会冒出有规律的子结构, 完全的无序不可能存在.

Slide 47

The Game of Sim

  • Here's a game you can play with two players.
    • One players plays as red, the other as blue.
    • Begin with six disconnected points.
    • Each turn, a player draws a line of their color.
    • The first to make a triangle of their color loses.
  • The theorem we just proved means the game can't end in a draw: someone must win and someone must lose.
  • The strategy is more subtle than it looks. Try playing this with a friend to see why!

Sim 游戏: 两人分别用红笔和蓝笔, 在 6 个点之间轮流画线, 先画出自己颜色三角形的人输. 由定理, 15 条线全画完时一定有单色三角形, 所以游戏不可能平局. 已知后手有必胜策略, 但策略很复杂.

原文笔误: “One players plays as red” 应为 “One player plays as red”.

Slide 48–49

A Little Math Puzzle

“In a group of n>0 people …

  • 90% of those people enjoyed CODA,
  • 80% of those people enjoyed Nomadland,
  • 70% of those people enjoyed Parasite, and
  • 60% of those people enjoyed Knives Out.

No one enjoyed all four movies. How many people enjoyed at least one of CODA and Parasite?”

(Adapted from here.)

谜题: n 个人里, 90% 喜欢 CODA (健听女孩), 80% 喜欢 Nomadland (无依之地), 70% 喜欢 Parasite (寄生虫), 60% 喜欢 Knives Out (利刃出鞘). 没有人四部都喜欢. 问: 有多少人至少喜欢 CODA 和 Parasite 中的一部? 答案是所有人, 下面用一个新的鸽巢型定理证明.

Slide 51–54

If m objects are distributed into n boxes, then [condition] holds.

If m objects are distributed into n boxes, then some box is loaded to at least the average m/n, and some box is loaded to at most the average m/n.

Theorem: If m objects are distributed into n bins, then there is a bin containing more than m/n objects if and only if there is a bin containing fewer than m/n objects.

鸽巢型结论都有「m 个物品放进 n 个盒子, 则某条件成立」的形式. 不取整的说法: 总有盒子不少于平均值 m/n, 也总有盒子不多于平均值.

新定理: 有盒子多于平均值, 当且仅当有盒子少于平均值. 直觉: 有人高于平均, 就一定有人低于平均把它拉回来; 反过来也一样. 推论: 如果没有盒子高于平均, 就也没有盒子低于平均, 即每个盒子恰好等于平均值.

Slide 55–58

Lemma: If m objects are distributed into n bins and there are no bins containing more than m/n objects, then there are no bins containing fewer than m/n objects.

Proof: Assume for the sake of contradiction that m objects are distributed into n bins such that no bin contains more than m/n objects, yet some bin has fewer than m/n objects.

For simplicity, denote by xi​ the number of objects in bin i. Without loss of generality, assume that bin 1 has fewer than m/n objects, meaning that x1​<m/n. Adding up the number of objects in each bin tells us that

m​amp;=x1​+x2​+x3​+⋯+xn​amp;<m/n+x2​+x3​+⋯+xn​amp;≤m/n+m/n+m/n+⋯+m/n.​

This third step follows because each remaining bin has at most m/n objects. Grouping the n copies of the m/n term here tells us that

m​amp;<m/n+m/n+m/n+⋯+m/namp;=m.​

But this means m<m, which is impossible. We've reached a contradiction, so our assumption was wrong, so if m objects are distributed into n bins and no bin has more than m/n objects, no bin has fewer than m/n objects either. ■

引理是定理的一个方向的逆否形式 (「有盒子少于平均 → 有盒子多于平均」的逆否). 反证:

  1. 假设没有盒子多于 m/n, 但有盒子少于 m/n. 不失一般性设是第 1 个盒子 (盒子编号可以随意重排).
  2. 总数 m 拆成各盒之和. 第 1 项严格小于 m/n, 其余每项不超过 m/n.
  3. 于是 m 严格小于 n 个 m/n 之和, 即 m < m, 矛盾.

注意第二步是 <, 第三步是 ≤: 链条里只要有一个严格不等号, 整体就是严格的.

Slide 69–72

(题目同上.)

Insight 1: Model movie preferences as balls (movies) in bins (people).
Insight 2: There are n total bins, one for each person.
Insight 3: There are 3n balls being distributed into n bins. .9n+.8n+.7n+.6n=3n
Insight 4: The average number of balls in each bin is 3.
Insight 5: No one enjoyed more than three movies…
Insight 6: … so no one enjoyed fewer than three movies…
Insight 7: … so everyone enjoyed exactly three movies.
Insight 8: You have to enjoy at least one of these movies to enjoy three of the four movies.

Conclusion: Everyone liked at least one of these two movies!

解题思路一步一步:

  1. 每个人是一个盒子 (n 个), 每次「某人喜欢某部电影」是一个球.
  2. 球的总数是 0.9n + 0.8n + 0.7n + 0.6n = 3n, 平均每个盒子 3 个.
  3. 没人四部都喜欢, 所以没有盒子超过 3 个 (超过平均).
  4. 由引理, 也没有盒子少于 3 个, 所以每人恰好喜欢 3 部.
  5. 如果有人 CODA 和 Parasite 都不喜欢, 他最多喜欢剩下 2 部, 与「恰好 3 部」矛盾.

所以全部 n 个人都至少喜欢 CODA 和 Parasite 之一. 关键是把看似和鸽巢无关的问题建模成「球放进盒子」.

Slide 73

Theorem: In the scenario described here, all n people enjoyed at least one of CODA and Parasite.

Proof: Suppose there is a group of n people meeting these criteria. We can model this problem by representing each person as a bin and each time a person enjoys a movie as a ball. The number of balls is

.9n+.8n+.7n+.6n=3n,

and since there are n people, there are n bins. Since no person liked all four movies, no bin contains more than 3=3n/n balls, so by our earlier theorem we see that no bin contains fewer than three balls. Therefore, each bin contains exactly three balls.

Now suppose for the sake of contradiction that someone didn't enjoy CODA and didn't enjoy Parasite. This means they could enjoy at most two of the four movies, contradicting that each person enjoys exactly three.

We've reached a contradiction, so our assumption was wrong and each person enjoyed at least one of CODA and Parasite. ■

正式证明把上面的思路写出来: 建模、算平均、用引理得出每人恰好 3 部, 最后用反证排除「两部都不喜欢」. 证明里用到的「earlier theorem」就是上面的引理.

延伸: 用同样方法还能推出更多, 例如每个人都至少喜欢 CODA 和 Knives Out 之一 (其他任意两部的组合也一样), 因为只缺一部.

Slide 82

Going Further

  • The pigeonhole principle can be used to prove a ton of amazing theorems. Here's a sampler:
    • There is always a way to fairly split rent among multiple people, even if different people want different rooms. (Sperner's lemma)
    • You and a friend can climb any mountain from two different starting points so that the two of you maintain the same altitude at each point in time. (Mountain-climbing theorem)
    • If you model coffee in a cup as a collection of infinitely many points and then stir the coffee, some point is always where it initially started. (Brower's fixed-point theorem)
    • A complex process that doesn't parallelize well must contain a large serial subprocess. (Mirksy's theorem)
    • Any positive integer n has a nonzero multiple that can be written purely using the digits 1 and 0. (Doesn't have a name, but still cool!)

鸽巢原理 (及其连续或组合的推广) 能证明许多出人意料的结论, 课件列了五个:

  • Sperner 引理: 几个人合租、对房间偏好不同时, 总存在一种房租分法, 让每人都分到自己最想要的房间而无人嫉妒.
  • 登山定理: 两人从山两侧的不同起点出发, 可以协调步伐, 使任何时刻海拔都相同.
  • 布劳威尔不动点定理: 把杯中咖啡看作无穷多个点, 连续地搅动之后, 总有一个点回到原来的位置.
  • Mirsky 定理: 一个难以并行的复杂过程, 一定包含一个很长的必须依次执行的子过程.
  • 每个正整数都有一个只由数字 0 和 1 写成的非零倍数: 看 1, 11, 111, … 共 n + 1 个数除以 n 的余数, 必有两个相同, 二者之差就是这样的倍数.

原文笔误: 与上一讲相同, Brower 应为 Brouwer, Mirksy 应为 Mirsky.
