Slide 4–8

R and RE

  • A language \( L \) is recognizable if there is a TM \( M \) with the following property: \( \forall w \in \Sigma^*.\ (M \text{ accepts } w \leftrightarrow w \in L) \).
    • That is, for any string \( w \): If \( w \in L \), then \( M \) accepts \( w \). If \( w \notin L \), them \( M \) does not accept \( w \). – It might reject \( w \), or it might loop on \( w \).
  • This is a “weak” notion of solving a problem.
  • The class RE consists of all the recognizable languages.
  • A language \( L \) is decidable if there is a TM \( M \) with the following properties: \( M \) halts on all inputs. \( \forall w \in \Sigma^*.\ (M \text{ accepts } w \leftrightarrow w \in L) \). That is: If \( w \in L \), then \( M \) accepts \( w \). If \( w \notin L \), then \( M \) rejects \( w \).
  • This is a “strong” notion of solving a problem.
  • The class R consists of all the decidable languages.

The Universal TM

  • The universal Turing machine, denoted \( U_{TM} \), is a TM with the following behavior: when run on a string ⟨M, w⟩, where M is a TM and w is a string, \( U_{TM} \) will accept / reject / loop on ⟨M, w⟩ exactly as M does on w.
  • \( A_{TM} \) is the language recognized by the universal TM. This is the language

    \( A_{TM} = \{ \langle M, w\rangle \mid M \text{ is a TM and } M \text{ accepts } w \} \)

  • \( U_{TM} \) is a ??? for \( A_{TM} \). — \( U_{TM} \) is a recognizer for \( A_{TM} \).

Self-Referential Programs: Theorem: It is possible to construct TMs that perform arbitrary computations on their own source code.

复习: 可识别 (在 L 外可以拒绝或死循环) 与可判定 (总停机, L 外一定拒绝); RE 和 R; 通用图灵机 \( U_{TM} \) 是 \( A_{TM} \) 的识别器; 程序可以拿到自己的源代码. 详见 CS103 21 和 CS103 22.

原文笔误: “If w ∉ L, them M does not accept w” 中 them 应为 then.

自我否定的对象
Part One: Self-Defeating Objects

Slide 13–16

A self-defeating object is an object whose essential properties ensure it doesn't exist.

Question: Why is there no largest integer? Answer: Because if \( n \) is the largest integer, what happens when we look at \( n+1 \)?

Self-Defeating Objects

Theorem: There is no largest integer.

Proof sketch: Suppose for the sake of contradiction that there is a largest integer. Call that integer \( n \). Consider the integer \( n+1 \). Notice that \( n \lt n+1 \). But then \( n \) isn't the largest integer. Contradiction! ■-ish

We're using \( n \) to construct something that undermines \( n \), hence the term “self-defeating.”

self-defeating object 自我否定的对象: 它的本质属性保证了它不可能存在. 经典例子是「最大的整数」: 假设 n 是最大的整数, 用它造出 n + 1, 比 n 还大, 矛盾. 关键在于用 n 本身构造出推翻 n 的东西, 所以叫「自我否定」. 第 2 讲证明「不存在最大的集合」(用幂集) 也是这个套路.

Slide 17–19

An Important Detail

Claim: There is a largest integer.

Proof: Assume \( x \) is the largest integer. Notice that \( x \gt x - 1 \). So there's no contradiction. ■-ish

Careful – we're assuming what we're trying to prove!

How do we know there's no contradiction? We just checked one case.

Self-Defeating Objects

  • If you can show \( x \text{ exists} \to \bot \) then you know that \( x \) doesn't exist. (A proof by contradiction.)
  • If you can show \( x \text{ exists} \to \top \) you cannot conclude that \( x \) exists. (Not a valid proof technique.)

一个错误的「证明」: 「存在最大整数」, 假设 x 最大, 检查 x > x − 1, 没有矛盾, 所以成立? 错在两处: 它假设了要证的东西; 「没发现矛盾」只是检查了一种情况, 不等于没有矛盾.

规则: 若「x 存在 → 矛盾 (⊥)」, 可以断定 x 不存在, 这是反证法. 若只得到「x 存在 → 某个真命题 (⊤)」, 不能断定 x 存在, 因为蕴含的前件可能是假的 (空真). 用逻辑说, \( P \to \top \) 永远为真, 什么也说明不了.

算命先生
Part Two: The Fortune Teller

Slide 21–23

The Fortune Teller

  • A fortune teller appears who claims they can see into the future.
  • For a nominal fee, the fortune teller will tell you anything you want to know about the future.
  • Of course, the fortune teller is a lying liar who lies. No one can see the future!
  • The fortune teller makes a living taking money from unsuspecting townsfolk.
  • Someone needs to put an end to this!
  • One day, a trickster arrives.
  • The trickster wants to expose that the fortune teller is a fraud.
  • The trickster says the following:

    “I have a yes/no question about the future. But before I ask my question, let's talk payment. If you answer ‘yes,’ then I'll pay you $42. If you answer ‘no,’ then I'll pay you $137.”

  • The fortune teller thinks for a moment, then agrees.
  • The trickster then asks this question: “Am I going to pay you $137?”
  • The fortune teller is trapped!

故事: 一个算命先生声称能预知未来, 收费回答任何关于未来的问题 (当然是骗人的). 一个捣蛋鬼想揭穿他: 先约定付款方式, 回答「是」付 42 美元, 回答「否」付 137 美元; 然后问: 「我会付给你 137 美元吗?」

算命先生无论怎么答都错: 答「是」, 按约定只付 42, 那么「付 137」没发生, 答错; 答「否」, 按约定付 137, 「付 137」发生了, 又答错.

Slide 24–25

The payment scheme the fortune teller agreed to means

Fortune Teller Says Yes ↔ Trickster Pays $42.

The trickster's question to the fortune teller means

Fortune Teller Says Yes ↔ Trickster Pays $137.

Putting this together, we get

Trickster Pays $137 ↔ Trickster Pays $42.

This is impossible!

  • The fortune teller is a self-defeating object.
  • The trickster's strategy is to couple the fortune teller's behavior to what the future holds.
  • The trickster's behavior is chosen in advance to make the fortune teller's answer wrong.
  • Therefore, the fortune teller can't answer all questions about all people in the future.

用逻辑写出来: 约定说「答是 ↔ 付 42」; 算命先生若总说真话, 问题要求「答是 ↔ 付 137」. 合起来「付 137 ↔ 付 42」, 但这两件事恰好只发生一件, 矛盾.

所以「能正确回答所有关于未来问题的算命先生」是自我否定的对象. 捣蛋鬼的策略: 让未来依赖于算命先生的回答, 并事先安排成与回答相反. 下面把它变成程序.

同类的悖论还有很多: 说谎者悖论「这句话是假的」; 理发师悖论「村里的理发师只给所有不给自己理发的人理发, 那他给不给自己理发?」; 罗素悖论「所有不包含自身的集合组成的集合, 包含它自己吗?」. 它们的共同结构是: 一个对象对「自己」的某种判断取反. 在集合论里, 罗素悖论说明「所有集合的集合」这种东西不能随便存在; 在计算理论里, 同样的结构说明「能预言一切程序行为的程序」不能存在.

为什么程序会死循环
Part Three: Why Do Programs Loop?

Slide 27–28

Thoughts on Loops

  • In practice, the programs we write sometimes go into infinite loops.
  • In Theoryland, Turing machines are allowed to loop. This happens if they don't accept and don't reject.
  • Question: Why are infinite loops possible?
  • Or rather: are infinite loops an inherent part of computation, or are they some weird sort of “accident” in how we program computers?

[Major] Theorem: The language \( A_{TM} \) is recognizable, but undecidable.

  • There's a recognizer for \( A_{TM} \) (specifically, the universal Turing machine \( U_{TM} \)).
  • It is impossible to build a decider for this language.
  • Stated differently, there's a program we can write (a universal TM) that has to loop infinitely on some inputs.
  • Goal: Prove this theorem, and explore its theoretical and philosophical implications.

实际编程中程序有时会死循环, 理论上图灵机也允许死循环. 问题: 死循环是计算固有的, 还是编程方式的「意外」? 能不能设计一种总会停机、又和图灵机一样强的模型?

重大定理: \( A_{TM} \) 可识别 (\( U_{TM} \) 就是识别器), 但不可判定. 换句话说, 通用图灵机这个程序不可避免地要在某些输入上死循环, 无法改进成总会停机的版本. 死循环是计算的本质, 不是意外.

Slide 29–31

\( A_{TM} \) Revisited

  • As a refresher, the language \( A_{TM} \) is \( A_{TM} = \{ \langle M, w\rangle \mid M \text{ is a TM and } M \text{ accepts } w \} \).
  • The universal TM \( U_{TM} \) has the following behavior when given as input a TM M and a string w: If M accepts w, then \( U_{TM} \) accepts ⟨M, w⟩. If M rejects w, then \( U_{TM} \) rejects ⟨M, w⟩. If M loops on w, then \( U_{TM} \) loops on ⟨M, w⟩.
  • \( U_{TM} \) is a recognizer for \( A_{TM} \), but because of that last case it's not a decider for \( A_{TM} \).

Given a TM M and a string w, a decider D for \( A_{TM} \) would need to have this behavior: If M accepts w, then D accepts ⟨M, w⟩. If M rejects w, then D rejects ⟨M, w⟩. If M loops on w, then D rejects ⟨M, w⟩.

This is basically the same set of requirements as \( U_{TM} \), except for what happens if M loops on w. Our goal is to prove that there is no way to build a program that meets these requirements.

We can envision a decider for \( A_{TM} \) as a function bool willAccept(string fn, string input) that takes as input the source code of a function (fn) and a string representing an input to that function (input). It then does the following:

  • If fn(input) returns true, willAccept(fn, input) returns true.
  • If fn(input) returns false, willAccept(fn, input) returns false.
  • If fn(input) loops, then willAccept(fn, input) returns false.

We're going to show it's impossible to write a function that actually does this. But for now, let's just explore what such a decider would do.

\( U_{TM} \) 不是判定器, 原因只在第三种情况: M 在 w 上死循环时它也死循环. 判定器 D 要求在这种情况下拒绝. 课堂投票的两个问号都是「rejects」.

用程序语言表述: 假想一个函数 willAccept(fn, input), 输入一个函数的源代码和一个输入, fn(input) 返回 true 时它返回 true, 返回 false 或死循环时它返回 false, 而且它自己总会停. 目标是证明这样的函数写不出来.

For each of these instances, what does willAccept(function, input) return?

三个例子:

  • g 永远循环 (不断把输入翻倍), 所以 willAccept 返回 false (判定器要在这里停下并说 false, 这正是 \( U_{TM} \) 做不到的).
  • h 对输入长度 n = \( 10^{137} \) 跑冰雹序列, 终止就返回 true. 用 Python 实际计算, 这个序列 2208 步后到达 1, 所以 willAccept 返回 true. 但 willAccept 不能靠「运行一下」来判断, 一般情形下它必须事先知道序列是否终止.
Slide 33–35

Deciding \( A_{TM} \)

Surprising fact: until 2019, no one knew whether there were integers \( x \), \( y \), and \( z \) where \( x^3 + y^3 + z^3 = 33 \). A heavily optimized computer search found this answer: \( x = 8{,}866{,}128{,}975{,}287{,}528 \), \( y = -8{,}778{,}405{,}442{,}862{,}239 \), \( z = -2{,}736{,}111{,}468{,}807{,}040 \). As of May 2025, no one knows whether there are integers \( x \), \( y \), and \( z \) where \( x^3 + y^3 + z^3 = 114 \).

Here's code for a recognizer to see whether such a triple exists:

bool hasTriple(int n) {
    for (int max = 0; ; max++)
        for (int x = -max; x <= max; x++)
            for (int y = -max; y <= max; y++)
                for (int z = -max; z <= max; z++)
                    if (x*x*x + y*y*y + z*z*z == n)
                        return true;
}

Imagine calling willAccept(/* hasTriple code */, 114). If such a triple exists, willAccept returns true. If no such triple exists, willAccept returns false.

Key Intuition: However willAccept is implemented, it has to be clever enough to resolve open problems in mathematics!

Why is \( A_{TM} \) Hard?

  • Intuition: A decider for \( A_{TM} \) would be able to…
    • … solve the Collatz conjecture for any input. (Write a recognizer that runs the hailstone sequence, then feed it into the decider for \( A_{TM} \).)
    • … determine whether \( x^3 + y^3 + z^3 = n \) has a solution for any \( n \). (Write a recognizer that tries all infinitely many triples of integers, then feed it into the decider for \( A_{TM} \).)
  • In other words, this seemingly simple problem of “is this program going to terminate?” accidentally scoops up a bunch of other seemingly harder problems.

直觉上 \( A_{TM} \) 为什么难: 把上一讲的立方和识别器 hasTriple 交给 willAccept, 问它在 114 上是否返回 true, 就等于问「114 能否写成三个立方之和」, 这是未解决的数学问题. 同样, 把冰雹序列程序交给它, 就能对任何 n 判断考拉兹猜想. 所以 willAccept 必须聪明到能解决这些开放问题.

「这个程序会不会终止」看似简单, 却「顺带」包含了大量困难的数学问题. 这让人怀疑它根本不可能实现, 下面给出证明.

注意论证的方向: 这只是「直觉上难」, 不是证明. 一个问题能推出难题的答案, 说明它至少和那些难题一样难, 但那些难题本身也许有一天会被解决 (33 的立方和就在 2019 年被解决了). 真正的不可判定性需要下面的自指论证.

综合起来: A_TM 不可判定
Part Four: Putting It All Together

Slide 37–41

To Recap

  • We're assuming that, somehow, someone wrote a function bool willAccept(string function, string input); that takes the code of a function and an input to that function, then
    • returns true if function(input) returns true, and
    • returns false if function(input) doesn't return true.
  • Goal: Show that this decider is “self-defeating;” its power is so great that it undermines itself.
  • Idea: Convert the fortune teller story into a program.
bool willAccept(string function, string input) {
    // Returns true if function(input) returns true.
    // Returns false otherwise.
}

bool trickster(string input) {
    string me = /* source code of trickster */;
    return !willAccept(me, input);
}

A decider for \( A_{TM} \) has to have this behavior: trickster(input) returns true ⟷ willAccept(me, input) returns true.

Because of how we wrote trickster: willAccept(me, input) returns true ⟷ trickster(input) does not return true.

willAccept — A self-defeating object. — “The largest integer n.” trickster — Using that object against itself. — “The integer n + 1.”

把算命先生的故事写成程序. willAccept 是「算命先生」, 能预言任何程序在任何输入上会不会返回 true. trickster 是「捣蛋鬼」:

  1. 它拿到自己的源代码 me (上一讲的自指能力).
  2. 它问 willAccept: 「我 (trickster) 在这个输入上会返回 true 吗?」
  3. 然后返回相反的答案.

两条等价关系: 由 willAccept 的定义, trickster(input) 返回 true ⟷ willAccept(me, input) 返回 true; 由 trickster 的写法, willAccept(me, input) 返回 true ⟷ trickster(input) 不返回 true. 对应关系: willAccept 是「最大的整数 n」这种自我否定的对象, trickster 是用它构造出的「n + 1」.

Slide 42–53

Theorem: \( A_{TM} \notin \mathbf{R} \).

Proof: By contradiction; assume that \( A_{TM} \in \mathbf{R} \). Then there is a decider D for \( A_{TM} \). We can represent D as a function

bool willAccept(string function, string w);

that takes in the source code of a function function and a string w, then returns true if function(w) returns true and returns false otherwise. Given this, consider this function trickster:

bool trickster(string input) {
    string me = /* source code of trickster */;
    return !willAccept(me, input);
}

Since willAccept decides \( A_{TM} \) and me holds the source of trickster, we know that

willAccept(me, input) returns true if and only if trickster(input) returns true.

Given how trickster is written, we see that

willAccept(me, input) returns true if and only if trickster(input) doesn't return true.

This means that

trickster(input) returns true if and only if trickster(input) doesn't return true.

This is impossible. We've reached a contradiction, so our assumption was wrong and \( A_{TM} \) is undecidable. \( \blacksquare \)

正式证明的结构:

  1. 反设 \( A_{TM} \) 可判定, 则有判定器, 写成函数 willAccept, 它总会返回, 而且答案正确.
  2. 构造 trickster. 注意 trickster 一定会停机: willAccept 总会返回, 取反后返回.
  3. 由判定器的正确性: willAccept(me, input) 为 true 当且仅当 trickster(input) 返回 true.
  4. 由 trickster 的写法: willAccept(me, input) 为 true 当且仅当 trickster(input) 不返回 true.
  5. 两式合并: trickster(input) 返回 true 当且仅当它不返回 true. 矛盾.

所以判定器不存在, \( A_{TM} \) 不可判定. 这和康托尔定理的对角线论证 (CS103 08) 结构相同: 对角集合 D 是「x ∉ f(x)」, trickster 是「对自己的预言取反」.

也可以按情况直接看矛盾. 任取一个输入 x, willAccept(me, x) 总会返回, 只有两种可能:

  • 若它返回 true, 意思是「trickster(x) 会返回 true」. 但 trickster 取反后返回 false. 预言错了.
  • 若它返回 false, 意思是「trickster(x) 不会返回 true」. 但 trickster 取反后返回 true. 又错了.

无论 willAccept 怎样实现, 都会在 trickster 上答错, 所以它不是 \( A_{TM} \) 的判定器. 这就是算命先生被问「我会付你 137 吗」时的处境.

What Does This Mean?

  • In one fell swoop, we've proven that
    • \( A_{TM} \) is undecidable; there is no general algorithm that can determine whether a TM will accept a string.
    • \( \mathbf{R} \ne \mathbf{RE} \), because \( A_{TM} \notin \mathbf{R} \) but \( A_{TM} \in \mathbf{RE} \).
  • What do these three statements really mean? As in, why should you care?

一举证明了两件事: \( A_{TM} \) undecidable 不可判定, 没有通用算法能判断图灵机是否接受给定的串; 以及 R ≠ RE, 因为 \( A_{TM} \) 在 RE 中却不在 R 中. 上一讲的两种图景里, 正确的是 R 严格小于 RE.

Slide 56–60

\( A_{TM} \notin \mathbf{R} \)

  • What exactly does it mean for \( A_{TM} \) to be undecidable?
  • Intuition: The only general way to find out what a program will do is to run it.
  • As you'll see, this means that it's provably impossible for computers to be able to answer most questions about what a program will do.
  • At a more fundamental level, the existence of undecidable problems tells us the following:

    There is a difference between what is true and what we can discover is true.

  • Given a TM M and a string w, one of these two statements is true: M accepts w / M does not accept w
  • But since \( A_{TM} \) is undecidable, there is no algorithm that can always determine which of these statements is true!

\( \mathbf{R} \ne \mathbf{RE} \)

  • Because \( \mathbf{R} \ne \mathbf{RE} \), there is a difference between decidability and recognizability:

    In some sense, it is fundamentally harder to solve a problem than it is to check an answer.

  • There are problems where, when the answer is “yes,” you can confirm it (run a recognizer), but where if you don't have the answer, you can't come up with it in a mechanical way (build a decider).

含义一 (\( A_{TM} \notin \) R): 想知道一个程序会做什么, 一般只能运行它. 下一讲会看到, 关于程序行为的大多数问题都无法由计算机回答.

含义二: 真和能被发现为真之间有差别. 对任何 M 和 w, 「M 接受 w」和「M 不接受 w」恰有一个为真, 但没有算法总能告诉我们是哪一个.

含义三 (R ≠ RE): 某种意义上, 解决问题比验证答案本质上更难. 有些问题, 答案为「是」时能确认, 但不知道答案时无法机械地算出来.

和 \( A_{TM} \) 密切相关的是著名的 halting problem 停机问题: 给定程序和输入, 判断它是否会停机. 用同样的 trickster 思路 (问「我会停机吗」, 会停就故意死循环, 不会停就立刻返回) 可以证明它也不可判定. 下一讲会看到它和更多「实际」问题的联系, 比如自动检查程序有没有死循环或安全漏洞的工具, 都不可能对所有程序都给出正确答案.
