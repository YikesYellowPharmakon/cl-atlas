Recap from Last Time

Slide 3–4

Formal Language Theory

  • An alphabet is a set, usually denoted \( \Sigma \), consisting of elements called characters.
  • A string over \( \Sigma \) is a finite sequence of zero or more characters taken from \( \Sigma \).
  • The empty string has no characters and is denoted \( \varepsilon \).
  • A language over \( \Sigma \) is a set of strings over \( \Sigma \).
  • The language \( \Sigma^* \) is the set of all strings over \( \Sigma \).

DFAs: A DFA is a Deterministic Finite Automaton. DFAs are the simplest type of automaton that we will see in this course.

复习上一讲的术语: 字母表、字符串 (有限长, 可以是空串 \( \varepsilon \))、语言 (字符串的集合)、\( \Sigma^* \) (所有字符串), 以及 DFA. 详见 CS103 14. 这一讲先把 DFA 的定义写成数学形式, 再引入正则语言和非确定型自动机 NFA.

Slide 5–9

DFAs, formally defined

  • A DFA consists of:
    • A set of states \( S \)
    • Exactly one element of the set of states designated as a start state
      • (as a consequence, the set of states must be nonempty)
    • A subset of the states designated as accepting states
    • A set of characters, the alphabet \( \Sigma \)
    • A transition function that maps (state, character) ordered pairs to states \( \delta : (S \times \Sigma) \to S \)

See how DFA formal definition draws on concepts we've learned about so far!

The Cartesian product of two sets \( A \) and \( B \), denoted \( A \times B \), is the set of all ordered pairs drawn from \( A \) and \( B \), as shown in this set builder notation:

\( A \times B = \{ (a, b) \mid a \in A \text{ and } b \in B \} \)

If \( S = \{q_0, q_1, q_2\} \) and \( \Sigma = \{0, 1\} \), what is the domain of the function \( \delta \)?

If \( S = \{q_0, q_1, q_2\} \) and \( \Sigma = \{0, 1\} \), then \( \delta : \{(q_0, 0), (q_0, 1), (q_1, 0), (q_1, 1), (q_2, 0), (q_2, 1)\} \to \{q_0, q_1, q_2\} \).

DFA 的正式定义由五部分组成: 状态集 S; S 中恰好一个起始状态 (所以 S 非空); S 的一个子集作为接受状态集; 字母表 \( \Sigma \); 转移函数 \( \delta : S \times \Sigma \to S \).

这里用到了前几讲的概念: 集合、子集、函数. 新概念是 Cartesian product 笛卡尔积 \( A \times B \): 所有「第一项来自 A、第二项来自 B」的有序对. 课堂提问: 3 个状态、2 个字符时, \( \delta \) 的定义域是 \( S \times \Sigma \), 有 3 × 2 = 6 个有序对. 「每个状态对每个字符恰好一条转移」正是「\( \delta \) 是函数」这一条: 每个输入有定义, 而且输出唯一.

Slide 10

The Language of an Automaton

  • If \( D \) is a DFA that processes strings over \( \Sigma \), the language of \( D \), denoted \( \mathscr{L}(D) \), is the set of all strings \( D \) accepts.
  • Formally: \( \mathscr{L}(D) = \{ w \in \Sigma^* \mid D \text{ accepts } w \} \)

DFA 的语言 \( \mathscr{L}(D) \) 是它接受的所有字符串. 同上一讲.

正则语言
The Regular Languages

Slide 13

A language \( L \) is called a regular language if there exists a DFA \( D \) such that \( \mathscr{L}(D) = L \).

If \( L \) is a language and \( \mathscr{L}(D) = L \), we say that \( D \) recognizes the language \( L \).

若存在一个 DFA 的语言恰好是 L, 就称 L 是 regular language 正则语言, 并说这个 DFA recognizes 识别 L. 上一讲的「以 a 结尾」「含 aa」「b 的个数模 3 余 2」都是正则语言. 注意定义是「存在」: 要证 L 正则, 只需构造一个 DFA; 要证不正则, 则要说明所有 DFA 都不行 (后面会学方法).

Slide 14–17

The Complement of a Language

  • Given a language \( L \subseteq \Sigma^* \), the complement of that language (denoted \( \overline{L} \)) is the set of all strings in \( \Sigma^* \) that aren't in \( L \). (The complement of a language is also a language.)
  • Formally: \( \overline{L} = \Sigma^* - L \)

(图: 在 \( \Sigma^* \) 的方框里画出 L, 方框中 L 以外的部分是 \( \overline{L} \).)

语言 L 的 complement 补 \( \overline{L} \) 是 \( \Sigma^* \) 中不属于 L 的所有字符串, 即差集 \( \Sigma^* - L \). 补也是一个语言. 注意补是相对 \( \Sigma^* \) 取的, 所以必须先说清字母表.

Slide 18–19

Complementing Regular Languages

Closure Properties

  • Theorem: If \( L \) is a regular language, then \( \overline{L} \) is also a regular language.
    • (We haven't formally proved this, but you may assume it's true in this class.)
  • As a result, we say that the regular languages are closed under complementation.

(图: 「All languages」的大圆里有一块「Regular languages」, L 和 \( \overline{L} \) 都在其中.)

读图: 把 DFA 的接受状态和非接受状态对调, 原来接受的串现在拒绝, 原来拒绝的现在接受, 得到的 DFA 识别补语言. 这依赖于 DFA 的确定性: 每个串的运行路线唯一, 停在唯一的状态.

所以正则语言的补还是正则语言, 叫做正则语言对补运算 closed 封闭. 课件说本课不正式证明, 可以直接用.

易错: 这个「对调接受状态」的方法对 NFA 不成立 (下面会看到, NFA 一个串可以有多条路径).

Slide 20–22

Theorem: If \( L \) is a regular language, then \( \overline{L} \) is also a regular language.

Possible theorem: If \( L \) is not a regular language, then \( \overline{L} \) is also not a regular language.

Answer in pollev: Is this true? In discussion: prove or disprove.

Theorem: For all languages \( L \), if \( L \) is not a regular language, then \( \overline{L} \) is also not a regular language.

Proof: Assume for the sake of contradiction that there exists a language \( L \) that is not regular, but its complement \( \overline{L} \) is regular. Since \( \overline{L} \) is regular, the complement of \( \overline{L} \) must also be regular, by the first theorem. But the complement of \( \overline{L} \) is \( L \), which by assumption is not regular, a contradiction. So the assumption must be false and the set of non-regular languages is closed under complement.

课堂问题: 「若 L 不正则, 则 \( \overline{L} \) 也不正则」对吗? 对. 反证:

  1. 要证的是「对所有 L, 若 L 不正则则补不正则」. 它的否定是: 存在 L, L 不正则而 \( \overline{L} \) 正则 (课件追问的正是这个否定).
  2. \( \overline{L} \) 正则, 由上面的定理, 它的补也正则.
  3. 补的补就是 L 自己, 于是 L 正则, 与假设矛盾.

这里用到 \( \overline{\overline{L}} = L \): 取两次补回到原样, 正是对合 (CS103 06) 的又一个例子.

非确定型有限自动机
NFAs

Slide 24–25

Revisiting a Problem

NFAs

  • An NFA is a Nondeterministic Finite Automaton
  • A model of computation is nondeterministic if the computing machine has a finite number of choices available to make at each point, possibly including zero.
  • Represents a fundamental shift in how we'll think about computation.

回到上一讲的「小问题」: 一个状态读同一个字符可以有多条转移. DFA 禁止这种情况; NFA (nondeterministic finite automaton, 非确定型有限自动机) 允许它.

计算模型是 nondeterministic 非确定的, 指机器每一步有有限个选择, 可能是多个, 也可能是零个 (卡住). 这是看待计算方式的根本转变: 机器不再只有一条运行路线.

Slide 26

NFA

  • State structure similar to a DFA
  • Different transition function:
    • DFA: \( \delta : (S \times \Sigma) \to S \)
      • always go to exactly one state
    • NFA: \( \delta : (\wp(S) \times \Sigma) \to \wp(S) \)
      • could go to one, many, or none!
  • Different accept condition:
    • Accepts if there exists a series of choices that ends in an accepting state.

If \( S = \{q_0, q_1\} \) and \( \Sigma = \{0, 1\} \), what is the co-domain of the function \( \delta \) for NFA?

NFA 和 DFA 的两个区别:

  • 转移函数: DFA 每步恰好到一个状态; NFA 每步可以到零个、一个或多个状态, 所以输出是状态的集合, 即 \( \wp(S) \) 的元素.
  • 接受条件: 只要存在一种选择序列最终停在接受状态, 就接受.

课堂提问: S 有两个状态时, 陪域是 \( \wp(S) = \{ \varnothing, \{q_0\}, \{q_1\}, \{q_0, q_1\} \} \), 共 4 个元素.

课件把 NFA 的转移函数写成输入「状态集合」: \( \delta : \wp(S) \times \Sigma \to \wp(S) \), 这对应下文「同时处在多个状态」的并行视角. 教科书 (如 Sipser) 的标准定义是输入单个状态: \( \delta : S \times \Sigma_\varepsilon \to \wp(S) \), 其中 \( \Sigma_\varepsilon = \Sigma \cup \{\varepsilon\} \) 把下文的 ε-转移也包括进来. 从单个状态的版本可以得到集合版本: 一个集合的下一步, 就是其中每个状态下一步的并集.

Slide 27–55

A Simple NFA

\( q_0 \) has two transitions defined on 1!

(动画: 在输入 0 1 0 1 1 上逐步运行, 每当 q₀ 读到 1 就分出两条路径.)

运行这个 NFA (同上图): q₀ 读 0 只能自环, 读 1 可以自环也可以去 q₁. 输入 01011 时, 每读到一个 1 路径就分叉. 有一条路径是: 前三个字符 0、1、0 都在 q₀ 自环, 第四个字符 1 去 q₁, 第五个 1 去接受状态 q₂, 所以接受. 其他路径停在非接受状态也没关系, 只要有一条接受就行.

这个 NFA 的语言是「以 11 结尾的字符串」: 它在最后两个字符处「猜中」去 q₁, q₂.

Slide 56–75

A More Complex NFA

If a NFA needs to make a transition when no transition exists, the automaton dies and that particular path does not accept.

(动画: 在输入 0 1 0 1 1 上运行.) Oh no! There's no transition defined!

NFA 中某个状态可以没有某字符的转移. 如果一条路径需要转移却没有可走的边, 这条路径就 dies 死掉, 不接受. 例如上图里过早走到 q₂ 的路径, 后面还有字符时就死了. 但死一条路径不影响整体: 输入 01011 仍有一条路径恰好在最后一步到达 q₂, 所以接受.

和上一张对比: 这个 NFA 去掉了死状态 q₃, 语言不变, 但图更简洁. NFA 不需要为每个字符都画边.

Hello, NFA!

Tragedy in Paradise

(图: 同一个 NFA, 输入 h i p.)

例子: 只有 h、i 两条边的 NFA. 输入 hi 走到 q₂, 接受. 输入 hip: 读完 hi 已经在接受状态, 但还要读 p, q₂ 没有 p 的转移, 路径死亡, 拒绝. 「中途经过接受状态」不算, 必须读完全部输入时停在接受状态. 这个 NFA 的语言是 {hi}.

ε-转移
ε-Transitions

Slide 90–109

ε-Transitions

  • NFAs have a special type of transition called the ε-transition.
  • An NFA may follow any number of ε-transitions at any time without consuming any input.

(动画: 在输入 b a a b b 上运行.)

Give a sequence of states (like \( q_0, q_1, \dots \)) to follow from here that would process the rest of the string, but not result in accept.

Not at all fun or rewarding exercise: what is the language of this NFA?

NFAs are not required to follow ε-transitions. It's simply another option at the machine's disposal.

ε-transition ε-转移: 标着 \( \varepsilon \) 的边, NFA 可以随时沿它走, 不读入任何字符. 可以连续走任意多条, 也可以不走, 它只是多出来的一个选项.

课堂练习 (原图状态, 当前在 q₃, 还剩 bb 没读): 一个不接受的走法是 q₃ 读 b 到 q₄, 再读 b 到 q₅, 停在非接受的 q₅. 这说明同一输入可能有接受和不接受的路径并存; 只要有一条接受, 整体就接受. 课件把「求这个 NFA 的语言」称为「既无趣也没收获的练习」, 说明 NFA 可以写得很难读.

理解非确定性
Intuiting Nondeterminism

Slide 110–111

Intuiting Nondeterminism

  • Nondeterministic machines are a serious departure from physical computers. How can we build up an intuition for them?
  • There are two particularly useful frameworks for interpreting nondeterminism:
    • Perfect positive guessing
    • Massive parallelism

Perfect Positive Guessing

  • We can view nondeterministic machines as having Magic Superpowers that enable them to guess choices that lead to an accepting state.
    • If there is at least one choice that leads to an accepting state, the machine will guess it.
    • If there are no choices, the machine guesses any one of the wrong guesses.
  • There is no known way to physically model this intuition of nondeterminism – this is quite a departure from reality!

非确定的机器和真实计算机差别很大. 两种理解方式:

第一种, perfect positive guessing 完美的正向猜测: 把 NFA 想成有「超能力」, 每次面临选择时总能猜中通往接受状态的那条路. 只要存在一条接受路径, 它就一定猜中; 如果不存在, 它随便猜错哪条都一样 (反正拒绝). 现实中没有已知的方式造出这样的机器.

Slide 112–122

(动画: 在输入 a b a b a 上, 机器在 q₀ 停留, 直到正确地「猜」出最后三个字符 aba 的开头, 才走 a → q₁, b → q₂, a → q₃.)

例: 识别以 aba 结尾的字符串. 在猜测视角下, NFA 在 q₀ 读前面的 ab, 然后「猜」出剩下的 aba 就是结尾, 依次走到 q₃ 接受. 它不需要记住任何东西, 只需要在正确的时刻离开 q₀.

Slide 123–156

Massive Parallelism

  • An NFA can be thought of as a DFA that can be in many states at once.
  • At each point in time, when the NFA needs to follow a transition, it tries all the options at the same time.

“Massive parallelism” matches how we defined the NFA transition function—we think of “where I am right now” as not just one state, but simultaneously all of some subset of the states. NFA: \( \delta : (\wp(S) \times \Sigma) \to \wp(S) \)

Reminder: NFA: \( \delta : (\wp(S) \times \Sigma) \to \wp(S) \). The output of \( \delta(\{q_0, q_2\}, a) \) is where we go next. What is that output?

The set of states we are in after processing the string contains at least one accepting state, there exists some path that gets us to an accepting state.

If the set of states we are in after processing the string does not contain ANY accepting state, there is no path that gets us to an accepting state, so we reject the string. (not going to go through it, but the string abab is one such string for this NFA)

第二种理解, massive parallelism 大规模并行: NFA 像一个能同时处在多个状态的 DFA. 每读一个字符, 它把所有选项同时试一遍. 「当前位置」不是一个状态, 而是一个状态集合, 这正对应课件写的集合版转移函数.

课堂提问: 当前处在 {q₀, q₂}, 读 a. q₀ 读 a 可以留在 q₀, 也可以去 q₁; q₂ 读 a 去 q₃. 所以下一步是 \( \{q_0, q_1, q_3\} \). q₃ 是接受状态; 如果此时输入恰好读完 (输入 aba), 就接受.

读完整个输入后: 状态集合里有接受状态就接受 (存在一条接受路径); 一个都没有就拒绝. 例如 abab 结尾不是 aba, 最终集合是 {q₀, q₂}, 拒绝.

这个视角也说明 NFA 并不比 DFA 强: 状态集合只有 \( 2^{|S|} \) 种, 可以把每个集合当成一个 DFA 状态. 这就是下一讲的子集构造.

设计 NFA
Designing NFAs

Slide 157

Designing NFAs

  • Embrace the nondeterminism!
  • Good model: Guess-and-check:
    • Is there some fact about the later part of the string that you'd really like to know early on?
      • Have the machine nondeterministically guess that information!
    • Then, have the machine deterministically check that the guess was correct.

设计 NFA 的思路是充分利用非确定性, 典型方法叫 guess-and-check 先猜后验: 如果希望提前知道字符串后面的某个信息 (比如「结尾会是什么」), 就让机器非确定地猜这个信息, 然后确定地验证猜得对不对. 猜错的路径会被验证步骤淘汰, 不影响结果.

Slide 158–167

Guess-and-Check

\( L = \{ w \in \{0, 1\}^* \mid w \text{ ends in 010 or 101} \} \)

Ask yourself these design questions:

  • Would it be really easy to design an NFA to detect the substring 010 at the end, if you knew that's what you were looking for, and when you'd reached the near-end?
  • Would it be really easy to design an NFA to detect the substring 101, if you knew that's what you were looking for, and when you'd reached the near-end?
  • Would it be really convenient if you could just magically guess that?

Detemrinistic machine for “substring 010 at the end” / Deterministic machine for “substring 101 at the end”

“Magic Guessing” nondeterminism knows when we are nearing end of string! … knows whether it will end in 010 or 101!

\( L = L_1 \cup L_2 \) where: \( L_1 = \{ w \in \{0, 1\}^* \mid w \text{ ends in 010} \} \), \( L_2 = \{ w \in \{0, 1\}^* \mid w \text{ ends in 101} \} \)

NFA Design Hack! If you can write the language as the union of two or more very simple languages: (1) make simple DFA/NFAs for those simple languages (2) a single start state dispatches to the simple DFA/NFAs using epsilon transitions

例: 以 010 或 101 结尾. 设计时问自己: 如果已知要找 010 而且已经到了快结尾的地方, 检查容易吗? 容易, 一条三步的链. 101 也一样. 那就让机器猜.

构造 (读图):

  1. 起始状态用两条 ε 边「猜」结尾是 010 还是 101, 分别进入上下两条链.
  2. 每条链的第一个状态用 \( \Sigma \) 自环吃掉前面的字符, 并「猜」何时到了结尾前的三个字符.
  3. 然后确定地验证: 依次读 0、1、0 (或 1、0、1) 到接受状态. 接受状态没有出边, 如果后面还有字符这条路径就死了.

通用技巧: 语言能写成几个简单语言的并集 \( L_1 \cup L_2 \) 时, 分别为每个简单语言做自动机, 再用一个新起始状态通过 ε 边分派到它们.

Slide 168–172

\( L = \{ w \in \{a, b, c\}^* \mid \text{at least one of a, b, or c is not in } w \} \)

Ask yourself these design questions: Would it be really easy to design an NFA to detect the string... ...has no a's in it, if you knew that's what you were looking for? ...has no b's in it, …? ..has no c's in it, …? Would it be really convenient if you could just magically guess which letter is the missing one this time?

\( L_1 = \{ w \in \{a, b, c\}^* \mid \text{a is not in } w \} \)
\( L_2 = \{ w \in \{a, b, c\}^* \mid \text{b is not in } w \} \)
\( L_3 = \{ w \in \{a, b, c\}^* \mid \text{c is not in } w \} \)
\( L = L_1 \cup L_2 \cup L_3 \)

(图: 起始状态用三条 ε 边分别进入三个接受状态, 它们各自对 b, c、a, c、a, b 自环.)

例: 字母 a、b、c 至少有一个没出现. 写成三个语言的并集: 不含 a、不含 b、不含 c. 每个都很简单: 一个接受状态, 对另外两个字母自环. 用 ε 边分派就得到只有 4 个状态的 NFA: 猜哪个字母缺席, 再验证它确实没出现 (出现了就没有边, 路径死亡).

对比原图里同一语言的 DFA: 它必须记住「到目前为止见过哪些字母」, 有 \( 2^3 = 8 \) 种可能 (空集到全部), 所以有 8 个状态, 见齐三个字母后进入死状态. NFA 用猜测省掉了这部分记忆.
