为什么关心正则语言
Why does this matter?

Slide 2

Theorem: The following are all equivalent:

  • \( L \) is a regular language.
  • There is a DFA \( D \) such that \( \mathscr{L}(D) = L \).
  • There is an NFA \( N \) such that \( \mathscr{L}(N) = L \).
  • There is a regular expression \( R \) such that \( \mathscr{L}(R) = L \).

上一讲的结论: 正则语言、DFA、NFA、正则表达式四者等价. 这一讲回答反面的问题: 哪些语言不是正则的, 以及怎样证明. 重点是可区分性的定义和 Myhill-Nerode 定理的用法.

Slide 8–9

Computers as Finite Automata

  • My computer has 12GB of RAM and about 150GB of hard disk space.
  • That's a total of 162GB of memory, which is 1,391,569,403,904 bits.
  • There are “only” \( 2^{1{,}391{,}569{,}403{,}904} \) possible configurations of the memory in my computer.
  • You could in principle build a DFA representing my computer, where there's one symbol per type of input the computer can receive.

A Powerful Intuition

  • Regular languages correspond to problems that can be solved with finite memory.
    • At each point in time, we only need to store one of finitely many pieces of information.
  • Nonregular languages correspond to problems that cannot be solved with finite memory.
  • Since every computer ever built has finite memory, in a sense, nonregular languages correspond to problems that cannot be solved by physical computers!

任何真实计算机的内存都是有限的. 例子: 12 GB 内存加 150 GB 硬盘共 162 GB, 按 \( 1\text{ GB} = 2^{30} \) 字节算是 1 391 569 403 904 位, 内存所有可能的内容「只有」\( 2^{1391569403904} \) 种. 原则上可以造一个状态这么多的 DFA 来模拟这台电脑.

由此得到直觉: 正则语言 = 用有限内存能解决的问题 (任何时刻只需记住有限种信息之一). 非正则语言需要无限内存. 从这个意义上说, 非正则语言是任何物理计算机都无法解决的问题 (虽然实际中内存大到可以当作无限来用).

核算: \( 162 \times 2^{30} \times 8 = 1\,391\,569\,403\,904 \), 课件的数字是按 GiB (二进制千兆) 计算的, 正确.

寻找非正则语言
Finding Nonregular Languages

Slide 11–12
  • To prove that a language is regular, we can just find a DFA, NFA, or regex for it.
  • To prove that a language is not regular, we need to prove that there is no possible DFA for it. (or no possible NFA, or regex---but since these are equivalent we only need to show one is impossible)
    • This sort of argument will be challenging!
  • What kind of characteristics make a language too hard for any of these to handle?

T/F: You can't make a DFA for a language (set of strings) with infinite cardinality, so a language with infinite cardinality cannot be a regular language.

证明正则只需给出一个 DFA/NFA/正则表达式; 证明不正则则要说明不存在任何 DFA (三者等价, 排除一种就够). 这是关于「所有可能的 DFA」的论证, 比构造难.

判断题答案: 错. 语言是无限集并不妨碍正则. \( \Sigma^* \)、a* 都是无限语言, 却有很简单的 DFA. DFA 状态有限, 但能处理无限多个不同的串, 因为它不需要把串记下来.

真正的障碍不是语言有多少个串, 而是识别它需要记住多少种不同的信息. a* 只需记住「目前是否都还是 a」这一种信息; \( a^n b^n \) 却要记住 a 的个数, 而个数可以任意大. 下面的可区分性就是把「需要记住的不同信息」变成可以证明的数学概念.

A Simple Language

  • Let \( \Sigma = \{a, b\} \) and consider the following language:

    \( E = \{ a^n b^n \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \)

  • \( E \) is the language of all strings of \( n \) a's followed by \( n \) b's:

    \( \{ \varepsilon, ab, aabb, aaabbb, aaaabbbb, \dots \} \)

Which of the following are correct regular expressions for the language E defined above?

  1. a*b*
  2. (ab)*
  3. \( \varepsilon \cup ab \cup a^2b^2 \cup a^3b^3 \)

例子语言 \( E = \{ a^n b^n \} \): 若干个 a 后面跟同样多的 b. 三个候选都不对:

  1. a*b* 不要求个数相等, 例如接受 aab.
  2. (ab)* 是 ab 重复, 例如接受 abab, 而且拒绝 aabb.
  3. 只列出了 n ≤ 3 的有限个串, 拒绝 aaaabbbb.

Slide 21–32

We seem to be running into some trouble. Why is that?

Let's imagine what a DFA for the language \( \{ a^n b^n \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \) would have to look like. Can we say anything about it?

This isn't a single transition. Think of it as “after reading aaaa, we end up at this state.”

These cannot be the same state!

What happens if \( q_n \) is… …an accepting state? We accept aabbbb ∉ E! …a rejecting state? We reject aaaabbbb ∈ E!

想象任意一个识别 E 的 DFA. 曲线箭头表示「读完这段串后到达的状态」. 设 aaaa 和 aa 读完后到达同一个状态 q_k. 之后都再读 bbbb, 由于 DFA 是确定的, 两者会到达同一个状态 q_n:

  • 若 q_n 接受, 就接受了 aabbbb, 但它不在 E 中;
  • 若 q_n 拒绝, 就拒绝了 aaaabbbb, 但它在 E 中.

两种都错, 所以 aaaa 和 aa 不能到达同一个状态.

Slide 33–34

What's Going On?

  • As you just saw, the strings \( a^4 \) and \( a^2 \) can't end up in the same state in any DFA for \( E = \{ a^n b^n \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \).
  • Two proof routes:
    • Direct: The states you reach for \( a^4 \) and \( a^2 \) have to behave differently when reading \( b^4 \) – in one case it should lead to an accept state, in the other it should lead to a reject state. Therefore, they must be different states.
    • Contradiction: Suppose you do end up in the same state. Then \( a^4 b^4 \) and \( a^2 b^4 \) end up in the same state, so we either reject \( a^4 b^4 \) (oops) or accept \( a^2 b^4 \) (oops).
  • Powerful intuition: Any DFA for \( E \) must keep \( a^4 \) and \( a^2 \) separated. It needs to remember something fundamentally different after reading those strings.

This idea – that two strings shouldn't end up in the same DFA state – is fundamental to discovering nonregular languages. Let's formalize this idea!

两种论证方式: 直接论证 (两个状态读 \( b^4 \) 后一个该接受一个该拒绝, 所以是不同状态) 和反证 (若相同就必然出错). 直觉: 任何识别 E 的 DFA 都必须把 \( a^4 \) 和 \( a^2 \) 分开, 读完它们之后要记住本质不同的信息. 「两个串不能落到同一个状态」是发现非正则语言的核心想法, 下面把它形式化.

可区分性
Distinguishability

Slide 35

Distinguishability

  • Let \( L \) be an arbitrary language over \( \Sigma \).
  • Two strings \( x \in \Sigma^* \) and \( y \in \Sigma^* \) are called distinguishable relative to \( L \) if there is a string \( w \in \Sigma^* \) such that exactly one of \( xw \) and \( yw \) is in \( L \).
  • We denote this by writing \( x \not\equiv_L y \).
  • In our previous example, we saw that \( a^2 \not\equiv_E a^4 \).
    • Try appending \( b^4 \) to both of them.
  • Formally, we say that \( x \not\equiv_L y \) if the following is true:

    \( \exists w \in \Sigma^*.\ (xw \in L \leftrightarrow yw \notin L) \)

定义: 两个串 x、y 相对于语言 L distinguishable 可区分, 指存在某个后缀 w, 使 xw 和 yw 中恰好一个属于 L. 记作 \( x \not\equiv_L y \). 一阶写法里 \( xw \in L \leftrightarrow yw \notin L \) 正是「恰好一个」: 二者一真一假.

例: \( a^2 \not\equiv_E a^4 \), 用的后缀是 \( w = b^4 \): \( a^4 b^4 \in E \), \( a^2 b^4 \notin E \).

易错: 可区分是相对某个语言而言的. 同样的 aa 和 aaaa, 相对 a* 就不可区分 (加任何后缀, 两者同时在或不在 a*).

对比一个正则语言: L = 「a 的个数为偶数」(字母表 {a, b}). \( \varepsilon \) 和 a 可区分: 加空后缀, \( \varepsilon \in L \), a ∉ L. 但任取三个串, 其中必有两个 a 的个数奇偶相同, 这两个串接上任何后缀后奇偶仍然相同, 不可区分. 所以 L 的区分集最多有 2 个元素, 正好对应它的两状态 DFA (偶 / 奇). 非正则语言的特征就是区分集可以无限大.

Slide 36–38

Theorem: Let \( L \) be an arbitrary language over \( \Sigma \). Let \( x \in \Sigma^* \) and \( y \in \Sigma^* \) be strings where \( x \not\equiv_L y \). Then if \( D \) is any DFA for \( L \), then \( D \) must end in different states when run on inputs \( x \) and \( y \).

Proof sketch: (diagram: \( x \) and \( y \) both lead from \( q_0 \) to \( q_k \); then \( w \) leads to \( q_n \), so \( xw \) and \( yw \) both end in \( q_n \).)

This diagram shows that we can make 3 distinguishable strings (forcing at least 3 states in this DFA, to keep those strings separated). If we expand the diagram, can we make 4? 5? How many?

定理: 若 \( x \not\equiv_L y \), 则任何识别 L 的 DFA 读完 x 和读完 y 必在不同状态. 证明梗概: 若都到 q_k, 再读区分后缀 w 就都到同一个 q_n, 于是 xw、yw 同时接受或同时拒绝, 与「恰好一个属于 L」矛盾.

推论: 如果有 k 个两两可区分的串, DFA 至少要有 k 个状态. 原图里 aa、aaa、aaaa 两两可区分 (例如 aa 和 aaa 用 bb 区分), 所以至少 3 个状态. 继续扩展: \( a^1, a^2, a^3, \dots \) 两两可区分, 想要多少个都可以, 这就是下面的论证.

Slide 39

A Bad Combination

  • Suppose there is a DFA \( D \) for the language \( E = \{ a^n b^n \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \).
  • We know the following:
    • Any two strings of the form \( a^m \) and \( a^n \), where \( m \ne n \), cannot end in the same state when run through \( D \).
    • There are infinitely many strings of the form \( a^m \).
    • However, there are only finitely many states they can end up in, since \( D \) is a deterministic finite automaton!
  • What happens if we put these pieces together?

三个事实放在一起: 任意两个不同的 \( a^m \)、\( a^n \) 必须落在不同状态; 这样的串有无穷多个; DFA 只有有限个状态. 鸽巢原理: 无穷多个串放进有限个状态, 必有两个落在同一状态. 矛盾, 所以 E 的 DFA 不存在.

Slide 40–42

Distinguishing Sets

  • Let \( L \) be a language over \( \Sigma \).
  • A distinguishing set for \( L \) is a set \( S \subseteq \Sigma^* \) where the following is true:

    \( \forall x \in S.\ \forall y \in S.\ (x \ne y \to x \not\equiv_L y) \)

If you pick any two distinct strings in \( S \) … then they're distinguishable relative to \( L \).

As an example, here's a distinguishing set for \( E = \{ a^n b^n \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \):

\( S = \{ a^n \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \)

IMPORTANT: A distinguishing set for language E is not a subset of E. It is a set of (beginning part) strings.

distinguishing set 区分集: 一个串的集合 S, 其中任意两个不同的串都相对于 L 可区分. 例: \( S = \{a^n\} \) 是 E 的区分集.

易错: 区分集不必是 L 的子集. S 里放的是「前缀」, 即读了一部分输入后 DFA 所处的情况. 例如 \( a^3 \) 不在 E 中, 但它是 S 的元素.

Slide 43–44

Theorem (Myhill-Nerode): If \( L \) is a language and \( S \) is a distinguishing set for \( L \) that contains infinitely many strings, then \( L \) is not regular.

Proof: Let \( L \) be an arbitrary language over \( \Sigma \) and let \( S \) be a distinguishing set for \( L \) that contains infinitely many strings. We will show that \( L \) is not regular.

Suppose for the sake of contradiction that \( L \) is regular. This means that there must be some DFA \( D \) for \( L \). Let \( k \) be the number of states in \( D \). Since there are infinitely many strings in \( S \), we can choose \( k+1 \) distinct strings from \( S \) and consider what happens when we run \( D \) on all of those strings. Because there are only \( k \) states in \( D \) and we've chosen \( k+1 \) strings from \( S \), by the pigeonhole principle we know that at least two strings from \( S \) must end in the same state in \( D \). Choose any two such strings and call them \( x \) and \( y \).

Because \( x \ne y \) and \( S \) is a distinguishing set for \( L \), we know that \( x \not\equiv_L y \). Our earlier theorem therefore tells us that when we run \( D \) on inputs \( x \) and \( y \), they must end up in different states. But this is impossible – we chose \( x \) and \( y \) precisely because they end in the same state when run through \( D \).

We have reached a contradiction, so our assumption must have been wrong. Thus \( L \) is not a regular language. \( \blacksquare \)

Myhill-Nerode 定理 (课程使用的版本): 若 L 有一个无限的区分集, 则 L 不正则. 证明就是上面「坏组合」的正式版:

  1. 反设 L 正则, 有一个 k 个状态的 DFA.
  2. 从无限集 S 中取 k + 1 个不同的串, 由鸽巢原理有两个 x、y 落在同一状态.
  3. 但 x ≠ y 且同在区分集里, 所以可区分, 由前面的定理必须落在不同状态. 矛盾.

完整的 Myhill-Nerode 定理还有另一半: 若 L 的所有区分集都有限, 则 L 正则 (最大区分集的大小就是最小 DFA 的状态数). 课件说这一半在 CS154 讲.

证明里的两个工具都是前面学过的: 反证法 (第 2 讲) 和鸽巢原理 (第 10 讲). 「取 k + 1 个串」这一步要求 S 至少有 k + 1 个元素, 这正是需要 S 无限的原因: 无论 DFA 有多少个状态, S 都比它多.

Slide 45–56

Using the Myhill-Nerode Theorem for \( E = \{ a^n b^n \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \)

Theorem: The language \( E = \{ a^n b^n \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \) is not regular.

Proof: Let \( S = \{ a^n \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \). We will prove that \( S \) is infinite and that \( S \) is a distinguishing set for \( E \).

To see that \( S \) is infinite, note that \( S \) contains one string for each natural number.

To see that \( S \) is a distinguishing set for \( E \), consider any strings strings \( a^m, a^n \in S \) where \( m \ne n \). Note that \( a^m b^m \in E \) and that \( a^n b^m \notin E \). Therefore, we see that \( a^m \not\equiv_E a^n \), as required.

Since \( S \) is infinite and is a distinguishing set for \( E \), by the Myhill-Nerode theorem we see that \( E \) is not regular. \( \blacksquare \)

What Just Happened?

  • We've just hit the limit of finite-memory computation.
  • To build a DFA for \( E = \{ a^n b^n \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \), we need to have different memory configurations (states) for all possible strings of the form \( a^n \).
  • There's no way to do this with finitely many possible states!

用 Myhill-Nerode 证明的模板, 两件事:

  1. S 是无限集 (每个自然数对应一个串).
  2. S 是区分集: 任取两个不同的 \( a^m \)、\( a^n \), 给出区分后缀 \( w = b^m \): \( a^m b^m \in E \), \( a^n b^m \notin E \) (个数不等).

注意证明里要对任意两个不同元素给出后缀, 后缀可以依赖于 m、n. 这里到达了有限内存计算的极限: 要识别 E, 必须为每个 \( a^n \) 准备不同的状态.

Slide 58–63

Another Language

  • Consider the following language EQ over the alphabet \( \Sigma = \{a, b, ≟\} \):

    EQ = { w≟w | w ∈ {a, b}* }

  • EQ is the language all strings consisting of the same string of a's and b's twice, with a ≟ symbol in-between.
  • Examples:

    ab≟ab ∈ EQ   bbb≟bbb ∈ EQ   ≟ ∈ EQ
    ab≟ba ∉ EQ   bbb≟aaa ∉ EQ   b≟ ∉ EQ

The Intuition

  • Intuitively, any machine for EQ has to be able to remember the contents of everything to the left of the ≟ so that it can match them against the contents of the string to the right of the ≟.
  • There are infinitely many possible strings we can see, but we only have finite memory to store which string we saw.
  • That's a problem... can we formalize this?

(图: x 和 y 都到达 q_k, 再读 ≟x 都到 q_n.) What happens if \( q_n \) is… …an accepting state? We accept y≟x ∉ EQ! …a rejecting state? We reject x≟x ∈ EQ!

语言 EQ: 一个 a、b 串, 一个 ≟ 符号, 再把同一个串写一遍. 例子里 ≟ 本身也在 EQ 中 (w 取空串). 直觉: 机器必须记住 ≟ 左边的全部内容才能和右边比较, 可能的内容有无穷多种, 有限内存记不住.

形式化: 两个不同的串 x、y 若落在同一状态, 都接上 ≟x, 就会同时接受或拒绝 x≟x (应接受) 和 y≟x (应拒绝).

Distinguishing Sets: We've identified a distinguishing set for EQ = { w≟w | w ∈ {a, b}* }: \( S = \{a, b\}^* \)

Theorem: The language EQ = { w≟w | w ∈ {a, b}* } is not regular.

Proof: Let \( S = \{a, b\}^* \). We will prove that \( S \) is infinite and that \( S \) is a distinguishing set for EQ.

To see that \( S \) is infinite, note that, for any \( n \in \mathbb{N} \), we have \( a^n \in S \). Therefore, \( S \) contains at least one string for each natural number, so \( S \) is infinite.

To see that \( S \) is a distinguishing set for EQ, consider any strings \( x, y \in S \) where \( x \ne y \). Then x≟x ∈ EQ and y≟x ∉ EQ. Therefore, \( x \not\equiv_{EQ} y \), as required.

Since \( S \) is infinite and a distinguishing set for EQ, by the Myhill-Nerode theorem we see that EQ is not regular, as required. \( \blacksquare \)

Slide 78

Approaching Myhill-Nerode

  • The challenge in using the Myhill-Nerode theorem is finding the right set of strings.
  • General intuition:
    • Start by thinking about what information a computer “must” remember in order to answer correctly.
    • Choose a group of strings that all require different information.
    • Prove that you have infinitely many strings and that the group of strings is a distinguishing set.

用 Myhill-Nerode 的难点是选对 S. 方法: 先想计算机「必须」记住什么 (如 a 的个数、≟ 左边的内容); 再选一组每个都需要不同信息的串; 最后证明 S 无限且两两可区分 (对每对给出后缀).

Which of the following are infinite distinguishing sets for P that we could use in a Myhill-Nerode proof?

(选项 1. \( \{ a^n \dots \} \), 2. \( \{ b^n \dots \} \), 3. …, 4. \( \{ a^n b \dots \} \), 5. \( \{ a^n b \dots \} \); 同样被截掉.)

回文语言 P: 正读反读相同的串. 例: abba、bbb、a、abaaba 是回文, abab、aabb 不是.

P 也不正则. 可用 \( S = \{ a^n b \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \): 任取 \( m \ne n \), 后缀 \( a^m \) 使 \( a^m b a^m \in P \), \( a^n b a^m \notin P \). 也可以直接用 \( \{a^n\} \): \( a^m \) 和 \( a^n \) 用后缀 \( b a^m \) 区分. 直觉: 机器要记住前半部分才能核对后半部分的镜像.

按模板写出完整证明: 设 \( S = \{ a^n b \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \} \). S 无限, 因为每个自然数 n 对应一个串. 任取 S 中两个不同的串 \( a^m b \) 和 \( a^n b \) (m ≠ n), 取后缀 \( w = a^m \): \( a^m b a^m \) 是回文, 在 P 中; \( a^n b a^m \) 中间的 b 两侧 a 的个数不同, 不是回文, 不在 P 中. 所以二者可区分, S 是无限区分集, 由 Myhill-Nerode 定理 P 不正则.

幻灯片错误: 回文语言的例子写成 “abba ∈ EQ”“abab ∉ EQ” 等, 应为 ∈ P / ∉ P; 这一页是从 EQ 那一页复制改的, 没改全 (Slide 80 的文字层里还残留着 ≟ 符号). 投票框同样伸出了幻灯片右边.

Slide 81–83

Tying Everything Together

  • One of the intuitions we hope you develop for DFAs is to have each state in a DFA represent some key piece of information the automaton has to remember.
  • If you only need to remember one of finitely many pieces of information, that gives you a DFA.
    • This can be made rigorous! Take CS154 for details.
  • If you need to remember one of infinitely many pieces of information, you can use the Myhill-Nerode theorem to prove that the language has no DFA.

Where We Stand

  • We've ended up where we are now by trying to answer the question “what problems can you solve with a computer?”
  • We defined a computer to be DFA, which means that the problems we can solve are precisely the regular languages.
  • We've discovered several equivalent ways to think about regular languages (DFAs, NFAs, and regular expressions) and used that to reason about the regular languages.
  • We now have a powerful intuition for where we ended up: DFAs are finite-memory computers, and regular languages correspond to problems solvable with finite memory.
  • Putting all of this together, we have a much deeper sense for what finite memory computation looks like – and what it doesn't look like!

总结: DFA 的每个状态代表一条要记住的信息. 只需记住有限种信息之一, 就能造出 DFA (严格的说法要到 CS154); 需要记住无限种信息之一, 就能用 Myhill-Nerode 证明不存在 DFA.

回顾这一段的路线: 从「计算机能解决什么问题」出发, 先把计算机建模成 DFA, 于是能解决的问题恰好是正则语言; 找到了 DFA、NFA、正则表达式三种等价描述; 最后得到直觉, DFA 就是有限内存的计算机, 正则语言就是有限内存能解决的问题. 现在既知道有限内存能做什么, 也知道它做不到什么 (如 \( a^n b^n \)、EQ、回文).
