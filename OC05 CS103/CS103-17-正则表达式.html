Language Concatenation

  • If \( w \in \Sigma^* \) and \( x \in \Sigma^* \), then \( wx \) is the concatenation of \( w \) and \( x \).
  • If \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) are languages over \( \Sigma \), the concatenation of \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) is the language \( L_1 L_2 \) defined as

    \( L_1 L_2 = \{ wx \mid w \in L_1 \text{ and } x \in L_2 \} \)

  • Example: if \( L_1 = \{ a, ba, bb \} \) and \( L_2 = \{ aa, bb \} \), then

    \( L_1 L_2 = \{ aaa, abb, baaa, babb, bbaa, bbbb \} \)

Lots and Lots of Concatenation: For \( L = \{ aa, b \} \): \( L^0 = \{\varepsilon\} \), \( L^2 = \{ aaaa, aab, baa, bb \} \), \( L^3 = \{ aaaaaa, aaaab, aabaa, aabb, baaaa, baab, bbaa, bbb \} \), …

The Kleene Closure: \( L^* = \{ w \in \Sigma^* \mid \exists n \in \mathbb{N}.\ w \in L^n \} \)

复习上一讲: 自动机的升级路线, 语言的连接、幂和克林闭包. 例子 \( L_1 L_2 \): L₁ 的 3 个串各接上 L₂ 的 2 个串, 共 6 个. 详见 CS103 16 · 封闭性.

Closure Properties

Theorem: If \( L_1 \) and \( L_2 \) are regular languages over an alphabet \( \Sigma \), then so are the following languages: \( \overline{L_1} \), \( L_1 \cup L_2 \), \( L_1 \cap L_2 \), \( L_1 L_2 \), \( L_1^* \).

These properties are called closure properties of the regular languages.

Quick check: Let \( \Sigma = \{1, 2, 3, a, b, c\} \). Let \( L_1 = \{aa, b\} \), \( L_2 = \{33, 2\} \) be languages over \( \Sigma \).

  1. Name two strings in \( L_1 \).
  2. Name two strings in \( L_1 \cup L_2 \).
  3. Name two strings in \( L_1 \cap L_2 \).
  4. Name two strings in \( L_1 L_2 \).
  5. Name two strings in \( L_1^* \).

五道快问的答案:

  1. \( L_1 \) 只有 aa 和 b 两个串.
  2. 并集有四个串, 例如 aa 和 33.
  3. 交集是空集: L₁ 和 L₂ 没有共同的串, 所以「说出两个」做不到. 这是题目里的陷阱.
  4. 连接: 例如 aa33、b2 (共 4 个: aa33, aa2, b33, b2).
  5. 星号: 例如 \( \varepsilon \) 和 aab. 注意空串总在 \( L^* \) 里.

Defining Regular Languages

  • We currently have several tools for showing a language \( L \) is regular:
    • Construct a DFA for \( L \).
      • We used this for a variety of languages, as well as proving closure for complement.
    • Construct an NFA for \( L \).
      • We used this for a variety of languages, as well as proving closure for union, concatenation, and Kleene *.
    • Combine several simpler regular languages together via closure properties to form \( L \).
      • We used this for proving closure for intersection.
      • (Today we'll greatly expand on this last idea.)

证明一个语言正则, 目前有三种方法: 构造 DFA; 构造 NFA; 用封闭性把简单的正则语言组合出来. 今天把第三种思路发展成正则表达式.

正则表达式
Another View of Regular Languages: Regular Expressions (regex)

Slide 15–17

(xkcd 漫画, 旁注: 80 minutes from now, you will be able to say this.)

Regular Expressions

  • Regular expressions are a way of describing a language via a string pattern-match representation.
  • They're used all over in practical day-to-day programming:
    • Data validation tools in just about every programming language (python, Javascript, C++, etc)
    • UNIX grep and flex tools to search files
    • Compilers

Constructing Regular Languages with RegEx

  • Idea: Build up all regular languages as follows:
    • Start with a small set of super-simple languages we already know to be regular.
    • Using closure properties, combine these simple languages together to form more languages.
  • Because we only use closure properties, the results of even the most elaborate combinations are guaranteed to be regular.
  • This is a bottom-up approach to the regular languages.

regular expression 正则表达式 (regex) 用一种模式串来描述语言. 它在编程中无处不在: 各种语言的数据校验、UNIX 的 grep 和 flex、编译器的词法分析.

思路是「自下而上」: 从几个最简单的、已知正则的语言出发, 只用保持正则的运算 (并、连接、星号) 组合. 因为只用了封闭运算, 组合出的结果一定正则.

Slide 19–21

RegEx “Starter Languages”

  • The regular expressions begin with three starter languages (a bit like base cases in induction!)
    1. The symbol \( \varnothing \) is a regular expression that represents the empty language \( \varnothing \).
    2. For any \( a \in \Sigma \), the symbol a is a regular expression for the language {a}. (So this “starter language” is actually many languages, one for each character in \( \Sigma \).)
    3. The symbol \( \varepsilon \) is a regular expression that represents the language \( \{\varepsilon\} \).

Combining RegEx

  • Let \( R_1 \) and \( R_2 \) be regular expressions (either one of the starter languages, or a more complex regex). We can combine them in these ways:
    • Concatenation: \( R_1 R_2 \)
    • Union: \( R_1 \cup R_2 \)
    • Kleene closure: \( R_1^* \)
    • Parentheses: \( (R_1) \) may be used to control order of operations, and for reading clarity.

Operator Precedence

  • Here's the operator precedence for regular expressions:

    \( (R_1) \)
    \( R_1^* \)
    \( R_1 R_2 \)
    \( R_1 \cup R_2 \)

  • So ab*c∪d is parsed as ((a(b*))c)∪d

正则表达式的定义也是递归的, 像归纳法一样有「基础情形」和「组合步骤」:

  • 三种起始表达式: \( \varnothing \) 表示空语言; 单个字符 a 表示 {a}; \( \varepsilon \) 表示 {\( \varepsilon \)}.
  • 三种组合: 连接 \( R_1 R_2 \), 并 \( R_1 \cup R_2 \), 星号 \( R_1^* \); 括号用来控制优先级.

优先级从高到低: 括号, 星号, 连接, 并. 所以 ab*c∪d 中星号只作用于 b, 然后 a、b*、c 连接, 最后和 d 取并.

易错: 星号只作用于紧挨着的那一个对象. ab* 是 a 后面跟任意多个 b, 不是任意多个 ab; 后者要写 (ab)*.

Slide 22–23

Regular Expression Examples

  • The regular expression trick∪treat represents the language { trick, treat }.
  • The regular expression booo* represents the regular language { boo, booo, boooo, … }.
  • The regular expression candy!(candy!)* represents the regular language { candy!, candy!candy!, candy!candy!candy!, … }.

Regular Expressions, Formally

  • The language of a regular expression is the language described by that regular expression.
  • Formally:
    • \( \mathscr{L}(\varepsilon) = \{\varepsilon\} \)
    • \( \mathscr{L}(\varnothing) = \varnothing \)
    • \( \mathscr{L}(a) = \{a\} \)
    • \( \mathscr{L}(R_1 R_2) = \mathscr{L}(R_1) \mathscr{L}(R_2) \)
    • \( \mathscr{L}(R_1 \cup R_2) = \mathscr{L}(R_1) \cup \mathscr{L}(R_2) \)
    • \( \mathscr{L}(R^*) = \mathscr{L}(R)^* \)
    • \( \mathscr{L}((R)) = \mathscr{L}(R) \)

Regex quick check: Let \( \Sigma = \{a, b, c, d\} \). Let \( L_1 = \mathscr{L}( a(b \cup c)((d)) ) \) be a language over \( \Sigma \). Name two strings in \( L_1 \). Name one string not in \( L_1 \).

例子: trick∪treat 是两个单词的集合; booo* 是 boo 后面跟零个或多个 o, 即 boo, booo, …; candy!(candy!)* 是 candy! 重复一次或多次.

正式地, 正则表达式 R 的语言 \( \mathscr{L}(R) \) 按结构递归定义, 每个组合符号对应上一讲的一个语言运算.

快问: a(b∪c)((d)) 去掉多余的括号就是 a(b∪c)d, 语言是 {abd, acd}, 恰好两个串. 不在其中的串例如 ad、abcd、\( \varepsilon \).

「至多一个 a」. 五个候选中正确的有 2 个:

  • Σ*aΣ*: 错, 表示「至少一个 a」, 而且不含没有 a 的串.
  • b*ab* ∪ b*: 对. 恰好一个 a, 或者没有 a.
  • b*(a ∪ ε)b*: 对. 中间那个位置要么是 a 要么什么都没有.
  • b*a*b* ∪ b*: 错. a* 允许 aa.
  • b*(a* ∪ ε)b*: 错. 同样 a* 允许多个 a.

常用简写 R? 表示「R 出现零次或一次」, 即 R ∪ ε, 所以答案可写成 b*a?b*.

A More Elaborate Design

  • Let \( \Sigma = \{ a, ., @ \} \), where a represents “some letter.”
  • Let's make a regex for email addresses.

cs103@cs.stanford.edu
first.middle.last@mail.site.org
dot.at@dot.com

aa*(.aa*)*
aa*(.aa*)*@
aa*(.aa*)*@aa*.aa*
aa*(.aa*)*@aa*.aa*(.aa*)*
a⁺(.aa*)*@aa*.aa*(.aa*)*
a⁺(.a⁺)*@a⁺.a⁺(.a⁺)*
a⁺(.a⁺)*@a⁺(.a⁺)⁺

写一个匹配邮件地址的正则 (a 代表任意字母). 逐步构造:

  1. 用户名: 一个或多个字母组成的块, 块之间用点分隔: aa*(.aa*)*.
  2. 接上 @.
  3. 域名: 至少两个块, 用点分隔: aa*.aa*(.aa*)*.
  4. 用简写 R⁺ = RR* (一次或多次) 化简: aa* 就是 a⁺.
  5. 域名「一块, 再接一个或多个 .块」就是 a⁺(.a⁺)⁺.

最终: a⁺(.a⁺)*@a⁺(.a⁺)⁺. 读法: 用户名可以没有点, 域名至少有一个点.

For Comparison

RegEx Shorthand Summary

  • \( R^n \) is shorthand for \( RR \dots R \) (\( n \) times).
    • Edge case: define \( R^0 = \varepsilon \).
  • \( \Sigma \) is shorthand for “any character in \( \Sigma \).”
  • \( R? \) is shorthand for \( (R \cup \varepsilon) \), meaning “zero or one copies of \( R \).”
  • \( R^+ \) is shorthand for \( RR^* \), meaning “one or more copies of \( R \).”

同一个邮件语言的 DFA 需要九个状态和一堆转移, 远不如一行正则表达式直观. 这说明正则表达式适合「描述语言长什么样」, 自动机适合「怎样一步步判断」.

简写汇总: \( R^n \) 是 R 重复 n 次 (\( R^0 = \varepsilon \)); \( \Sigma \) 是任意一个字符; R? 是零次或一次; R⁺ 是一次或多次. 这些都只是缩写, 不增加表达能力.

The Lay of the Land

Slide 70–72

(图: 「Languages you can build a DFA for」和「Languages you can build an NFA for」是同一个区域 Regular Languages; 问: Where do Regex languages fit?)

The Power of Regular Expressions

Theorem: If \( R \) is a regular expression, then \( \mathscr{L}(R) \) is regular.

Proof idea: Use induction!

  • Base cases: The atomic regular expressions all represent regular languages. (Remember I said they were like induction base cases? Well they literally are!)
  • The combination steps represent closure properties.
  • So anything you can make from them must be regular!

Thompson's Algorithm

  • In practice, many regex matchers use an algorithm called Thompson's algorithm to convert regular expressions into NFAs (and, from there, to DFAs).
    • Read Sipser if you're curious!
  • Fun fact: the “Thompson” here is Ken Thompson, one of the co-inventors of Unix!

DFA 和 NFA 识别的语言类相同 (正则语言). 正则表达式描述的语言在哪里? 第一个方向: 每个正则表达式的语言都正则. 证明用对表达式结构的归纳 (structural induction 结构归纳): 三种起始表达式的语言显然正则 (基础情形); 三种组合对应三种封闭性 (归纳步).

实际的正则匹配器常用 Thompson's algorithm: 按这个归纳过程把正则表达式直接拼成 NFA (每种组合用上一讲的 ε 边构造), 再按需转成 DFA. Thompson 就是 Unix 的共同发明人 Ken Thompson.

The Power of Regular Expressions

Theorem: For all languages \( L \), if \( L \) is a regular language, then there is a regular expression for \( L \).

This is not obvious!

Proof plan:

  • Assume: Pick an arbitrary regular language \( L \).
  • Want to show: We want to show there exists a regex for \( L \).
  • Since \( L \) is regular, there exists an NFA \( N \) for \( L \).
  • Describe how to transform \( N \) into a Regex, in a generalizable way. For the next few slides, we'll explore how to do that.

反方向: 每个正则语言都能写成正则表达式吗? 这一点不显然, 因为自动机可以有任意复杂的环路. 证明计划: 任取正则语言 L, 它有一个 NFA N; 给出一个通用方法把 N 变成正则表达式.

两个关键想法:

  1. 设想一种「广义 NFA」, 边上可以标任意正则表达式: 沿这条边走, 就是读入一段匹配该表达式的串. 真正的 NFA 不允许这样, 这只是证明里的思想实验.

Slide 131–132

The State-Elimination Algorithm

  • Start with an NFA \( N \) for the language \( L \).
  • Add a new start state \( q_s \) and accept state \( q_f \) to the NFA.
    • Add an ε-transition from \( q_s \) to the old start state of \( N \).
    • Add ε-transitions from each accepting state of \( N \) to \( q_f \), then mark them as not accepting.
  • Repeatedly remove states other than \( q_s \) and \( q_f \) from the NFA by “shortcutting” them until only two states remain: \( q_s \) and \( q_f \).
  • The transition from \( q_s \) to \( q_f \) is then a regular expression for the NFA.
  • To eliminate a state \( q \) from the automaton, do the following for each pair of states \( q_0 \) and \( q_1 \), where there's a transition from \( q_0 \) into \( q \) and a transition from \( q \) into \( q_1 \):
    • Let \( R_{in} \) be the regex on the transition from \( q_0 \) to \( q \).
    • Let \( R_{out} \) be the regex on the transition from \( q \) to \( q_1 \).
    • If there is a regular expression \( R_{stay} \) on a transition from \( q \) to itself, add a new transition from \( q_0 \) to \( q_1 \) labeled \( ((R_{in})(R_{stay})^*(R_{out})) \).
    • If there isn't, add a new transition from \( q_0 \) to \( q_1 \) labeled \( ((R_{in})(R_{out})) \)
  • If a pair of states has multiple transitions between them labeled \( R_1, R_2, \dots, R_k \), replace them with a single transition labeled \( R_1 \cup R_2 \cup \dots \cup R_k \).

一般算法 (state elimination 状态消除):

  1. 加新起点 qₛ 和新终点 q_f, 用 ε 边接好, 旧接受状态改为不接受.
  2. 逐个删去其他状态. 删状态 q 时, 对每一对「有边进 q 的状态 q₀」和「有边从 q 出的状态 q₁」, 加一条捷径边 q₀ → q₁, 标签是 \( R_{in} R_{stay}^* R_{out} \) (q 没有自环时就是 \( R_{in} R_{out} \)). 注意 q₀ 和 q₁ 可以是同一个状态, 这时捷径边是自环.
  3. 同一对状态之间有多条边时, 合并成一条, 标签用并连起来.
  4. 只剩 qₛ 和 q_f 时, 它们之间那条边的标签就是答案.

删除顺序不影响正确性, 但会影响表达式的长短.

小例子: 字母表 {a}, DFA 有两个状态, q₀ (起始, 接受) 和 q₁, 读 a 在二者之间来回, 语言是偶数个 a.

  1. 加 qₛ —ε→ q₀, 加 q₀ —ε→ q_f, q₀ 改为不接受.
  2. 删 q₁: 进 q₁ 的只有 q₀ —a→ q₁, 出 q₁ 的只有 q₁ —a→ q₀, q₁ 无自环. 得到捷径 q₀ —aa→ q₀, 是 q₀ 的自环.
  3. 删 q₀: 进来 qₛ —ε→ q₀, 出去 q₀ —ε→ q_f, 自环 aa. 得到 qₛ —ε(aa)*ε→ q_f.

化简得 (aa)*, 正是「偶数个 a」. 状态消除的结果常常带着多余的 ε 和括号, 需要手工化简.

(图: Regular Languages = Languages you can build a DFA for = Languages you can build an NFA for = Languages you can write a Regex for.)

Our Transformations: DFA → NFA (direct conversion); NFA → DFA (subset construction); NFA → Regexp (state elimination); Regexp → NFA (Thompson's algorithm).

Theorem: The following are all equivalent:

  • \( L \) is a regular language.
  • There is a DFA \( D \) such that \( \mathscr{L}(D) = L \).
  • There is an NFA \( N \) such that \( \mathscr{L}(N) = L \).
  • There is a regular expression \( R \) such that \( \mathscr{L}(R) = L \).

三种描述完全等价, 四个转换构成闭环: DFA 直接就是 NFA; NFA 经子集构造变 DFA; NFA 经状态消除变正则表达式; 正则表达式经 Thompson 算法变 NFA. 这个结论通常叫 Kleene's theorem 克林定理: 一个语言正则, 当且仅当有 DFA, 当且仅当有 NFA, 当且仅当有正则表达式. 证明某语言正则时可以选最方便的一种.
