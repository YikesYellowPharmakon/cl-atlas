Venn diagram 韦恩图用圆表示集合, 圆的重叠部分表示两个集合共有的元素. 这里 A = {1, 2, 3}, B = {3, 4, 5}, 只有 3 同时属于两者, 所以画在中间的交叠区; 1、2 只在 A 的圆里, 4、5 只在 B 的圆里.

Slide 6

Venn Diagrams

两种最基本的组合:

  • Union 并集 A∪B: 属于 A 或属于 B (或两者都属于) 的元素, 这里是 {1,2,3,4,5}. 两个圆全部涂满.
  • Intersection 交集 A∩B: 同时属于 A 和 B 的元素, 这里是 {3}. 只涂交叠的部分.

写成集合构造记法: A∪B={x∣x∈A or x∈B}, A∩B={x∣x∈A and x∈B}. 并集里的「或」是包含意义的或, 3 在两边都有, 仍只算一次.

Slide 7

Venn Diagrams

另外两种组合:

  • Difference 差集 A−B, 也写作 A∖B: 属于 A 但不属于 B 的元素, 这里是 {1,2}. 从 A 里去掉和 B 共有的部分.
  • Symmetric difference 对称差 AΔB: 恰好属于其中一个集合的元素, 这里是 {1,2,4,5}. 两个圆都涂, 但交叠部分空着.

对称差可以用前面的运算表示: AΔB=(A∖B)∪(B∖A)=(A∪B)∖(A∩B).

易错: 差集没有交换律, B∖A={4,5}=A∖B; 并、交、对称差都有交换律.

什么是证明
What is a Proof?

Slide 11–13

A proof is an argument that demonstrates why a conclusion is true, subject to certain standards of truth.

A mathematical proof is an argument that demonstrates why a mathematical statement is true, following the rules of mathematics.

Proofwriting is not like other forms of writing, or even other forms of math problems, and we understand this can be a big adjustment!

Here is some advice from years of teaching proofwriting.

proof 证明是一段论证, 按照某种公认的「真」的标准说明一个结论为什么成立. mathematical proof 数学证明则按数学规则说明一个数学命题为什么成立. 法庭上的证据、实验科学的统计检验也是证明, 只是标准不同; 数学证明的标准最严: 每一步都必须由定义、已知定理或逻辑规则推出.

写证明和写作文不同, 也和算题不同: 算题只要答案, 证明要的是理由的完整链条. 下面是几条规则和配套建议.

Slide 14

Rule: Proofs are meant to argue something precisely and completely.

Advice: Well trained readers should find this persuasive, however precision and completeness are the goals you should have in mind, not persuasion in the usual social-emotional-rhetorical way that we think of persuasion.

规则一: 证明要精确而完整地论证一件事. 受过训练的读者读完自然会被说服, 但你写的时候要追求的是精确和完整, 而不是平常意义上的「说服」: 不靠语气、修辞、情感, 不写「显然」「大家都知道」来糊过去.

Slide 15

Rule: Proofs are structured, mechanical, functional. They describe precise processes (algorithms) on formally introduced variables.

Advice: Think of a proof less as storytelling and more like programming (or driving directions, as we'll see next). Though, it can be very helpful as a proofwriter to have a vision of the story you want to tell in your mind and, as you write, translate that story into proof structure.

规则二: 证明是有结构的、机械的、讲求功能的. 它描述的是对正式引入的变量执行的一套精确步骤, 像算法一样. 所以写证明更像写程序 (或者写路线说明), 而不像讲故事: 每个变量先声明再使用, 每一步都有依据.

不过, 动笔前心里有一个「故事」(为什么会成立的直觉) 很有帮助; 写的时候再把这个故事翻译成证明的结构.

Slide 16

Rule: Skipping even one step of a proof is a big deal—it makes the proof logically invalid.

Advice: Think of a proof as written driving directions from Point A to Point B, for someone who has never been, and who doesn't have a GPS/phone. If you leave out one step, the driver will never get to the next street name in the sequence. They simply can't continue, and will be permanently lost!

(图: 斯坦福 Gates 楼到 Bryant St 的导航步骤截图, 旁注: You could drive on Campus Dr forever and never get to Bryant St! :-( )

Proofs are powerful, but their correctness is quite fragile.

规则三: 证明里漏掉哪怕一步, 整个证明在逻辑上就不成立. 比喻: 你给一个从没去过、也没有手机导航的人写路线. 漏掉一个转弯, 他就永远到不了下一条街, 只能在 Campus Dr 上一直开下去.

证明的力量在于它一旦成立就绝对成立, 代价是它很「脆」: 一个缺口就让整条链断掉.

写出第一个证明
Writing our First Proof

Slide 18

Definitions:

An integer n is called even if there is an integer k where n=2k.

An integer n is called odd if there is an integer k where n=2k+1.

Additionally, in this class, we will assume the following:

  1. Every integer is either even or odd.
  2. No integer is both even and odd.

两个正式定义: 整数 n 是 even 偶数, 指存在整数 k 使 n=2k; 是 odd 奇数, 指存在整数 k 使 n=2k+1. 注意定义里的「存在一个整数 k」: 偶数不是「能被 2 整除」这种模糊说法, 而是「能写成 2 乘以某个整数」.

另外课程直接假定两条事实: 每个整数非奇即偶, 没有整数既奇又偶. 这两条可以证明 (用带余除法), 但本课不要求, 做题时可以直接用.

例子: −3 是奇数, 因为 −3=2⋅(−2)+1, 这里 k = −2 也是整数. 0 是偶数, 因为 0=2⋅0.

Slide 19–21

Theorem: For all integers n, if n is even, then n2 is even.

Step 1: find the formal definitions for any terms in the theorem. (Not just what you intuitively understand the concept of “even” to mean.)

Definition: An integer n is called even if there is an integer k where n=2k.

要证的定理: 对所有整数 n, 如果 n 是偶数, 那么 n2 也是偶数.

第一步: 找出定理里每个术语的正式定义, 而不是凭直觉理解「偶数」是什么意思. 这里只有一个术语 even, 定义见上一张卡片. 证明里所有关于「偶」的推理都要回到「存在 k 使 n = 2k」这句话上.

Slide 22–25

Theorem: For all integers n, if n is even, then n2 is even.

Step 2: examine the grammatical structure of the theorem, because this dictates how we will structure our proof.

“For all” means we need this to be true for all examples of elements of the set integers. So we challenge our reader: “pick ANY integer!!”

The “if...then...” means that the “then” part is only guaranteed to hold when the “if” part is true. So we are going to tell our reader to pick any integer assuming that the “if” condition is true of it. In other words, only pick even integers.

第二步: 分析定理的语法结构, 因为结构决定证明怎么写. 这个定理有两层:

  • 「For all integers n」(对所有整数): 结论要对每一个整数都成立. 所以证明时我们向读者挑战: 「随便挑一个整数!」
  • 「if … then …」(如果 … 那么 …): 只有当「如果」部分成立时, 「那么」部分才被保证成立. 所以我们让读者挑的整数要满足「如果」的条件, 也就是只挑偶数.

两层合起来, 证明的开头就是「任取一个偶数 n」.

Slide 26–30

Our First Proof!

Theorem: For all integers n, if n is even, then n2 is even.

Proof: Pick an arbitrary even integer n. We want to show that n2 is even.

Since n is even, there is some integer k such that n=2k. This means that

n2​amp;=(2k)2amp;=4k2amp;=2(2k2).​

From this, we see that there is an integer m (namely, 2k2) where n2=2m. Therefore, n2 is even, which is what we wanted to show. ■

逐句看这个证明:

  1. 「任取一个偶数 n」: 处理 for all 和 if 两层结构.
  2. 「我们要证 n2 是偶数」: 说明目标.
  3. 「因为 n 是偶数, 存在整数 k 使 n = 2k」: 把假设按定义展开.
  4. 代数计算: n2=(2k)2=4k2=2(2k2). 最后一步故意写成「2 乘以某个东西」, 这样才能对上偶数的定义.
  5. 「所以存在整数 m (就是 2k2) 使 n2=2m」: 这正是「n2 是偶数」的定义. 这里要用到「整数的乘积还是整数」, 所以 2k2 是整数.

结尾的 ■ 表示证明结束.

幻灯片错误: Slide 26–28 的证明第二句写成 “We want to show that n is even.”, 要证的应该是 n2 是偶数 (n 是偶数是假设, 不用证). Slide 29 起已改成 “We want to show that n² is even.”, 上面按改正后的版本照录.

在动笔写证明之前, 可以先在草稿纸上试几个例子: 2、10、0、−8 的平方都能写成 2 乘以某个整数. 问题是: 2 后面那个数 (2, 50, 0, 32) 有什么规律, 怎样对任意的 n 预测它? 规律是: n = 2k 时, 那个数是 2k2 (k = 1 时是 2, k = 5 时是 50, k = −4 时是 32).

Slide 39–41

This symbol (■) means “end of proof.” It's basically math nerd “mic drop.”

To prove a statement of the form “For all x...” — Pick an arbitrary x.

To prove a statement of the form “If P, then Q.” — Start by assuming P, then state “We want to show that Q.”

“Assume”   “Want to Show” (nickname “WTS” for short)

These first two sentences of setup for the proof are so critical that they have their own names to refer to them!

从第一个证明里提炼出两条通用规则:

  • 要证「对所有 x, …」: 先任取一个 x (pick an arbitrary x).
  • 要证「如果 P, 那么 Q」: 先假设 P, 再写「我们要证 Q」.

证明的头两句非常关键, 各有名字: 第一句叫 Assume (假设), 第二句叫 Want to Show (要证), 简称 WTS. 这种「假设前提、推出结论」的证明叫 direct proof 直接证明. 末尾的 ■ 是「证毕」, 课件开玩笑说它相当于数学人的「扔麦克风」.

下一个证明
Our Next Proof

Slide 44–45

Theorem: For all integers m and n, if m and n are odd, then m+n is even.

To prove a statement of the form “For all x...” — Pick an arbitrary x.

To prove a statement of the form “If P, then Q.” — Start by assuming P, then state “We want to show that Q.”

第二个定理: 对所有整数 m 和 n, 如果 m 和 n 都是奇数, 那么 m + n 是偶数. 结构和第一个一样, 只是有两个变量: 「对所有」→ 任取 m 和 n; 「如果 … 那么」→ 假设二者是奇数, 要证和是偶数.

Slide 46

Theorem: For all integers m and n, if m and n are odd, then m+n is even.

Your Turn: How many of these would be a good first sentence of our proof? (the “Assume” step)

  • We want to show that if m and n are odd, then m+n is even.
  • Pick arbitrary odd integers n and m.
  • Consider n=7 and m=3.
  • Let n and m be arbitrary integers.
  • Since n and m are odd, there are integers k and r such that n=2k+1 and m=2r+1.

合格的只有第二句「任取奇数 n 和 m」, 它同时完成了「任取」和「假设奇数」. 其余几句的问题:

  • 第一句是在复述整个定理, 不是假设.
  • 第三句只取了 7 和 3 这一个例子, 证明了这个例子不等于证明了所有情况.
  • 第四句只任取了整数, 没有假设它们是奇数; 后面要用到奇数的定义就没有依据 (除非紧接着补一句「假设 m 和 n 是奇数」).
  • 第五句是展开定义的一步, 应该是第三句; 作为第一句它用了还没引入的 n 和 m.
Slide 47–51

Theorem: For all integers m and n, if m and n are odd, then m+n is even.

Proof: Pick arbitrary integers m and n where m and n are odd. We want to show that m+n is even.

Since m is odd, we know that there is an integer k where

m=2k+1.(1)

Similarly, because n is odd there must be some integer r such that

n=2r+1.(2)

By adding equations (1) and (2) we learn that

m+n​amp;=2k+1+2r+1amp;=2k+2r+2amp;=2(k+r+1).(3)​

Equation (3) tells us that there is an integer s (namely, k+r+1) such that m+n=2s. Therefore, we see that m+n is even, as required. ■

结构和第一个证明完全平行:

  1. Assume: 任取整数 m、n, 并假设二者都是奇数. WTS: 要证 m + n 是偶数.
  2. 按定义展开两个假设. 关键是两个数要用不同的字母: m = 2k + 1, n = 2r + 1. 如果都写成 2k + 1, 就等于偷偷假设了 m = n.
  3. 相加并整理成「2 乘以某个整数」的形式: 2(k+r+1).
  4. 指出 s = k + r + 1 是整数, 对上偶数的定义, 证毕.

易错: 第 2 步用同一个 k 是最常见的错误. 课件在最后「本讲规则」里也强调: 不要复用或混用变量名.

画图的直觉: 奇数 2k + 1 画成「两排各 k 块, 再多一块」. 两个奇数放在一起, 两个「多出来的一块」正好凑成一对, 拼完是两整排, 每排 k + r + 1 块. 所以和是偶数. 这就是式 (3) 里 2k+2r+2=2(k+r+1) 的几何意思.

Slide 59–61

We ask the reader to make an arbitrary choice. Rather than specifying what m and n are, we're signaling to the reader that they could, in principle, supply any choices of m and n that they'd like. By letting the reader pick m and n arbitrarily, anything we prove about m and n will generalize to all possible choices for those values. This bold challenge to take on any choice of values makes our proof strong!

Style note: (optional) Numbering these equalities lets us refer back to them later on, making the flow of the proof a bit easier to understand.

Style note: (required) This is a complete sentence! Proofs are expected to be written in complete sentences, so you'll often use punctuation at the end of formulas.

三条说明:

  • 为什么「任取」就够了: 我们不指定 m 和 n 是多少, 而是告诉读者「你想选什么都行」. 既然证明对读者的任何选择都成立, 结论就推广到了所有可能的值. 这正是「对所有」的证法.
  • 给等式编号 (可选): 像 (1)(2)(3) 那样编号, 后面就能写「把 (1) 和 (2) 相加」, 读起来更清楚.
  • 写完整的句子 (必须): 证明是由句子组成的, 公式也是句子的一部分, 所以公式后面常常要加句号或逗号. 例如上面的 m=2k+1. 是一个完整句子的结尾.

四条关于奇偶的定理: 偶 ± 偶 = 偶; 奇 ± 偶 = 奇; 任意整数 × 偶 = 偶; 奇 × 奇 = 奇. 以后可以直接引用, 作业里也能用. 想练习的话, 每一条都能用今天的套路证明. 以最后一条为例:

  1. Assume: 任取奇数 m、n. WTS: mn 是奇数.
  2. 展开: m = 2k + 1, n = 2r + 1.
  3. 计算: mn=4kr+2k+2r+1=2(2kr+k+r)+1.
  4. 2kr + k + r 是整数, 所以 mn 是奇数.

全称命题与存在命题
Universal and Existential Statements

Slide 65–67

Theorem: For all odd integers n, there exist integers r and s where r2−s2=n.

This result is true for every possible choice of odd integer n. It'll work for n=1, n=137, n=103, etc.

We aren't saying this is true for every choice of r and s. Rather, we're saying that somewhere out there are (one or more) choices of r and s where this works.

定理: 对所有奇数 n, 存在整数 r 和 s, 使 r2−s2=n. 也就是每个奇数都能写成两个平方数之差.

这个定理里有两种不同的说法. 对 n 是「对所有」: 每个奇数都要成立, 1、137、103 都行. 对 r 和 s 是「存在」: 不是说任意 r、s 都满足, 而是说至少能找到一组 r、s 让等式成立.

Slide 68

Universal vs. Existential Statements

  • A universally-quantified statement is a statement of the form

    For all x, [some-property] holds for x.

    • We've seen how to prove these statements.
  • An existentially-quantified statement is a statement of the form

    There is some x where [some-property] holds for x.

    • How do you prove an existentially-quantified statement?

universally-quantified statement 全称量化命题: 「对所有 x, x 具有某性质」, 证法已经会了 (任取 x). existentially-quantified statement 存在量化命题: 「存在某个 x 具有某性质」. 存在命题怎么证? 见下面两张.

Slide 69–75

Theorem: For all integers n, if n is odd, then there exist integers r and s where r2−s2=n.

Proof: Pick an arbitrary odd integer n. We will show that there exist integers r and s where r2−s2=n.

Since n is odd, we know there is an integer k where n=2k+1. Now, let r=k+1 and s=k. Then we see that

r2−s2​amp;=(k+1)2−k2amp;=k2+2k+1−k2amp;=2k+1amp;=n.​

This means that r2−s2=n, which is what we needed to show. ■

Your Turn: Write the first sentence of the proof (Assume step). / Write the second sentence of the proof (WTS step).

课堂练习问的是头两句: Assume 是「任取一个奇数 n」(Pick an arbitrary odd integer n.), WTS 是「我们要证存在整数 r 和 s 使 r2−s2=n」. 证明正文:

  1. 展开定义: n = 2k + 1.
  2. 给出具体的 r 和 s: r = k + 1, s = k. 这是存在命题的证法, 直接把要找的东西摆出来.
  3. 验证它们确实满足: (k+1)2−k2=2k+1=n.

例子: n = 137 时 k = 68, 取 r = 69, s = 68, 验算 692−682=4761−4624=137.

Slide 76

Proving an Existential Statement

  • Over the course of the quarter, we will see several different ways to prove an existentially-quantified statement of the form

    There is an x where [some-property] holds for x.

  • Approach: Search far and wide, find a concrete example value for x that has the right property. In the proof, (1) announce the find to your reader, then (2) show why your choice is correct.

证明存在命题的基本方法: 先在草稿纸上到处找, 找到一个具体的、满足性质的 x; 写证明时分两步: (1) 向读者宣布你找到的值 (「令 r = k + 1」), (2) 说明它为什么满足要求. 课程后面还会学别的方法 (如反证), 但这是最直接的一种.

易错: 「找」的过程 (试例子、画图) 不写进证明, 证明里只写结果和验证.

取整函数与分情况证明
Floors, Ceilings, and Proof by Cases

Slide 91–92

Theorem: If n is an integer, then ⌈n/2⌉+⌊n/2⌋=n.

Floors and Ceilings

  • The notation ⌈x⌉ represents the ceiling of x, the smallest integer greater than or equal to x.

    ⌈1⌉=1⌈1.5⌉=2⌈−1⌉=−1⌈−1.5⌉=−1

  • The notation ⌊x⌋ represents is the floor of x, the largest integer less than or equal to x.

    ⌊1⌋=1⌊1.5⌋=1⌊−1⌋=−1⌊−1.5⌋=−2

(图: 一条标着 −3 到 3 的数轴.)

新定理: 对任意整数 n, ⌈n/2⌉+⌊n/2⌋=n. 先认识两个记号:

  • ceiling 向上取整 ⌈x⌉: 大于或等于 x 的最小整数. 在数轴上往右走到第一个整数.
  • floor 向下取整 ⌊x⌋: 小于或等于 x 的最大整数. 在数轴上往左走到第一个整数.

x 本身是整数时两者都等于 x. 易错: 负数. ⌊−1.5⌋=−2 而不是 −1, 因为 −2 在 −1.5 的左边; ⌈−1.5⌉=−1. 取整不是「去掉小数部分」(那叫 truncation, 对负数结果不同).

原文笔误: Slide 92 写 “The notation ⌊x⌋ represents is the floor of x”, 多了一个 is, 应为 “represents the floor of x”.

Slide 101–104

Theorem: If n is an integer, then ⌊n/2⌋+⌈n/2⌉=n.

Proof: Let n be an integer. We want to show that ⌊n/2⌋+⌈n/2⌉=n. To do so, we consider two cases:

Case 1: n is even. This means there is an integer k such that n=2k. Some algebra then tells us that

⌈2n​⌉+⌊2n​⌋​amp;=⌈22k​⌉+⌊22k​⌋amp;=⌈k⌉+⌊k⌋amp;=2kamp;=n.​

Case 2: n is odd. Then there's an integer k where n=2k+1, and

⌈2n​⌉+⌊2n​⌋​amp;=⌈22k+1​⌉+⌊22k+1​⌋amp;=⌈k+21​⌉+⌊k+21​⌋amp;=(k+1)+kamp;=2k+1amp;=n.​

In either case, we see that ⌊n/2⌋+⌈n/2⌉=n, as required. ■

这叫 proof by cases 分情况证明. 课程假定每个整数非奇即偶, 所以只要两种情况都证了, 就覆盖了所有整数.

  • 情况 1, n = 2k: n/2 = k 是整数, 上取整和下取整都是 k, 相加 2k = n.
  • 情况 2, n = 2k + 1: n/2 = k + 1/2. 比它大的最小整数是 k + 1, 比它小的最大整数是 k, 相加 2k + 1 = n.

最后一句「在两种情况下都有 …」把结论收拢, 是给读者的交代.

Slide 101–103

Turns out that we can just smash our two proofs together—the odd one and the even one—and that counts as a proof for all integers—yay!! Thanks, Proof by Cases!

The case labels in effect introduce the new assumptions you wish you had, to make the proof solvable. Then you proceed to show your work from there.

At the end of a split into cases, it's a nice courtesy to explain to the reader what it was that you established in each case.

分情况证明的三个要点:

  • 把偶数情况和奇数情况的两个证明「拼」在一起, 就是对所有整数的证明, 前提是各情况合起来覆盖全部可能.
  • 情况标签 (Case 1: n is even) 实际上给你引入了一个「你希望有的」新假设, 于是又有定义可以展开了.
  • 分完情况后, 最好向读者总结一下每种情况各自证明了什么.

本讲学到的证明规则
Reviewing What We Learned Today

Slide 106

Proofwriting Rules We Learned Today

  • Direct proof: A proof pattern/technique where the first two sentences of the proof are (1) assume the “if” part of theorem, (2) “want to show” the “then” part of the theorem.
  • To prove a universal, “pick an arbitrary.”
  • To prove an existential, (1) announce a concrete value that works, then (2) justify that it works.
  • Pay attention to and use formal definitions of terms.
  • Write in complete sentences.
  • Clearly introduce variable names using prescribed language. Don't reuse/overlap variable names.
  • Proof by Cases: A proof technique where you divide a situation into all possible cases, and prove each one separately, to prove the whole. Give clear case labels, which act as assumptions.
  • For these and more rules, see the Proofwriting Checklist!

逐条对应本笔记的位置:

  • Direct proof: 会写头两句: 假设「如果」部分 (Assume), 写出要证的「那么」部分 (WTS). 见 第一个证明.
  • Universal: 会用「任取」处理「对所有」, 并理解为什么这能推广到全部. 见 第二个证明.
  • Existential: 会先给出具体值再验证, 如 r = k + 1, s = k. 见 全称与存在.
  • Formal definitions: 会把「偶」「奇」展开成 n = 2k、n = 2k + 1 并在结尾对回定义.
  • Complete sentences: 公式是句子的一部分, 带标点.
  • Variable names: 用规定的说法引入变量, 不同对象用不同字母 (m = 2k + 1, n = 2r + 1).
  • Proof by Cases: 会按奇偶等方式分情况, 情况要覆盖全部, 每种情况标签当作假设. 见 分情况证明.

更多规则见课程的 Proofwriting Checklist (本课的指南合集里有笔记).

证明是一场对话
Ways of thinking about Proofwriting: Proofs as a Dialog

Slide 107–118

Proofs as a Dialog

Pick an arbitrary odd integer n.
Since n is an odd integer, there is an integer k such that n=2k+1.
Now, let z=k−34.

Proof Writer (You)   Proof Reader

n=137 — Reader Picks
k=68 — Neither Picks (just a consequence of prior Reader Pick)
z=34 — Writer Picks

Reader picks are for universally quantified entities. Nobody picks consequences of definitions. Writer picks are for satisfying existentially quantified WTS.

把证明想象成写证明的人 (你) 和读者之间的对话, 每个变量都要问: 谁来选它的值?

  • 「任取一个奇数 n」: 读者选. 比如读者选了 n = 137. 全称量化的变量归读者选, 你的证明必须应付读者的任何选择.
  • 「存在 k 使 n = 2k + 1」: 谁也没选. n = 137 一旦定了, k 只能是 68, 它是定义推出来的结果.
  • 「令 z = k − 34」: 你 (写的人) 选, 这里 z = 34. 你选值是为了满足存在量化的 WTS.

这个视角可以检查证明是否合法: 如果你在全称变量上偷偷选了具体值 (「取 n = 7」), 读者就会说「我没选 7」, 证明就失效了.

Slide 119

Who Owns What?

  • The reader chooses and owns a value if you use wording like this:
    • Pick an arbitrary natural number n.
    • Consider some n∈N.
    • Let n be a natural number.
    • Let n be an arbitrary n∈N.
  • The writer (you) chooses and owns a value if you use wording like this:
    • We choose r=n+1.
    • Pick s=n.
  • Neither of you chooses a value if you use wording like this:
    • Since n is even, we know there is some k∈Z where n=2k.
    • Because n is odd, there must be some integer k where n=2k+1.

措辞决定变量归谁:

  • 读者选: Pick an arbitrary …, Consider some …, Let n be a … . 不给具体值, 只说 n 属于什么集合.
  • 你选: We choose r = …, Pick s = … . 带等号, 给出具体的值或式子.
  • 谁都不选: Since n is even, there is some k … . 由已知事实和定义推出存在的对象.

易错: 同一个动词 Pick 两种用法都有: 「Pick an arbitrary n」是读者选, 「Pick s = n」是你选, 区别在于有没有给出具体值.
