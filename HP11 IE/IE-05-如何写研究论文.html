Cognitive Offloading Effect

  • Cognitive offloading occurs when people rely on external tools to perform mental tasks they once did themselves.
  • It is natural and advantageous for humans to cognitively offload some things – however, with GenAI, there is evidence that people are going far beyond offloading calculation, analysis or data retrieval. They are now “outsourcing even the smallest decisions to GenAI.”
  • This erodes the critical thinking that makes human mind valuable!

! Judgmental mind ≠ Critical mind

https://dialoguereview.com/the-risks-of-cognitive-offloading/

第 4 周的研讨课讲怎样写研究论文, 先从「用 AI 做研究、写论文、做报告」讲起. cognitive offloading 认知卸载: 人依赖外部工具去完成以前自己动脑做的事. 把一部分事情卸载出去是自然而且有利的 (比如用计算器), 但有证据表明, 有了生成式 AI, 人们卸载的远不止计算、分析或查资料, 而是「连最小的决定都外包给 AI」. 这会侵蚀批判性思维, 而批判性思维正是人脑的价值所在.

「Judgmental mind ≠ Critical mind」: 爱下判断 (很快地说好坏) 不等于有批判性思维 (检查理由和证据再下结论).

Skills in the age of AI

  • "seniorization" of roles. Because AI can instantly execute routine, lower-level cognitive tasks, the market has rapidly pivoted toward professionals who function as high-level "connectors" and comprehensive end-to-end builders. > The "Missing Rung" problem > "Synthetic Senior"
  • critical thinking to fact-check,

  • And some additional Soft-Skills "Glue": Leadership, resilience, empathy, and active listening, basically strong communication skills
  • Report on CHANGING LANDSCAPE OF SKILLS IN THE AGE OF AI

AI 时代需要的技能: 岗位在「资深化」. AI 能立刻完成常规的、低层次的认知工作, 劳动力市场于是迅速转向能做高层「连接者」、能从头到尾搭建完整系统的人. 由此带来两个说法: 「缺失的梯级」(入门岗位被 AI 取代, 新人没有台阶往上爬) 和「合成的资深者」(靠 AI 显得资深, 其实没有经验). 需要的能力: 用批判性思维核查事实; 审核 AI 应用并对它做评测 (evals); 再加上起「粘合」作用的软技能: 领导力、抗压能力、同理心、积极倾听, 归结起来就是很强的沟通能力.

Slide 5–7

AI Paradox

AI is supposed to be your junior assistant, doing the heavy lifting.

  • The Reality: You cannot manage an assistant if you do not know their job.
  • Without your own knowledge/experience, you cannot tell the difference between brilliant insights and confident lies (hallucinations).

The Verification Trap

  • Zero Knowledge: You blindly copy-paste. If the AI makes a mistake, you inherit the failure.
  • Deep Knowledge: You scan the AI output, instantly spot the logical flaw, edit it, and move forward.
  • AI only saves you time if you are skilled enough to verify its work instantly.

So don’t replace your brain with AI, use it as your assistant

  • Empty Offloading: Asking AI to write your essay, solve your math problem, or summarize a book you didn't read.
    • Result: Cognitive decline.
  • Strategic Offloading: Generating brainstorming ideas, finding counter-arguments to your thesis, or checking your completed code for bugs.
    • Result: Cognitive growth.

AI 悖论: AI 本该是替你干重活的初级助手; 但现实是, 你不懂这份工作就管不好助手, 没有自己的知识和经验, 就分不清精彩的洞见和自信的谎言 (幻觉). 核查陷阱: 一无所知的人只能盲目复制粘贴, AI 的错误就成了你的错误; 懂行的人扫一眼就发现逻辑漏洞, 改掉继续. 所以 AI 只有在你有能力马上核查它的产出时才真正省时间.

结论: 别用 AI 代替大脑, 把它当助手. 空洞的卸载 (让 AI 写文章、解数学题、总结你没读过的书) 导致认知衰退; 策略性的卸载 (头脑风暴、为你的论点找反驳、检查你已经写好的代码有没有 bug) 带来认知成长. 区别在于: 后者里思考的主体仍然是你自己.

Start with the core rule (PRINCIPLE)

  • AI can support your learning process, but it should not replace your own work.
  • Your final submission should reflect your understanding, analysis, evidence choices, and academic voice.
  • A helpful test: if you cannot explain a sentence, argument, citation, or decision, it should not be in your paper.
  • Always follow your university, course, and instructor policy on AI use.

Simple standard: Use AI to think better, not to hide the thinking. · Acceptable: Questions, Outlines, Revision ideas, Study support · Dangerous: Fake sources, Copied answers, Unverified claims, Hidden authorship

Helpful vs. risky AI use (BOUNDARIES)

Helpful usesRisky uses
Generating topic ideas to evaluate yourselfSubmitting generated writing as your own
Explaining difficult concepts in simpler languageInventing sources or using unverifiable references
Creating a draft outline you revise heavilyAsking AI to replace required research
Suggesting grammar and clarity improvementsIgnoring your course AI policy
Testing whether your argument is clearUsing AI to avoid learning the assignment

Never trust AI citations automatically (SOURCE VERIFICATION)

  • AI tools can produce references that look real but are incomplete, inaccurate, or entirely fabricated.
  • Do not cite a source unless you have personally located and checked it.
  • Verify the author, title, journal/book, date, DOI/URL, and the claim you are using.
  • If you cannot access or confirm a source, replace it with a real academic source.

Red flag: A perfect-looking reference that cannot be found in Google Scholar, library databases, or the publisher website. · Verification rule: Every reference should be traceable to a real document you have read or checked.

这几张来自网上一份「学术写作中负责任地使用 AI」的学生指南. 核心规则: AI 可以支持学习, 但不能代替你自己的工作; 交上去的东西要体现你自己的理解、分析、证据选择和学术语气. 一个好用的检验: 某个句子、论点、引用或决定你解释不了, 它就不该出现在论文里. 永远遵守学校、课程和老师关于 AI 的规定. 一句话标准: 用 AI 帮你想得更好, 而不是掩盖你没想. 可以的: 提问、列提纲、修改思路、学习辅助; 危险的: 假文献、抄来的答案、没核实的说法、隐瞒作者身份.

有益与有风险的用法逐条对照见上表. 绝不自动相信 AI 给的引用: AI 会编出看起来很真的文献, 其实不完整、不准确, 甚至完全是捏造的; 没有亲自找到并核对过的文献不能引用; 要核对作者、标题、期刊或书名、日期、DOI/URL, 以及你引用的那个说法原文里是否真的有; 找不到或确认不了的, 换成真实的学术文献. 危险信号: 一条格式完美的引用, 在 Google Scholar、图书馆数据库和出版社网站上都查不到.

Slide 11–15

AI-assisted paraphrasing still needs your judgment (PARAPHRASING)

Weak approach — Copy-change method: Pasting a paragraph and asking AI to “make it original” can lead to patchwriting and loss of meaning. · Responsible approach — Read-think-write method: Read the source, close it, explain the idea in your own words, then use AI only to improve clarity or grammar.

Keep your own academic voice visible (VOICE)

  • Use language you understand and can defend.
  • Keep your own thesis, reasoning, examples, and source choices.
  • Avoid generic AI phrases that make writing sound polished but empty.
  • After AI editing, reread the paper aloud to check whether it still sounds like you.
  • Add course-specific concepts, class readings, and your own analysis.

Academic voice means: Clear, precise, evidence-based writing that reflects your own understanding. · Warning sign: The writing sounds impressive, but you cannot explain what it means.

Fact-check every claim that matters (ACCURACY)

  • Definitions: Does the definition match a credible academic source?
  • Statistics: Is the number current, traceable, and from a reliable source?
  • Historical claims: Are dates, names, and events accurate?
  • Theories: Are theorists and concepts represented correctly?
  • Quotations: Does the exact wording appear in the original source?
  • Examples: Are case examples real and relevant?

Use AI as an editing assistant (REVISION)

  • Ask for clarity, concision, grammar, and transition suggestions.
  • Keep the original argument and evidence intact.
  • Review every change before accepting it.
  • Avoid edits that change meaning, remove citations, or make claims stronger than your evidence supports.
  • Use the final pass to ensure the writing still follows your assignment instructions.

Good prompt: Improve clarity and grammar without changing my argument, meaning, or citations. · Final check: Compare the edited version against your original outline and sources.

Final AI-use checklist before submission (CHECKLIST)

  • I followed my course or university AI policy.
  • I can explain every argument and paragraph.
  • All sources are real and personally verified.
  • Every cited claim is supported by a credible source.
  • The writing reflects my own analysis and learning.
  • Any AI assistance has been disclosed if required.
  • I proofread the final version after AI-supported edits.

改写 (paraphrasing): 差的做法是「复制再改」: 把一段原文贴给 AI, 让它「改得原创一点」, 结果是 patchwriting 拼凑式写作 (只换了词、结构还是原文的, 仍算抄袭) 还会丢失原意. 负责的做法是「读、想、写」: 读原文, 合上, 用自己的话讲出来, 之后只让 AI 改清楚程度和语法. 保持自己的学术语气: 只用自己懂、能为之辩护的语言; 保留自己的论点、推理、例子和文献选择; 避开听起来光鲜却空洞的 AI 套话; AI 改过后大声读一遍, 看还像不像你写的; 加入课程概念、课上读物和自己的分析. 警示信号: 文字听着很厉害, 你却解释不了它的意思.

核查每个重要说法: 定义是否和可靠的学术来源一致; 统计数字是否最新、可追溯、来源可靠; 历史上的日期人名事件是否准确; 理论和提出者是否表述正确; 引语原文里是否真有这几个字; 案例是否真实、相关. 把 AI 当编辑助手: 只请它改清晰、简洁、语法和过渡; 论点和证据保持不变; 每处修改都审过再接受; 不接受改变意思、删掉引用、或把说法改得比证据更强的修改. 提交前对照最后那张清单逐条打勾 (遵守规定、每段都讲得清、文献真实且亲自核过、每个引用的说法都有可靠来源、体现自己的分析、按要求声明 AI 的使用、AI 修改后再校对一遍).

IDEA

  • Define a research question(s)
    • Literature review helps you to gain a broader understanding of your topic. PS. You can start with the influential papers
  • Define keywords for your literature review
  • Also check recent papers
  • But do not forget the keystone papers of the domain
  • What has been done in the domain?

Research Questions

  • If more than one, clearly define the distinctions and their relevance!
  • Has it been addressed before?
  • If yes, how?
  • In the end, ensure that you have transparent and answerable questions given the evaluation metrics you have used!

Story

  • Find a good story based on your contribution.
  • Form the skeleton of your paper
  • Look at the bigger picture and bring the focus to your small domain
  • be aware of different aspects (local, global etc.)
  • Different methods, Different datasets
  • Already solved issues, remaining challenges

想法: 定研究问题. 文献综述帮你更全面地了解题目 (可以从有影响力的论文入手); 定好检索关键词; 看最新论文, 也别忘了这个领域的奠基性论文 (keystone papers); 问自己: 领域里已经做了什么? 什么还没探索、还没有定论? 问题太宽还是太窄? 然后修正研究问题. 研究问题: 有几个问题时要讲清它们的区别和各自的意义; 以前有人研究过吗? 怎么研究的? 最后要保证问题是清楚的、用你选的评估指标能回答的. 故事: 围绕你的贡献找一条好的叙事线, 搭出论文骨架; 先讲大背景, 再把焦点收到你的小领域上; 注意不同层面 (局部的、全局的)、不同方法、不同数据集、已解决的问题和剩下的挑战.

Slide 27–29

Literature Review / Related Work

  • A good related works section indicates that the author has a good knowledge of the research domain .
  • A valid recognition of previous RELATED studies (not just a list)
    • always bind the cited material to your research area/question/method, etc.
    • Point out the shortcomings of the previous works
    • with justification (why they are weak, missing, etc.).
    • Use this justification to explain why your study is needed!
    • Mention whether there is a similar study and how yours differs from it.
    • Replication studies are not pointless but deliver the point clearly!

Intro and Literature Review

  • Establishing why your topic is important
    • “Multimodal learning has many application areas in the field of “
  • Outlining the past-present history of the study
    • Recent findings regarding multimodal integration in hate speech detection have led to ….
  • If applicable, outlining the possible future
    • In the next five years…..
  • Indicate the possible limitations
    • Previous work has only focused on / been limited to / failed to address
  • State the aim of your paper and its contribution
    • This paper examines/seeks to address / focuses on / discusses /investigates how to solve
  • Explain the key terminology in your field
  • Give the structure of the paper - what is and is not included

Source: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-26094-5

文献综述 / 相关工作: 好的相关工作部分说明作者熟悉这个领域. 它要有效地评述相关的前人研究, 而不是罗列: 每篇引用的工作都要和你的领域、问题、方法挂上钩; 指出前人工作的不足, 并说明理由 (为什么弱、缺了什么); 用这些理由说明你的研究为什么有必要; 如果有类似的研究, 要说明你的有什么不同; 复现研究不是没意义, 但要把它的意义讲清楚.

引言和综述的套路 (幻灯片附了可以直接套用的英文句式): 说明题目为什么重要 (Multimodal learning has many application areas …); 勾勒研究的过去和现状 (Recent findings … have led to …); 如有必要展望未来 (In the next five years …); 指出前人的局限 (Previous work has only focused on / been limited to / failed to address …); 说明本文的目标和贡献 (This paper examines / seeks to address / investigates …); 解释领域里的关键术语; 交代全文结构, 包括哪些内容不讨论. 来源是 Springer 出的一本英语学术写作指南 (Wallwork, English for Writing Research Papers).

原文笔误: Slide 27「good knowledge of the research domain .」句号前多了一个空格; Slide 28 的例句「in the field of “」引号前多了空格, 句子也没写完 (应接一个领域名).

Slide 30–31

Avoid Plagiarism

  • After you have finished reading something, set it aside, write it in your own words, compare it with what you read earlier in the source document, and refine your writing. Make sure to cite the source document.
  • Don't misinterpret or manipulate what was intended in a source to suit your needs (for text, charts, graphs, images, music, and video).
  • Based on citations from secondary sources, make sure those citations actually exist and find out what was really conveyed in those cited sources so that you are not reproducing someone else's misinterpretation.
  • https://www.niu.edu/academic-integrity/students/plagiarism/tips-on-avoiding.shtml

Writing style

  • Check grammar
  • Check whether there are repeated words (verbs, connectors)
  • Whether the abbreviations are introduced
  • Use italics/bold when necessary (e.g. , italics for terms)
  • Pay attention to Figures and Tables
    • meaningful and standalone captions,
    • proper and consistent reference in the main text
    • Readable content

避免抄袭: 读完一段材料, 放到一边, 用自己的话写出来, 再和原文对照、修改, 并注明出处; 不要为了自己的需要曲解或篡改来源的原意 (文字、图表、图片、音乐、视频都一样); 引用二手来源里提到的文献时, 要确认那篇文献真的存在, 并亲自查明它到底说了什么, 免得照搬别人的误读.

写作风格: 检查语法; 检查有没有反复用同一个词 (动词、连接词); 缩写第一次出现时要给全称; 必要时用斜体或粗体 (例如术语用斜体); 注意图表: 图注要有意义、能脱离正文单独看懂, 正文里引用图表的方式要规范统一, 内容要清楚可读.

Experimental Setup

  • Describe the apparatus and materials used and their source
  • Give basic statistics about your participants, dataset, conditions, etc.
  • Describe the purpose of the methods used.
  • Report the details of the models, dataset used
  • Outline alternative approaches and limitations

Source: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-26094-5

NLP Oriented Methodology Section

  • SOTA results on the dataset/model/approach etc.
  • The details of the model (framework, parameters, hyperparameters)
    • E.g. If there is an imbalance in the class distributions, do you handle them?
    • Do not make it too crowded, you can use Appendices! Still be sure that the paper is not too dependent on the Appendices as well!

Evaluation/Metrics

  • Introduce your metrics
  • Explain why you use them and what they measure.
  • Then in the RESULT section, you will report the results one by one for each metric
  • Interpret them individually and in combination with other metrics (if relevant)

实验设置: 描述用到的设备、材料和它们的来源; 给出参与者、数据集、实验条件等的基本统计; 说明各方法的目的; 报告模型和数据集的细节; 概述其他可选做法和局限. NLP 论文的方法部分: 给出这个数据集 / 模型 / 方法上目前最好的结果 (SOTA) 作参照; 模型细节 (框架、参数、超参数), 比如类别分布不平衡时你有没有处理、怎么处理; 别写得太挤, 可以放进附录, 但正文离开附录也要能读懂.

评估指标: 先介绍你用的指标, 说明为什么用、各自衡量什么 (如 IE 04 · 常用评估指标 里的准确率、召回率、F1); 结果部分再按指标逐个报告; 既要单独解释每个指标, 必要时也要结合起来解释 (例如准确率高但召回率低说明什么).

Slide 35–36

Results and Discussion — Mind the difference!

  • Briefly re-explain how you got your results
  • Revisit your research questions/hypothesis
    • State what you found
    • State what you did not find
  • Highlight significant results and achievements
  • State that your results confirm or contradict previous evidence
  • Express caution regarding the interpretation of results / Admitting limitations
  • Mention Ethical Considerations

Source: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-26094-5

Conclusion

  • Restate the results
  • Highlight achievements
  • Outline possible applications and implications of your work
  • Mention the future work already underway or planned by the authors

结果与讨论 (注意两者的区别: 结果是客观报告数字, 讨论是解释它们的意义): 简要重述结果是怎样得到的; 回到研究问题或假设, 说明发现了什么、没有发现什么; 突出显著的结果和成就; 说明结果是证实还是反驳了前人的证据; 解释结果时要谨慎, 承认局限; 提到伦理方面的考虑. 结论: 重述结果; 突出成就; 概述工作可能的应用和意义; 提到作者正在进行或计划中的后续工作.

可复现的代码: 先建好文件夹结构 (数据、图、脚本、模型 …); 确保脚本能无错运行并产生输出; 代码里始终写注释. 开放科学: 公开数据和方法, 用 Git 加云存储的组合; 注意版权和敏感信息 (隐私); README 里写上必要的使用说明. 幻灯片推荐了乌得勒支大学的计算可复现性教程.

Slide 42–44

References

  • Please use a standard bibliographic format for your references, e.g. APA
  • This includes authors, date, title, and venue, like this:
    • Academic Journal
      Alexander Fraser, Helmut Schmid, Richard Farkas, Renjing Wang, Hinrich Schuetze (2013). Knowledge Sources for Constituent Parsing of German, a Morphologically Rich and Less Configurational Language. Computational Linguistics, 39(1), pages 57-85.
    • Academic Conference
      Alexander Fraser, Marion Weller, Aoife Cahill, Fabienne Cap (2012). Modeling Inflection and Word Formation in SMT. In Proceedings of the 13th Conference of the European Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics (EACL), pages 664-674, Avignon, France, April.

References (Cont.’)

In the Hausarbeit,

  • use *inline* citations (e.g. APA style):
    • "As shown by Fraser et al. (2012), the moon does not consist of cheese”
    • "We build upon previous work (Fraser and Marcu 2007; Fraser et al. 2012) by ..."
  • Sometimes it is also appropriate to include a page number (and you *must* include a page number for a quote or graphic)
  • DO NOT USE: footnotes containing the citations
  • If you use graphics (or quotes) from a research paper, MAKE SURE THESE ARE CITED ON THE *SAME SLIDE* IN YOUR PRESENTATION!
  • These should be cited in the Hausarbeit in the caption of the graphic.
  • Web pages should also use a standard bibliographic format, particularly including the date when they were downloaded
  • Wikipedia can be useful for background, but please don't cite Wikipedia pages!
  • You also cannot use student work (if not peer-reviewed) as a primary source

参考文献: 用标准格式 (如 APA): 作者、年份、标题、发表处. 幻灯片给了期刊文章和会议论文各一个例子. 课程论文 (Hausarbeit) 里用正文内引用 (inline citation): 「As shown by Fraser et al. (2012), …」或括号里列出「(Fraser and Marcu 2007; Fraser et al. 2012)」; 有时要加页码, 直接引语和引用的图必须加页码; 不要用脚注引用 (德国人文学科常用脚注, 计算语言学不用).

报告里用了论文里的图或引语, 必须在同一张幻灯片上注明出处; 课程论文里写在图注里. 网页也要用标准格式, 特别要写下载日期. 维基百科可以用来了解背景, 但不要引用. 没经过同行评审的学生作品不能当主要来源.

原文笔误: 例句「"As shown by Fraser et al. (2012), the moon does not consist of cheese”」开头是直引号、结尾是弯引号, 不配对; 标题「References (Cont.’)」里的撇号多余, 应为 (Cont.).
