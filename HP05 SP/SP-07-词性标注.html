Collocations and Bigrams (remaining from last week)

Slide 3–13

Collocations and Bigrams

  • Collocation: sequence of words that occur together unusually often
  • red wine: collocation ≠ the wine: no collocation.
  • Collocations: resistant to substitution with words that have similar senses; e.g., maroon wine sounds very odd

Collocation vs Concordance – What is the difference?

Reminder: Concordance: A list of all occurrences of a given word together with its context. Also known as keyword in context.

Collocations and Bigrams, Trigrams, N-grams: Bigrams: a list of consecutive word pairs extracted from a text · Trigrams: 3 consecutive words extracted from a text · 4-grams, 5-grams, etc. - generally called n-grams: list of n words · Collocations: frequent bigrams

Generating Random Text with Bigrams I–VI

>>> import nltk
>>> def generate_model(cfdist, word, num=15):
...     for i in range(num):
...         print(word, end=' ')
...         word = cfdist[word].max()
>>> text = nltk.corpus.genesis.words("english-kjv.txt")
>>> bigrams = nltk.bigrams(text)
>>> cfd = nltk.ConditionalFreqDist(bigrams)
>>> print(list(cfd["living"]))
['creature', 'thing', 'soul', '.', 'substance', ',']
>>> print(list(cfd["living"].values()))
[7, 4, 1, 1, 2, 1]
>>> generate_model(cfd, 'living')
living creature that he said , and the land of the land of the land

The generated text is “living creature that he said , and the land of the land of the land”. Is this satisfactory – why / why not?

词性标注基础
POS Tagging Basics

词性
Parts of speech (POS)

Slide 15
  • Parts of speech = morphosyntactic word classes
  • Words that belong to the same part of speech share some syntactic and morphological properties
  • Typical categories (as learned in school)
    • Noun: linguistics, apple
    • Verb: go, read
    • Adjective: blue, happy
    • Adverb: well, fast
    • Pronoun: I, yours
    • Determiner: a, the, some
    • Adposition: in, onto
    • Conjunction: and, or, because
    • Interjection: uh, hey

Adapted from Dr. Çağrı Çöltekin

Wortart 词性 (part of speech, 缩写 POS) 就是形态句法上的词类: 同一词性的词共享一些句法和形态特征, 比如名词能带冠词、有单复数. 学校里学的典型类别: 名词、动词、形容词、副词、代词、限定词、adposition 附置词 (介词和后置词的统称)、连词、叹词.

为什么叫 adposition 而不是 preposition: 有的语言把它放在名词后面 (德语 meiner Meinung nach, 日语、土耳其语几乎都是后置), 所以多语言的标签集用这个上位词.

定义
POS Tagging Definitions

Slide 16
  • POS tagset: the set of POS tags actually used for a corpus. Can depend on...
    • the linguistic theory behind the categorization
    • the language (some tagsets aim to be applicable to all languages, others are language-specific)
    • the downstream purpose the POS tags are used for
  • Part-of-speech (POS) tagging = the task of labeling each word with its corresponding POS.
  • Example: 
    [('to', 'TO'),
 ('permit', 'VB'),
 ('us', 'PRP'),
 ('to', 'TO'),
 ('obtain', 'VB'),
 ('the', 'DT'),
 ('permit', 'NN')]
    • Here with the Penn Treebank POS tags/UPenn tagset, which defines what POS tags like ‘VB’ or ‘NN’ mean

Tagset 标签集是一个语料实际使用的词性标签的集合. 它取决于: 分类背后的语言学理论; 语言 (有的标签集想适用于所有语言, 有的只针对一种); 标签之后要拿来做什么. POS-Tagging 词性标注就是给每个词标上它的词性. 例子里同一个 permit 第一次是动词原形 VB, 第二次是名词 NN: 标注看的是这个词在这个位置上的用法. 输出格式是 NLTK 的 (词, 标签) 元组列表, 标签用 Penn Treebank 标签集.

为什么要词性
POS Tagging – Motivation

Slide 18
  • Why POS? Basis (or replacement) for analyzing texts at a syntactic or shallow semantic level
  • Automatically obtained POS tags are often more robust than deeper automatic linguistic analysis
  • Name an application for which knowing the POS tag would be useful

为什么要词性? 它是在句法层或浅层语义层分析文本的基础, 有时也可以代替更深的分析. 自动得到的词性标签往往比更深的自动分析 (完整句法树) 更可靠: 标注只需要局部上下文, 错一个词不会连带整句. 课堂问题「举一个用得上词性的应用」的答案就是下面四页.

Slide 19

Motivation – Examples · Information extraction

  • “The NB-kappa-B activation is triggered by ..”
  • Nouns typically refer to entities, verbs indicating relations between entities
  • If wug is a noun, also search for wugs

信息抽取: 名词通常指实体 (NB-kappa-B、activation), 动词表示实体之间的关系 (is triggered by). 知道 wug 是名词, 就知道还要搜它的复数 wugs; 如果是动词, 要搜的是 wugged、wugging.

Slide 20

Motivation – Examples · Machine translation

  • “I like..”
    • “Ich mag..” (verb)
    • “Ich wie..” (preposition)

Adapted from Dr. Mariana Neves

机器翻译: like 是动词时译成 mag (喜欢), 是介词时译成 wie (像). 「I like ..」里它显然是动词, 「Ich wie ..」是错译. 先标对词性, 就能选对译词.

开放类与封闭类
Open vs. Closed POS tag classes

Slide 23
  • Closed
    • Limited number of words in each category, do not grow usually
    • e.g., Auxiliary, Determiner, Conjunction, Pronoun, Preposition, Particle, Interjection
  • Open
    • Unlimited number of words
    • e.g., Noun, Verb, Adverb, Adjective

geschlossene Wortklasse 封闭类: 成员有限, 一般不增加, 如助动词、限定词、连词、代词、介词、小品词、叹词. offene Wortklasse 开放类: 成员无限, 新词不断加入, 如名词、动词、副词、形容词 (to google、selfie 都是新加入的).

对标注器的意义: 封闭类的词可以列表穷举, 而且都很高频; 没见过的新词几乎一定属于开放类. 后面的查表标注器正是利用了这一点.

粒度与形态特征
Granularity and morphological features

Slide 24

Depending on the tagset, classes can be subdivided into more fine-grained classes

  • E.g., noun:
    • Proper noun (Mary)
    • Common noun: count (chair) vs. mass (happiness)
  • E.g., pronoun
    • Personal pronoun (I, me)
    • Possessive pronoun (my, mine)
    • Relative pronoun (who, whose, which)
    • Interrogative pronoun (who, whose, which)

Often, words are additionally annotated with morphological features that depend on the POS (and the language!), e.g.

  • Noun: number, case, gender
  • Verb: tense, aspect, mood, evidentiality, voice
  • Adjective: degree, agreement with noun features

Adapted from Dr. Çağrı Çöltekin

按标签集不同, 大类还能细分. 名词分专有名词 (Mary) 和普通名词, 普通名词再分可数 (chair) 和不可数 (happiness). 代词分人称、物主、关系、疑问代词; 注意关系代词和疑问代词的词形完全一样 (who、whose、which), 只能靠上下文区分. 词还常常另外标上依赖词性和语言的形态特征: 名词的数、格、性; 动词的时、体、式、evidentiality 示证 (说话人怎样得知这件事: 亲见还是听说, 土耳其语、藏语有专门的形式)、态; 形容词的比较级和与名词的一致.

标签集有很多种, 它规定标签的数量、粒度和每个标签的定义. 常用的: 英语 Penn Treebank 核心标签 36 个 (Marcus 等 1993, 也叫 UPenn 或 WSJ 标签集, 因为语料是《华尔街日报》); 德语 STTS (斯图加特-图宾根标签集); 多语言的 Universal Dependencies 标签集, 早期版本 (Google Universal tagset) 12 个, NLTK 用的就是它, 当前第 2 版 17 个.

Penn 的 36 个不含标点; 加上标点和符号类标签共 45 个 (后面 Slide 51 说的「45-tag tagset」就是这个).

幻灯片小错: STTS 的官方指南 (Schiller 等 1999) 列出的是 54 个标签 (含 3 个标点标签 $, $. $(), 不是 52 个. 不同资料对是否计入个别标签说法略有出入, 但 52 这个数不对应任何一种常见计法.

Note! Some POS tags denote variation of the same word type, e.g. NN, NNS, NNP, NNPS, such can be looked up via regular expressions.

>>> nltk.help.upenn_tagset('NN*')

    common-carrier cabbage knuckle-duster Casino ...
NNP: noun, proper, singular
    Motown Venneboerger Czestochwa Ranzer Conchita ...

    undergraduates scotches bric-a-brac ...

nltk.help.upenn_tagset(标签) 打印标签的说明和例词 (要先 nltk.download('tagsets_json')). 参数是正则表达式, 所以 'NN*' 一次列出 NN、NNP、NNPS、NNS 四个名词标签 (按字母顺序). 实测 (NLTK 3.10) 输出与幻灯片一致, 只是 CC 的例词在 & 后面多一个 'n (rock 'n roll 的 'n); 幻灯片里的「...」是省略, 实际会把例词全部列出.

易错: 'NN*' 是正则, 不是通配符. 正则里 * 表示前一个字符重复零次或多次, NN* 本意是 N 后面跟零个或多个 N; 它能匹配 NNP 是因为 NLTK 用 re.match 只检查开头.

Slide 32

Disambiguation

POS tagging does not always provide the same label for a given word, but decides on the correct label for the specific context – disambiguates across the word classes.

>>> import nltk
>>> text = nltk.word_tokenize("They refuse to permit us to obtain the refuse permit")
>>> print(nltk.pos_tag(text))
[('They', 'PRP'), ('refuse', 'VBP'), ('to', 'TO'),
('permit', 'VB'), ('us', 'PRP'), ('to', 'TO'),
('obtain', 'VB'), ('the', 'DT'), ('refuse', 'NN'),
('permit', 'NN')]

词性标注不是给每个词一个固定标签, 而是根据具体上下文选对的那个: 在词类之间做Disambiguierung 消歧. 句中 refuse 和 permit 各出现两次: 第一次 refuse 是动词 (VBP, 拒绝), 第二次是名词 (NN, 垃圾, 读音也不同); permit 先是动词 (VB, 允许), 后是名词 (NN, 许可证). 实测 (NLTK 3.10 的 averaged perceptron 标注器, 需要 nltk.download('averaged_perceptron_tagger_eng')) 与幻灯片完全一致.

Slide 33

Example from Brown

Whenever a corpus contains tagged text, the NLTK corpus interface will have a tagged_words() method.

>>> nltk.corpus.brown.words()
['The', 'Fulton', 'County', 'Grand', 'Jury', 'said', ...]
>>> nltk.corpus.brown.tagged_words()
[('The', 'AT'), ('Fulton', 'NP-TL'), ...]

只要语料带标注, NLTK 的语料接口就有 tagged_words() 方法, 返回 (词, 标签) 元组. Brown 语料用的是它自己的 Brown 标签集: AT 是冠词, NP 是专有名词, 后缀 -TL 表示这个词出现在标题里 (title). 实测输出相同.

Mapping between tagsets isn’t always trivial

  • Going from fine-grained to coarse-grained/simplified can be easy, but not vice versa
  • Decisions about how to deal with edge cases or specific word (sub)classes can be very different, e.g.,
    • Penn: something = NN (noun)
    • Universal POS: something = PRON (pronoun)

标签集之间的映射并不总是简单的: 细 → 粗容易 (把 NN、NNS、NNP 都并成 NOUN), 粗 → 细不行 (只知道 NOUN, 推不出是单数还是复数、专有还是普通). 边界情况的处理也可能完全不同: something 在 Penn 里是名词 NN, 在 Universal 里是代词 PRON. 所以不同标签集标出来的语料, 不能简单地互相比较准确率.

The regular expression tagger assigns tags to tokens on the basis of matching patterns:

正则标注器按词形猜标签: -ing 结尾标动名词 VBG, -ed 过去式 VBD, -es 第三人称单数 VBZ, -ould 情态动词 MD, -'s 名词属格, -s 复数名词 NNS, 数字 CD, 其余全部 NN (最后的 .* 匹配任何词, 相当于默认标注器). 规则按顺序检查, 用第一条匹配的.

为什么顺序重要: -es 必须排在 -s 前面, 否则 goes 先被 .*s$ 匹配成 NNS; .* 必须最后, 否则后面的规则永远轮不到. 易错: 数字规则里的 . 没有转义, 匹配任意字符, 本意是小数点, 应写 \.; 所以 1x5 也会被标成 CD. NLTK 书原样如此.

查表标注器
Lookup Tagger

Slide 65

A lot of high-frequency words do not have the NN tag. Let’s find the hundred most frequent words and store their most likely tag. We can then use this information as the model for a “lookup tagger” (an NLTK UnigramTagger):

import nltk
from nltk.corpus import brown

fd = nltk.FreqDist(brown.words(categories='news'))
cfd = nltk.ConditionalFreqDist(brown.tagged_words(categories='news'))
most_freq_words = fd.most_common(100)
likely_tags = dict((word, cfd[word].max())
                   for (word, _) in most_freq_words)
baseline_tagger = nltk.UnigramTagger(model=likely_tags)
sent = brown.sents(categories='news')[3]
print(baseline_tagger.tag(sent))

很多高频词不是 NN. 思路: 找出新闻部分最常见的 100 个词, 记下每个词最可能的标签, 拿这张表当模型. 代码逐行: fd 数词频; cfd 以词为条件统计标签; most_common(100) 取前 100 个 (词, 次数); 字典 likely_tags 把每个词映射到它最常见的标签 (_ 是不用的次数); UnigramTagger(model=…) 直接用这张字典做标注器.

General N-Gram Tagging

Problem of unigram tagging → assigns one tag irrespective of context, e.g.,

  • the wind
  • to wind

hoping for the wind to stop blowing

  • unigram tagging – one item of context: wind
  • bigram tagging – two items of context: the wind
  • trigram tagging – three items of context: for the wind
  • n-gram tagging – n items of context

一元标注的问题: 不管上下文, 一个词永远一个标签. the wind 里 wind 是名词, to wind 里是动词, 一元标注器只能选一个. 解决办法是看更多上下文: 二元标注看当前词和前一个 (the wind), 三元看前两个 (for the wind), n 元看 n 项.

Data Sets

Note! Not only do we need to separate training and test set from each other, but there are a number of other issues that we need to keep in mind:

  • Usually, the larger the training data is, the better the system is trained – more data beats a cleverer algorithm
  • If the test set is too small, it will not provide an objective evaluation
  • Select training data that is representative of the problem – if you test on the news category, train on this type of data as well.

除了把训练集和测试集分开, 还要注意: 训练数据通常越多越好, 「更多的数据胜过更聪明的算法」; 测试集太小, 评估就不客观 (几十个词元, 错一个就差好几个百分点); 训练数据要有代表性: 要在新闻上测, 就也用新闻训练.

Slide 78

So, we have a number of different data partitions that are used in Machine Learning:

  • Training data – numerous examples for which the correct answers are already provided and which can be used to train a predictive model. In this case, the training process involves inspecting the tag of each word and storing the most likely tag for the 100 most often seen words in it.
  • Test data – a set of data that the system needs to label, which is used to evaluate its performance.
  • Development data – a set of data used as “test set” during system development (validation set).

机器学习常用三种数据划分: Trainingsdaten 训练数据, 带正确答案、用来训练模型 (查表标注器的训练就是数出 100 个高频词最可能的标签); Testdaten 测试数据, 让系统去标、用来评估性能; Entwicklungsdaten 开发数据, 开发过程中当「测试集」用, 也叫validation set 验证集.

为什么要三份: 开发时会根据开发集上的分数反复调整系统, 调到最后系统已经「适应」了开发集, 它上面的分数也偏高. 测试集只在最后用一次, 才能给出公正的估计.

Developing a Tagger

Developing a tagger (similar to developing most other NLP tools) is an iterative process:

  1. Implement a base version
  2. Train
  3. Test (use development data)
  4. Analyze errors
  5. Implement improvements – optimize
  6. Go back to step 2
  7. ...
  8. Test optimized version (use test data)

开发标注器 (和开发大多数 NLP 工具一样) 是一个迭代过程: 先实现一个基础版本, 训练, 在开发数据上测, 分析错误, 改进, 回到训练, 如此反复; 最后才在测试数据上测一次优化好的版本.

Optimization

Optimizing a tagger would mean that a better solution needs to be provided for ambiguous cases. This could be achieved by:

  • more training data
  • looking at a wider context - increasing n
  • based on the error analysis, e.g., confused labels

优化标注器就是给歧义情况更好的解决办法: 更多训练数据; 看更宽的上下文, 即增大 n (加一个 TrigramTagger(train, backoff=t2)); 根据错误分析针对性地改, 比如混淆矩阵显示新词大量错成 NOUN, 就把最后一级的默认标注器换成看词尾的正则标注器.

More on Evaluating a POS Tagger

On dev set / held-out data we can quantify:

  • how the tagger is doing overall:
    • accuracy: the % of tokens for which the predicted label matched the gold label, i.e. #(pred=gold) / #(tokens)
  • how the tagger is doing for a particular tag (say, VERB) different error types:
    • false positives: VERB predicted, should have been something else, i.e. #(pred=V, gold!=V)
    • false negatives: should have predicted VERB, actually predicted something else, i.e. #(pred!=V, gold=V)
    • true positives: correctly predicted VERB, i.e. #(pred=gold=V)
    • true negatives: tokens that don’t involve VERB at all – not relevant to the question

在开发集或held-out data 留出数据上可以量化两件事. 整体表现: 准确率. 某一个标签 (比如 VERB) 的各种错误: false positive 假正例, 预测成 VERB 但其实不是; false negative 假负例, 应该是 VERB 却预测成别的; true positive 真正例, 正确地预测为 VERB; true negative 真负例, 既不是 VERB 也没预测成 VERB 的词元, 和这个问题无关.

对应到 Slide 86 的矩阵: VERB 的 TP 是对角线 8885, FN 是 VERB 行其余各格 (956 + 30 + 18 + 7 = 1011), FP 是 VERB 列其余各格 (26 + 234 + 3 = 263).

Slide 89

To measure relative performance with respect to a particular tag (say, VERB):

  • precision = % of VERB predictions that were correct
    • TP / (TP+FP)
  • recall = % of actual VERB tokens that were correctly tagged
    • TP / (TP+FN)

Notes! Same numerator, different denominators!

To combine these into one score we typically use F1 score = harmonic mean, computed as:

  • F1 = 2*Precision*Recall / (Precision + Recall) = 2*TP / (2*TP + FP + FN)

Will revisit these metrics in detail in Lecture 9

Precision 精确率: 预测为 VERB 的里面有多少真是 VERB; Recall 召回率: 真正的 VERB 里有多少被标出来了. 分子相同, 分母不同. 两者用F1 (调和平均) 合成一个数, 化简后等于 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN): 把 P=TP/(TP+FP)、R=TP/(TP+FN) 代入 2PR/(P+R), 分子分母同乘 (TP+FP)(TP+FN)/TP 即得. 第 9 讲还会详细讲.

用 Slide 86 的数算 VERB: P=8885/9148≈0.971, R=8885/9896≈0.898, F1​=17770/19044≈0.933. 召回明显低于精确率: 很多动词被错标成了名词. 同样的定义在 EICL 08 里用于文本分类.
