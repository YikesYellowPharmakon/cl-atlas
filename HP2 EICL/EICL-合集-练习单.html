Language is a system that consists of the development, acquisition, maintenance and use of complex systems of communication, particularly the human ability to do so; and a language is any specific example of such a system.

The scientific study of language is called linguistics. Questions concerning the philosophy of language, such as whether words can represent experience, have been debated at least since Gorgias and Plato in ancient Greece. Thinkers such as Rousseau have argued that language originated from emotions while others like Kant have held that it originated from rational and logical thought. 20th-century philosophers such as Wittgenstein argued that philosophy is really the study of language. Major figures in linguistics include Ferdinand de Saussure and Noam Chomsky.

Estimates of the number of human languages in the world vary between 5,000 and 7,000. However, any precise estimate depends on a partly arbitrary distinction between languages and dialects. Natural languages are spoken or signed, but any language can be encoded into secondary media using auditory, visual, or tactile stimuli – for example, in whistling, signed, or braille. This is because human language is modality-independent. Depending on philosophical perspectives regarding the definition of language and meaning, when used as a general concept, "language" may refer to the cognitive ability to learn and use systems of complex communication, or to describe the set of rules that makes up these systems, or the set of utterances that can be produced from those rules. All languages rely on the process of semiosis to relate signs to particular meanings. Oral, manual and tactile languages contain a phonological system that governs how symbols are used to form sequences known as words or morphemes, and a syntactic system that governs how words and morphemes are combined to form phrases and utterances.

Human language has the properties of productivity and displacement, and relies entirely on social convention and learning. Its complex structure affords a much wider range of expressions than any known system of animal communication. Language is thought to have originated when early hominins started gradually changing their primate communication systems, acquiring the ability to form a theory of other minds and a shared intentionality. This development is sometimes thought to have coincided with an increase in brain volume, and many linguists see the structures of language as having evolved to serve specific communicative and social functions. Language is processed in many different locations in the human brain, but especially in Broca's and Wernicke's areas. Humans acquire language through social interaction in early childhood, and children generally speak fluently by approximately three years old. The use of language is deeply entrenched in human culture. Therefore, in addition to its strictly communicative uses, language also has many social and cultural uses, such as signifying group identity, social stratification, as well as social grooming and entertainment.

Languages evolve and diversify over time, and the history of their evolution can be reconstructed by comparing modern languages to determine which traits their ancestral languages must have had in order for the later developmental stages to occur. A group of languages that descend from a common ancestor is known as a language family. The Indo-European family is the most widely spoken and includes languages as diverse as English, Russian and Hindi; the Sino-Tibetan family includes Mandarin, Bodo and the other Chinese languages, and Tibetan; the Afro-Asiatic family includes Arabic, Somali, and Hebrew; the Bantu languages include Swahili, and Zulu, and hundreds of other languages spoken throughout Africa; and the Malayo-Polynesian languages include Indonesian, Malay, Tagalog, and hundreds of other languages spoken throughout the Pacific. The languages of the Dravidian family, spoken mostly in Southern India, include Tamil, Telugu and Kannada. Academic consensus holds that between 50% and 90% of languages spoken at the beginning of the 21st century will probably have become extinct by the year 2100.

Animal languages are forms of non-human animal communication that show similarities to human language. Animals communicate by using a variety of signs such as sounds or movements. Such signing may be considered complex enough to be called a form of language if the inventory of signs is large, the signs are relatively arbitrary, and the animals seem to produce them with a degree of volition (as opposed to relatively automatic conditioned behaviors or unconditioned instincts, usually including facial expressions). In experimental tests, animal communication may also be evidenced through the use of lexigrams (as used by chimpanzees and bonobos). While the term "animal language" is widely used, researchers agree that animal languages are not as complex or expressive as human language.

Many researchers argue that animal communication lacks a key aspect of human language, that is, the creation of new patterns of signs under varied circumstances. (In contrast, for example, humans routinely produce entirely new combinations of words.) Some researchers, including the linguist Charles Hockett, argue that human language and animal communication differ so much that the underlying principles are unrelated. Accordingly, linguist Thomas A. Sebeok has proposed to not use the term "language" for animal sign systems. Marc Hauser, Noam Chomsky, and W. Tecumseh Fitch assert an evolutionary continuum exists between the communication methods of animal and human language.

Frage 3 · 用 WordNet 给词选义项
Wörterbucheinträge aus WordNet

Aufgabe 01 · Frage 3 · 3 Punkte

Benutzen Sie die Wörterbucheinträge (s.u. in diesem Übungsabschnitt) aus der Datenbank von WordNet (https://wordnet.princeton.edu/), um den gekennzeichneten Wörtern in den drei untenstehenden Sätzen die richtige Bedeutung zuzuweisen.

Betrachten Sie zunächst aber den Eintrag zu dem Beispielwort "test" in WordNet. Sie finden dort sechs verschiedene Bedeutungen zu dem Nomen "test" und sieben verschiedene Bedeutungen zu dem Verb "test", die jeweils von 1 bis 6 bzw. bis 7 nummeriert sind. Zu allen Bedeutungen von "test" sind dort zunächst jeweils Synonyme angegeben (sofern vorhanden), dann eine kurze Bedeutungserklärung, und bei den meisten Bedeutungen auch noch Beispielsätze (in Anführungsstrichen).

Benutzen Sie in Ihrer Antwort V für Verb und N für Nomen und geben Sie dann an, an wievielter Stelle der jeweiligen Kategorie die passende Definition steht. Wenn es zwei oder mehr richtige Lösungen gibt, tragen Sie bitte nur zwei ein. Falls es nur eine richtige Lösung gibt, geben Sie beim zweiten Platzhalter bitte Null ein.

Beispiel:
"Yesterday the professor tested us on grammar."
Da "test" hier als Verb in der gleichen Bedeutung wie 'quiz' verwendet wird, ist die einzige richtige Bedeutung diejenige von test3 bei WordNet. Deshalb müssten Sie bei diesem Beispiel als Antwort eingeben:
Vtest3 Null

Nun zur konkreten Aufgabe mit folgenden Beispielsätzen:

  • We were sitting at the peak of Mount K2. ____ ____
  • I saw the bat flying through the air. ____ ____
  • The bulldozer is about to level the building. ____ ____

翻译 (题目): 用本节最后的 WordNet 词条, 给下面三个句子里标出的词选出正确的义项. 先看示例词 test: 名词有 6 个义项, 动词有 7 个, 各自编号; 每个义项先列同义词, 再给简短释义, 大多还有引号里的例句. 答案格式: V 表示动词、N 表示名词, 加上词和这个词性下的义项编号. 有两个或更多正确义项时只填两个; 只有一个正确义项时, 第二个空填 Null. 例: 「Yesterday the professor tested us on grammar.」里 test 是动词、意思同 quiz, 唯一正确的是 Vtest3, 所以填「Vtest3　Null」.

方法 (三步):

  1. 定词性: 看它在句子里是名词还是动词. the peak / the bat 前面有冠词, 是名词; to level 前面有 to、后面跟宾语 the building, 是动词.
  2. 逐条读释义: 在这个词性的义项里, 把每条释义放回句子里试, 看意思通不通.
  3. 数正确义项: WordNet 的义项划分很细, 常常有两个义项都说得通. 题目规定此时填两个, 只有确实只剩一个时才填 Null.
答案 (先自己做再看)
句子正确义项为什么
We were sitting at the peak of Mount K2.Npeak4、Npeak6peak4「某物 (通常是山) 的顶端」, 例句就是山顶; peak6「(某物的) 最高点」, 例句 at the peak of the pyramid 和 at the peak of Mount K2 结构完全一样. 两个都对. peak3 是抽象的「顶峰、极致」 (the peak of perfection), peak1 是数值的极值, 都不对.
I saw the bat flying through the air.Nbat1, 以及 Nbat3/4/5 中任一个句子本身有歧义: 飞过空中的可以是一只蝙蝠 (bat1), 也可以是一支被扔出去的球拍/球棒 (bat3 壁球拍、bat4 板球棒、bat5 各种球类的球棒). 语境里没有排除哪一种, 所以四个都算对, 填其中两个即可. 最好的填法是 Nbat1 + 一个球棒义项, 体现出这正是一个词汇歧义的例子. bat2「击球轮次」是一个动作机会, 不会飞.
The bulldozer is about to level the building.Vlevel2、Nulllevel2「推倒、使与地面齐平」, 同义词 raze、tear down, 例句 The building was levelled. level3「使平整」 (level the ground) 的宾语是地面, 不是建筑物, 所以不选. 只有一个正确义项, 第二空填 Null.

易错: ① 看到第一个对得上的义项就停. 做这类题要把这个词性下的义项全部读完再决定; ② 格式必须是「词性 + 词 + 编号」, 如 Npeak4, 不能写成 peak4 或 N4; ③ Null 只在确实只有一个正确义项时用.

这道题就是计算语言学里的 Word Sense Disambiguation (WSD) 词义消歧: 给定一个多义词在句子里的一次出现, 从词典的义项列表中选出它在这里的意思. Jurafsky & Martin 第 1 章把它列为语言理解系统的组成部分 (文献合集 1.2); WordNet 正是 WSD 研究里最常用的义项清单. 题目的难点也是 WSD 的难点: WordNet 义项分得很细 (peak4 和 peak6 几乎是同一个意思), 连人都很难只选一个. 所以 WSD 评测里常常把细粒度义项合并成粗粒度再打分.

Frage 4 · 判断歧义的类型
Arten von Ambiguität

Aufgabe 01 · Frage 4 · 5 Punkte

Ordnen Sie den Beispielsätzen die richtigen Arten von Ambiguität zu.

Auswahl: Pragmatische Ambiguität · Syntaktische Ambiguität · Morphologische Ambiguität · Lexikalische Ambiguität · Phonetische Ambiguität · Skopusambiguität

  1. One morning I shot an elephant in my pyjamas. How he got into my pyjamas I’ll never know.
  2. Wissen Sie, wo die Goethestaße ist?
  3. Pete has a hairy brown mole.
  4. "How is bread made?" […] "You take some flour -" “Where do you pick the flower?” the White Queen asked. - Lewis Carroll, Alice in Wonderland
  5. "Wo ist bitte die Wachstube?" "Gibt es Wachs in der Tube überhaupt?"
  6. Meine beiden Freundinnen haben vier Kinder.

翻译 (题目): 给例句配上正确的歧义类型. 六种类型都来自第 1 讲 (七类歧义总表).

思路: 先问歧义出在哪一层: 声音、词内部的切分、一个词的多个意义、短语结构、量词或否定的作用范围、还是说话人的意图.

答案 (先自己做再看)
例句类型两种读法
I shot an elephant in my pyjamas.syntaktisch 句法歧义介词短语 in my pyjamas 挂在哪里: 修饰「我」 (我穿着睡衣射了大象) 还是修饰「大象」 (大象穿着我的睡衣). 第二句的笑点就是故意选了荒唐的那种结构. 词都一样, 只有结构不同. 这是格劳乔·马克思 (Groucho Marx) 的名句, Jurafsky & Martin 也用它讲句法歧义.
Wissen Sie, wo die Goethestraße ist?pragmatisch 语用歧义字面上是是非问句 (「您知道吗?」答「知道」就够了), 实际用意是请求 (「请告诉我在哪」). 句子意思清楚, 歧义在说话人的意图上, 对应 J&M 里 HAL 要分辨「请求、陈述、提问」的例子.
Pete has a hairy brown mole.lexikalisch 词汇歧义mole 有两个不相关的意思: 鼹鼠 (Maulwurf) 或 (长毛的) 痣 (Muttermal). 一个词、几个意义, 就是同形同音异义 (Homonymie).
flour / flowerphonetisch 语音歧义flour (面粉) 和 flower (花) 读音相同、拼写不同 (同音异形词, Homophone). 白皇后只听到声音, 就把「面粉」理解成了「花」, 所以问「去哪儿摘」.
Wachstubemorphologisch 形态歧义字母相同、语素切分不同: Wach-stube (警卫室) 或 Wachs-tube (一管蜡). 第 1 讲和 Morphologie 讲 (Folie 39) 都用了这个例子.
Meine beiden Freundinnen haben vier Kinder.Skopus 辖域歧义两人一共四个孩子, 还是每人各四个? 取决于「vier」在「beide」的辖域里面还是外面 (分配读法 vs. 集合读法). 句法结构相同, 量词的作用范围不同.

区分要点: 先问「歧义出在哪一层」: 声音 (语音) → 词内部的切分 (形态) → 一个词的多个意义 (词汇) → 短语结构 (句法) → 量词、否定的作用范围 (辖域) → 说话人的意图 (语用).

易错: flour/flower 是语音歧义而不是词汇歧义: 两个词拼写不同, 只是读音一样; mole 是同一个形式有两个意思, 才是词汇歧义.

题目原文笔误: 「Goethestaße」应为「Goethestraße」 (缺 r).

Frage 5 · 双重结构: 找出最小的有意义单位
Duality of patterning

Aufgabe 01 · Frage 5 · 1 Punkt

Duality of patterning (DoP) was proposed by Charles F. Hockett as one of the design features of language (Hockett 1958, 1960) that distinguish it from other biological communication systems.

Definition of DoP: The meaningful units of language (words, morphemes, ...) are made up of meaningless units (phonemes, letters, ...). The only function of meaningless units is to distinguish the meaningful units from one another.

[This idea seems straightforward, but many complications arise when we want to make DoP precise. You may want to think about this.]

An example: If you were to identify "arie" und "oft" as the only meaningful units of the sentence Marie lacht oft, then your solution would be (written down as a comma separated list without blank, in order of appearance):
arie,oft

Now to your assignment:
For the following sentence:
I see you jump

思路: 把句子切开, 看每一段单独拿出来有没有意义; 能不能再切出更小的、仍有意义的部分 (比如屈折词尾).

答案: I,see,you,jump. 四个词各自都是一个语素, 不能再切出有意义的部分: s-e-e、j-u-m-p 里的字母或音位单独都没有意义 (这就是「无意义单位」那一层); 这个句子里也没有词尾 (不是 sees、jumped), 所以每个词恰好是一个最小意义单位.

两层结构:

层次单位本句里
第一层: 有意义语素 / 词I · see · you · jump
第二层: 无意义, 只起区分作用音位 / 字母/s/ /iː/ · /dʒ/ /ʌ/ /m/ /p/ … (把 jump 的 /dʒ/ 换成 /b/ 就成了 bump)

这正是第 2 讲和 Flohr & Pfingsten 2.1 节讲的音位 vs. 语素: 音位区别意义, 语素承载意义. 少量无意义的音位可以组合出成千上万个有意义的语素, 这也是 Morphologie 讲 Folie 12 洪堡「有限手段的无限运用」的一个体现.

易错: 句子里有屈折词尾时要拆开. 例如 She sees you jumping 的最小意义单位是 She,see,s,you,jump,ing: -s (第三人称单数) 和 -ing 都是有意义的语素.

括号里说的「真要说精确会遇到麻烦」, 举两个例子: ① you 有意义, 但 y-ou 切开后, ou 在 out、soup 里读音完全不同, 「字母」和「音位」并不一一对应, 所以「无意义单位」到底是字母还是音位要先定好; ② unikale Morpheme 唯一语素 (Morphologie 讲 Folie 19 的 kunter、Him-beere 的 Him-) 没有可说出的意义, 但又是能切出来的单位, 处在两层之间. 示例「Marie lacht oft → arie,oft」故意给了一个错误的切分, 只是演示格式: Marie 是专有名词, arie 不是有意义的单位; lacht 至少能切成 lach + t.

Begriffe: Derivations-Affix · Fugenelement · Wurzel · Basis · Flexions-Affix

答案 (先自己做再看)

bemuttern = [[be + mutter] + n]. 词根是名词 Mutter (母亲). 前缀 be- 把名词变成动词「像母亲一样照顾 (某人)」, 改变了意义和词类, 是 Derivations-Affix. be + mutter 合起来的 bemutter- 是动词的基础, 但它含有词缀, 所以只能是 Basis, 不是词根. 最后的 -n 是不定式词尾 (Mutter 以 -er 结尾, 不定式词尾 -en 在 -er 后缩成 -n, 如 wandern、ändern), 是 Flexions-Affix.

答案 (先自己做再看)

ungebräuchlichere = [[[un + [[ge + bräuch] + lich]] + er] + e]. 从里往外:

  • bräuch 是动词 brauchen 的词根 (带变音的语子, 同 Häus) → Wurzel; ge- 是前缀, 构成 gebrauchen (使用) / Gebrauch (用法), 改变了 brauchen 的意义 → D.-Aff.; gebräuch- → Basis.
